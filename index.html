Why a new Resources section?

The current Resources tab lists only a few items, and some of its links no longer work. This page shows how the tab could become a dependable study hub for UUI students: every item opens directly in the browser, works on mobile, and is free to use.
The text of The Urantia Book has been in the public domain since 2001.
