Introduction — Angels Who Long to Start at the Bottom

Paper 38 surveyed the origin and nature of the seraphim; Paper 39 lays out, order by order, the work they actually do. The Mother Spirit intends to produce uniformly perfect seraphim, but for some unknown reason her offspring are very diverse. The author cautiously suggests this may be due to the interposition of experiential Deity, and he notes that once trained, seraphim fall distinctly into seven groups. (39:0.1)

It would hardly be true to call any order inferior to another. Each angel is at first limited to its original group, yet Manotia, the author’s seraphic associate, began as a supreme seraphim and by devoted service has achieved all seven services. Seraphim have no childhood, but they are experiential creatures who must gain knowledge and experience much as we do. So the more highly endowed an angel is, the more persistently it seeks assignment to the lower services. (39:0.9, 39:0.10, 39:0.11)

And they all crave to start at the bottom, on the lowest possible level of ministry (39:0.11)

I. Supreme Seraphim — Speaking for Mercy Within Justice

The supreme seraphim are the highest of the seven revealed orders and function in seven groups. Among the Son-Spirit ministers are the 144 bestowal attendants, completion seraphim who have attained Paradise, who organize and direct all angelic activity on an Avonal’s bestowal mission. (39:1.1, 39:1.4, 39:1.5)

The account of the court advisers is especially striking. The universe tribunals exist not to hand down punishments but to adjudicate honest differences and decree survival. Justice requires that every default be adjudicated, but mercy requires that every misstep be fairly judged in light of the creature’s nature and the divine purpose. The seraphic advisers speak for that element of mercy. (39:1.7, 39:1.8)

These angels are the exponents and exemplification of the element of mercy inherent in divine justice—of fairness based on the knowledge of the underlying facts of personal motives and racial tendencies. (39:1.8)

The universe orientators ease the feeling, something like nostalgia, of ascenders leaving their local universe. Just when they think they stand at the climax of the morontia ascent, these angels help them see that they are really at the very bottom of the long ladder of spiritual ascent. Here again appears a universe principle: the end of one stage is the beginning of the next. (39:1.10, 39:1.11)

II. Superior Seraphim — The Voice of Mercy and the Universe Transporters

The superior seraphim are so named not because they are qualitatively superior but because they handle the higher activities of the local universe. They serve as Gabriel’s intelligence corps, gathering information from all the realms, and as recorders and broadcasters of Salvington’s records. Their second group, the Voice of Mercy, reveals the character of the whole order. (39:2.1, 39:2.3, 39:2.15)

Mercy is the keynote of seraphic service and angelic ministry. (39:2.5)

The description of the transporters gives a preview of what ascenders will actually experience. Until they awaken on Paradise, ascenders depend on seraphic transport to travel from world to world. Enseraphimed, they sleep for a set time and awaken at the appointed moment, while the Adjuster remains fully conscious. Angels cannot carry bodies of flesh and blood, and they do not function in natural death. (39:2.10, 39:2.11, 39:2.12, 39:2.13)

You “sow a mortal body” in the grave; you “reap a morontia form” on the mansion worlds. (39:2.13)

III. Supervisor Seraphim — Tending Law, Society, and Ethics

The supervisor seraphim have their headquarters on the constellation capitals. The law forecasters place before the lawmakers a forecast of how any proposed enactment would affect the lives of freewill creatures. They speak not for man’s passing wishes but for the true longings of the evolving morontia soul, and in this way even mortals contribute to the evolution of universe law. (39:3.1, 39:3.3)

These seraphim seek no special favors for one group or another, but they do appear before the celestial lawmakers to speak for those who cannot be present to speak for themselves. (39:3.3)

The social architects seek to strip associations of all artificiality and to bring people together on the basis of real self-understanding and mutual appreciation. Two beings form a partnership, but when three or more are grouped, it becomes a social problem and falls under their jurisdiction. They cannot always assemble the ideal group, so they use the best material available. The ethical sensitizers foster appreciation of the morality of interpersonal relationships and give their full guidance in the brotherhood schools of Edentia. (39:3.4, 39:3.5, 39:3.6, 39:3.7)

The constellation transporters do not fly like inanimate projectiles; they are fully conscious of their velocity, direction, and position, and on long journeys they average more than 555,000 miles per second. (39:3.8, 39:3.9)

IV. Administrator Seraphim — Teachers of Loyalty and Sincerity

The administrator seraphim assist with the affairs of the local systems; the present acting ruler of Urantia is aided by a thousand of them. The justice guides prepare the defense in cases of doubtful survival, seeing that justice is not defeated but dealt out with generous mercy. In the Lucifer rebellion very few justice guides were lost, but more than a quarter of the other administrator seraphim and lower seraphic orders were misled. (39:4.3, 39:4.4, 39:4.5, 39:4.6)

The interpreters of cosmic citizenship guide ascenders who have finished the mansion worlds through their first breathing spell, when as citizens of Jerusem they taste the sweetness of goal fulfillment. The quickeners of morality teach the interplay of liberty and loyalty. Loyalty is the fruit of an intelligent appreciation of universe brotherhood, and there is an order to the personality scale. (39:4.7, 39:4.10, 39:4.11)

one could not take so much and give nothing. (39:4.11)

These seraphim teach that stagnation is certain death, but overrapid growth is equally suicidal. Progress is gradual, like water flowing through a succession of short falls. And our short life on Urantia is only the first link in a long chain, so the experience of living this life matters more than what we learn in it. (39:4.12, 39:4.13)

Even the work of this world, paramount though it is, is not nearly so important as the way in which you do this work. (39:4.13)
The keys of the kingdom of heaven are: sincerity, more sincerity, and more sincerity. (39:4.14)

V. Planetary Helpers — Angels Who Plant Brotherhood, Peace, and Trust

The planetary helpers serve the Planetary Adams, the biologic uplifters of the evolutionary races. On Urantia most were withdrawn when the Adamic regime collapsed, but those who remain are still at work. Their names point to virtues that evolutionary humanity does not naturally possess, qualities heaven itself must cultivate. (39:5.1, 39:5.2)

The passage on the spirits of trust turns our usual idea of faith around. Before man trusts God, the Gods trust man: the Father freely entrusts himself, as the Adjuster, to association with us. In more advanced ages these angels teach that uncertainty is the secret of contented continuity, and that the unknown future holds romance and charm. (39:5.7, 39:5.9)

The Gods are very trustful (39:5.7)

This section also explains where the idea of winged angels came from. People who glimpsed seraphim being prepared for transport saw what looked like double sets of wings from head to foot; these are in reality energy insulators, friction shields. (39:5.12)

VI–VII. Transition Ministers; Seraphim of the Future

The transition ministers serve wherever creatures pass from the material to the spiritual estate, and in Satania they now devote their greatest efforts to educating survivors on the seven mansion worlds. They have seven assignments, seraphic evangels, racial interpreters, mind planners, morontia counselors, technicians, recorder-teachers, and ministering reserves, which are described more fully in the papers on the mansion worlds. The seraphim of the future work mainly on advanced planets in preparation for the age of light and life; since they are not now directly concerned with Urantia, their description is withheld. (39:6.1, 39:6.9, 39:7.1, 39:7.2)

VIII–IX. Seraphic Destiny; The Corps of Seraphic Completion — On to Seraphington

The eternal goal of all seraphim is Seraphington, the angelic destination of the grand universe. Some rise to high offices such as Brilliant Evening Stars in the local universe, but Seraphington always remains the angelic threshold from the ministry of time to the service of eternity. (39:8.1)

Source 39:8.2
Main routes by which seraphim reach ParadiseWhat they involve
1. Perfection of specialized servicePerfecting service as celestial artisan, Technical Adviser, or Celestial Recorder; entering the Paradise seraphic abode
2. Summons to SeraphingtonThey cannot initiate departure; they wait patiently for the summons of tertiary supernaphim
3. The evolutionary mortal techniqueAscending with a mortal ward as guardian of destiny — the surest path

Angels are created “a little higher than you,” so they cannot start from the very bottom. Instead it has been arranged that they go down to those who do start at the bottom and pilot them, step by step, to the portals of Havona. The guardians then bid their pilgrims a temporary farewell to achieve the seven circles of Seraphington, and many rejoin them on Paradise. No angel attaining Seraphington has ever gone astray. (39:8.7, 39:8.9, 39:8.10)

sin will never find response in the heart of a seraphim of completion. (39:8.9)

For angels who enter the Corps of Seraphic Completion, the career of time is finished. They become servants of God and peers of the finaliters, yet many return to their native universes to add experiential perfection to their divine endowment. What they most desire, still, is to serve the plan that sets evolutionary mortals on the journey to Paradise. (39:9.1, 39:9.2, 39:9.3)

Throughout the whole mortal adventure of finding God and of achieving divine perfection, these spirit ministers of seraphic completion, together with the faithful ministering spirits of time, are always and forever your true friends and unfailing helpers. (39:9.3)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 39 is a list of angelic duties, but the names of those duties add up to an ethic. Mercy, brotherhood, peace, trust, loyalty, and sincerity are both the angels’ names and their work. They too long to start at the bottom, grow by experience, and determine their own destiny by guiding us. Service is the path of ascension: that is the central message of this paper. (39:0.11, 39:8.5)
