Introduction — Energy, an Everlasting Monument

Paper 42 is the most “physical” paper in The Urantia Book. It covers the levels of force and energy, the classification of matter, wave phenomena, ultimatons, electrons, and atoms, and even the cohesion of the atomic nucleus. Yet the author’s starting point is not the laboratory but God. The universe is material in the sense that energy is the basis of all existence, but that pure energy is controlled by the Universal Father. (42:0.1)

Force, energy, is the one thing which stands as an everlasting monument demonstrating and proving the existence and presence of the Universal Absolute. (42:0.1)

The manipulation of energy follows the personal will of the Father, though it is modified by the acts of the Eternal Son, the Conjoint Actor, and a vast number of subordinates. None of this lessens the truth that all force-energy is under the ultimate control of a personal God resident at the center of all things. That sentence is the key to the whole paper: even a physical world that looks impersonal is bound to a personal source. (42:0.2)

I. Paradise Forces and Energies — A Stream That Returns to Its Source

The foundation of the universe is material, but the essence of life is spirit. (42:1.1)

Matter and energy are diverse manifestations of the same cosmic reality, and the lines of gravity in all matter derive from Paradise. However far science advances, even to controlling the electrical units of matter, scientists will forever be powerless to create one atom, to originate one flash of energy, or to add life to matter. The creation of energy and the bestowal of life belong to the Universal Father and his associate Creator personalities. (42:1.2, 42:1.4, 42:1.5)

Force-energy is imperishable; it can be transformed without end but never extinguished. Yet it is eternal, not infinite, and whatever goes out from Paradise must return there, even if age upon age is required to complete the circuit. The author takes the absence of any evidence of energy depletion as confirmation of a circular and orderly universe. (42:1.7, 42:1.8, 42:1.9)

That which is of Paradise Deity origin can have only a Paradise destination or a Deity destiny. (42:1.8)

II. Universal Nonspiritual Energy Systems (Physical Energies) — From Space Potency to Monota

The author first admits the limits of language. In this paper “energy” denotes all phases of phenomenal motion and potential, while “force” is used for the pregravity and “power” for the postgravity stages of energy. On that basis he proposes a classification of physical energy to lessen conceptual confusion. (42:2.1, 42:2.2)

What stands out is the author’s humility. He is quite uncertain about the early causes of force evolution, and he doubts that even the power directors fully comprehend the function of the force organizers. He confesses, too, that Paradise spirit and monota seem indistinguishable. The virtue of this section is that revelation does not pretend to explain everything; it honestly marks the boundary of what can be known. (42:2.13, 42:2.20, 42:2.21)

Matter in all universes except the central universe is identical. Its properties depend on the revolutionary rates of its component members, their number and size, their distance from the nucleus, and certain forces not yet discovered on Urantia. In suns, planets, and space bodies there are ten grand divisions of matter. (42:3.1, 42:3.2)

IV–V. Energy and Matter Transmutations; Wave-Energy Manifestations

Light, heat, electricity, magnetism, chemism, energy, and matter are one in origin, nature, and destiny. The same energy may appear as light in one universe, as heat in another, and ages later as electricity or matter. Next to gravity, temperature governs these changes: heat breaks atoms apart, while cold organizes ultimatons into electrons. And because energy is absorbed or released only in exact amounts called quanta, the integrity of matter is preserved. (42:4.1, 42:4.7, 42:4.13)

Thus matter sweeps on, undergoing the transmutations of time but swinging ever true to the circle of eternity (42:4.2)

Orvonton has one hundred octaves of wave energy, sixty-four of which are recognized on Urantia. The author sorts them into ten groups, from infraultimatonic rays to Hertzian waves, yet the human eye responds to just one octave, the light of ordinary sunlight. The world we see is a very narrow band of the world that is, and that fact teaches humility. (42:5.1, 42:5.2, 42:5.13)

The so-called ether is merely a collective name for force and energy activities in space; it does not exist. Yet space is never empty. Even the most nearly empty space in Nebadon holds about one hundred ultimatons in each cubic inch, and the space within an atom is not empty either. (42:4.6, 42:5.14, 42:5.16)

Space is not empty (42:5.16)

VI–VIII. Ultimatons, Electrons, and Atoms — A Little Universe Like the Solar System

A typical electron consists of exactly one hundred ultimatons. Ultimatons respond only to circular Paradise gravity; linear gravity becomes fully operative with the appearance of atomic organization. The formation of all matter follows the pattern of the solar system, with electrons revolving like planets about the central proton. Local universes are of decimal construction, so there are just one hundred atomic materializations, and when one hundred and one electrons have been introduced, the center has instantly broken apart. (42:6.3, 42:6.5, 42:7.1, 42:7.4, 42:7.7)

Neither electric nor gravitational force holds the atomic nucleus together. The mesotron, 180 times as heavy as the electron, keeps the nucleus intact by tossing the electric charge back and forth between protons and neutrons. Even so, the force that binds proton to proton and neutron to neutron remains an energy not yet discovered. And from the twenty-eighth atom upward, the unpredictability of the presence of the Unqualified Absolute grows. (42:8.3, 42:8.4, 42:8.6, 42:7.10)

Atoms therefore are similar to persons as to predictability. (42:7.10)

Statistics can state laws for large numbers of atoms or persons, but never for a single atom or a single person. This insight, that room for individuality exists even in the depths of the physical world, warns us against reducing people to numbers. (42:7.10)

IX. Natural Philosophy — Traces of Seven and Ten

Natural philosophy tends to dogmatize as much as religion does. The number ten, the decimal system, is inherent in energy and matter, but the number seven is basic to the central universe and the spiritual system. When the elements are arranged by atomic weight, their properties recur in groups of seven, and the author calls this a birthmark carried by the material world, pointing to its far-distant spiritual origin. (42:9.1, 42:9.2, 42:9.3)

If such a metamorphosis could not be seen, a scientist would be inclined to deny the possibility of developing a butterfly out of a caterpillar. (42:9.4)

The philosophy of the universe cannot be built on observation alone. Nature is at once stable and biologically elastic only because of the well-nigh infinite wisdom of the Master Architects. Science and faith alike need the attitude that does not conclude something is absent simply because it cannot be seen. (42:9.4, 42:9.5)

X–XI. Material Mind Systems; Universe Mechanisms — Mind Within the Machine

The latter part of the paper turns from energy to mind. The nonspiritual energies are reassociated on various levels in the living systems of non-Creator minds, and energy-matter is progressively brought under the overcontrol of spirit through the striving of living and personal mind. (42:10.1, 42:10.2)

The universe is neither mechanical nor magical; it is a creation of mind and a mechanism of law. But the mind that governs it is spirit-mind functioning on creator levels, so finite scientific methods can hardly discern even a trace of it. The principle is that the higher the mind behind a phenomenon, the harder it is for lower minds to discover it. (42:11.1, 42:11.2, 42:11.8)

the universe of universes in toto is mind planned, mind made, and mind administered. (42:11.2)

Spirituality in time and space is measured inversely to the linear-gravity response. The human material mind responds to gravity’s interactions, losing that response only in proportion to spirit identification. The very fact that mechanisms are not fixed but evolve points to the dominance of a purposive mind. (42:11.4, 42:11.7)

The evolving animal mind, while naturally God-seeking, is not alone and of itself inherently God-knowing. (42:11.8)

XII. Pattern and Form — Mind Dominance: The Spirit Has a Body

The human ability to design machines shows the superiority of creative mind. Mind reaches out from the creation of material mechanisms through the discovery of mysteries, the formulation of mental systems, and the attainment of wisdom and spirit levels, to the accomplishment of divine destinies—supreme, ultimate, and absolute. Mind is also always creative, producing a body suited to each creature’s identity. (42:12.1, 42:12.8, 42:12.9)

Most spirit beings, too, have real forms, and morontia forms differ from one another just as human bodies do. There are exceptions, such as Thought Adjusters, which appear formless until they fuse with the soul. The most striking moment of the section is a reversal of perspective. We think of a body as having a spirit; the author regards the spirit as having a body. (42:12.10, 42:12.11, 42:12.12)

The spirit is the architect, the mind is the builder, the body is the material building. (42:12.12)
Mind universally dominates matter, even as it is in turn responsive to the ultimate overcontrol of spirit. (42:12.15)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 42 descends from ultimatons to atoms and then climbs back up to mind and spirit. Energy comes from Paradise and returns to Paradise; matter is dominated by mind, and mind by the overcontrol of spirit. For mortals, the hope of survival lies in a mind that freely follows the spirit’s direction. Studying the physical world, then, becomes a way of finding our own place within this order. (42:12.13, 42:12.15)
