Introduction — A First Look at Heaven

Paper 45 introduced the administration of Satania; Paper 46 portrays its capital, Jerusem, as a world. Jerusem is an average capital of a local system and, apart from irregularities caused by the Lucifer rebellion and Michael’s bestowal, typical of such spheres. Satania has passed through stormy experiences, but it is now administered most efficiently, and the results of disharmony are being slowly but surely eradicated. (46:0.1)

the system headquarters is truly the heaven visualized by the majority of twentieth-century religious believers. (46:0.1)

This sentence sets the perspective for reading the paper. The “heaven” people have vaguely imagined is in fact closer to the first station of the ascent than to its end. And even that first station far exceeds anything we can picture. (46:2.6, 46:4.9)

I. Physical Aspects of Jerusem — Light Without Day or Night

Jerusem is divided into one thousand latitudinal sectors and ten thousand longitudinal zones, with seven major capitals and seventy minor administrative centers. The Master Physical Controllers circulate the energy charges of space through zone channels, and the heat produced by resistance in those channels keeps the temperature even. (46:1.1, 46:1.3)

The light does not come from one place; it sifts out of the whole sky and carries much less heat than sunlight. Such headquarters worlds therefore do not shine in space; Jerusem would not be visible even if it were near Urantia. For seventy-five per cent of the day the light is even; then it recedes to about the brightness of a full moon, and the whole world enjoys a quiet hour of rest and rehabilitation. (46:1.4, 46:1.5, 46:1.7)

The author compares the gases of Jerusem’s upper ionosphere, which reflect the light, to the upper-air gases on Urantia connected with the northern lights. On Urantia the same kind of gas shield turns broadcast waves back toward the earth, keeping them near the surface. Even when describing an unfamiliar world, he gives us clues from phenomena we already know. (46:1.6)

worlds like Jerusem are not subject to the vicissitudes of sun disturbances, neither are they confronted with the problem of a cooling or dying sun. (46:1.8)

II. Physical Features of Jerusem — A World Without a Struggle for Existence

Jerusem has neither earthquakes nor rainfall, no raging rivers or expansive oceans. Instead there are thousands upon thousands of small lakes joined by canals, and moisture condenses each day during the light recession. The air is like Urantia’s with a third gas added for morontia life. Material beings travel on energy currents at two to five hundred miles an hour, and transport birds fly at about one hundred. (46:2.1, 46:2.2, 46:2.3, 46:2.4)

Most striking is the order of life. Because there is no organic evolution on Jerusem, there are no conflicting forms of life, no struggle for existence, and no survival of the fittest. In their place is a creative adaptation that foreshadows the beauty and harmony of the central universe. (46:2.5)

Jerusem is indeed a foretaste of paradisiacal glory and grandeur. (46:2.6)

Yet the author reminds us that Jerusem is far more material than spiritual. It has no smoking chimneys, but it does have an intricate material economy and an extensive laboratory sector. From Mount Seraph, almost fifteen thousand feet high, a seraphic transport departs every three seconds. That the first stop is not so different from earthly life shows that the passage after death is a continuation, not an abrupt break. (46:2.7, 46:2.8)

III. The Jerusem Broadcasts — The Joy of Hearing Universe News

Superuniverse and Paradise-Havona broadcasts are received in liaison with Salvington by a technique involving the sea of glass. In addition, three groups of receiving stations take in broadcasts from the local worlds, the constellation headquarters, and the local universe capital. The amphitheater around the receiving station seats over five billion material and morontia beings. (46:3.1, 46:3.2)

of all preoccupations for an ascendant mortal on Jerusem, none is more engaging and engrossing than that of listening in on the never-ending stream of universe space reports. (46:3.1)

When Paradise messages arrive, the whole population gathers around the sea of glass, and the Uversa friends add reflectivity so that everything heard becomes visible. Listening to the broadcasts is the only activity that does not slow down during the light recession. Only the messages of Michael sometimes go directly to their destinations over the archangels’ circuit. (46:3.2, 46:3.3, 46:3.4)

The observation that there is great educational value in mingling with very different groups of personalities applies directly to our own community life today. (46:5.29)

VI–VII. The Executive-Administrative Squares; The Rectangles — The Spornagia

The executive-administrative departments occupy one thousand squares grouped in ten divisions: physical maintenance; arbitration and ethics; planetary affairs; constellation and universe affairs; education; science; morontia affairs; pure spirit activities; the ministry to ascenders; and grand universe philosophy. These structures are transparent, so even student visitors can watch all system activities. Administration with nothing to hide is the picture of heavenly government. (46:6.1, 46:6.12)

The spornagia of the rectangles are the landscape gardeners of the headquarters worlds. They have no Adjusters, no survival souls, and no personality, yet they live forty to fifty thousand standard years, and when their bodies wear out they take up residence in new ones. They are the only creatures in all Nebadon who experience reincarnation. They respond only to the first five adjutant mind-spirits and combine the traits of a faithful horse and an affectionate dog, with an intelligence exceeding that of the highest chimpanzee. (46:7.2, 46:7.3, 46:7.4, 46:7.5, 46:7.7)

Some, the author adds, believe that in a future universe age these faithful spornagia will rise above the animal level to intellectual growth and even spiritual achievement. That such a possibility may be open even to beings without soul or personality invites us to reconsider how we treat all life in creation. (46:7.8)

They do not comprehend worship of the Infinite, nor do they grasp the import of the Eternal, but they do, through affection for their masters, join in the outward spiritual devotions of their realms. (46:7.7)

VIII. The Jerusem Triangles — Erased History and the Promise of Restoration

The routine affairs of Jerusem are handled in one hundred triangles, which are surrounded by a panoramic depiction of the history of the system headquarters. In that history, however, there is an erasure of over two standard miles. It will be restored when Satania is readmitted into the constellation family. (46:8.1)

There will come an end for rebels and rebellion. (46:8.4)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 46 turns a vague notion of heaven into the concrete picture of a first station. There we find creative harmony instead of a struggle for existence, transparency instead of hidden administration, and continuity with earthly life instead of a break. And the story of Urantia, the least of worlds, stands at the center of the capital. The wound of rebellion remains, but the erased history will surely be restored. (46:2.5, 46:5.19, 46:8.3)
