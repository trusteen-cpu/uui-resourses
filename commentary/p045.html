Introduction — The First Universe Capital Ascenders Meet

Paper 43 dealt with the constellation; Paper 45 introduces the unit below it, the local system, and the administration of our own system, Satania. Its administrative center is a cluster of fifty-seven architectural spheres: Jerusem, its seven major satellites, and forty-nine subsatellites. Jerusem is almost one hundred times the size of Urantia, though its gravity is a trifle less. (45:0.1)

These fifty-seven worlds are independently lighted, heated, watered, and energized by the co-ordination of the Satania Power Center and the Master Physical Controllers, and are physically cared for by the native spornagia. They are specially created architectural worlds that do not depend on a sun as the evolutionary planets do. (45:0.3)

The seven mansion worlds are the seven subsatellites of transition world number one. (45:0.2)

What brings this paper close to home is that the mansion worlds, where we awaken after death, are part of this very system administration. It is around Jerusem that we first learn universe citizenship, make up for missed experience, and learn to live together in society. (45:6.3, 45:7.3)

II. The System Sovereign — Lanaforge, Who Held Firm Through Rebellion

The chief executive of a local system is a primary Lanonandek Son, the System Sovereign. In Nebadon these sovereigns are entrusted with unusually wide powers of personal discretion, and in all its history they have shown disloyalty only three times, the Lucifer rebellion being the last and most widespread. Even so, no changes were made afterward in the technique of system administration. (45:2.1, 45:2.2)

The present sovereign, Lanaforge, is a rebellion-tested ruler. During the second system rebellion in Nebadon, as first assistant to the fallen sovereign, he seized the reins of government so that comparatively few personalities were lost. He is the only primary Lanonandek in Nebadon who served Michael loyally in the very presence of the default of his superior. He often visits Urantia, and every ten days he holds a purely social and spiritual gathering on the sea of glass. (45:2.3, 45:2.4, 45:2.5, 45:2.6)

The Sovereign presides over the system council of world rulers, made up of the Planetary Princes and the resident governors general of the isolated worlds. The old records describe the times when this council assembles at the system headquarters in these words. (45:2.4)

When the Sons of God come together. (45:2.4)
On Jerusem there exists the utmost fraternity between all the various orders of beings and between each of these groups and the System Sovereign. (45:2.5)

III. The System Government — Seven Lanonandeks and the Executive Council

A System Sovereign is normally supported by two or three assistants, but because of the emergency of the rebellion, Satania is run by an expanded administration of seven Lanonandeks. The system is the unit of administration, not adjudication, so there are only minor courts on Jerusem. (45:3.1, 45:3.9)

These seats were placed no more than nineteen hundred years ago, soon after Michael was elevated to the full sovereignty of Nebadon, and the counselors were recruited from the eight Urantia races. (45:4.1, 45:4.2)

V–VI. The Material Sons; Adamic Training of Ascenders

At the heart of Jerusem stands the chief temple of the Material Sons. The Material Sons and Daughters are the highest type of sex-reproducing beings on the training spheres of the evolving universes, the last physical link in the chain of personality reaching from divinity to humanity. At the last registration there were 161,432,840 of them with citizenship on the system capitals of Nebadon. The Adamites of Jerusem govern themselves by universal suffrage and representative government and hope one day for virtually complete autonomy. (45:5.1, 45:5.3, 45:5.4, 45:5.6)

These Material Sons are the last and physical link in the chain of personalities extending from divinity and perfection above down to humanity and material existence below. (45:5.3)

The Material Sons and Daughters are the immediate sponsors and mentors of ascending mortals who have attained Jerusem citizenship. On the mansion worlds we can make up for nearly every experiential deprivation caused by heredity, environment, or early death, with one exception: deficiencies related to sex life. Their social, intellectual, emotional, and spiritual aspects are compensated by living closely with Adamic families. (45:6.2, 45:6.3)

The relationship of child and parent is fundamental to the essential concept of the Universal Father and his universe children. (45:6.4)

For this reason no mortal, midwayer, or seraphim may enter the Corps of the Finality without the experience of parenthood or its equivalent. Those who lack it gain it in the homes of the Material Sons on Jerusem or in the probation nursery on the finaliter world. There, children who died before acquiring spiritual status are reassembled; if either parent ascends, the child is repersonalized and given the opportunity to choose its own path. (45:6.4, 45:6.6, 45:6.7)

This passage is a deep comfort to parents who have lost a child early. It also shows how precious, in universe terms, the training of parenthood is. The labor of raising children is a school in which we learn how God deals with us. (45:6.4, 45:6.7)

VII. The Melchizedek Schools — Citizenship by Mota

The mansion worlds are detention planets. Those who did not achieve fusion with the Adjuster during life in the flesh, and those whose soul attainment was cut short by hereditary handicap or unfavorable environment, learn there the essentials of the eternal career and acquire the traits they could not gain on earth. The Melchizedeks conduct upward of thirty educational centers on Jerusem. (45:7.1, 45:7.3)

all who are true of purpose and worthy in spirit find themselves, as themselves, present on the continuing planets (45:7.1)
Representative government is the divine ideal of self-government among nonperfect beings. (45:7.3)

Suffrage on Jerusem is universal, but the value of a vote ranges from one up to one thousand according to one’s mota, or morontia wisdom. Citizens pass through three stages of examination. (45:7.6, 45:7.7)

Every one hundred years of universe time each system chooses ten representatives for the constellation legislature. Both the council of one thousand electors who choose them and the representatives themselves must be graduates of the highest school of the Melchizedek College of Administration. The vote belongs to three orders of citizenship: the Material Sons and Daughters, the seraphim and their associates, and the ascending mortals. (45:7.4, 45:7.5)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 45 may look like a description of administrative structure, but beneath it runs a theme of restoration and education. A loyal ruler holds the system steady after the wound of rebellion; a rehabilitated Adam and Eve sit on the council; and the way is open for missed experience to be made up and lost children to be reunited with their parents. The universe does not abandon those who have failed; it teaches them again. (45:2.3, 45:4.11, 45:6.7)
