Introduction — A Universe Made Perfect, Not Evolved

Paper 14 takes up Havona, the divine central universe at the heart of the universe of universes. Havona is the central planetary family of one billion spheres surrounding the Isle of Paradise, and all the creations of time and space revolve around it. The presenter states at the outset that its true magnitude is beyond the grasp of the human mind. (14:0.1)

This is a wholly created and perfect universe; it is not an evolutionary development. (14:0.2)

This one sentence is the key to the whole paper. The universe we live in is a universe of experiment and adventure, while Havona has been perfect from the beginning. The Creator Sons strive to duplicate in time and reproduce in space the perfection of this pattern universe. To know Havona, then, is to see in advance what our own universe is moving toward. (14:0.2)

I. The Paradise-Havona System — Seven Zones of Space and the Day of God

From the periphery of Paradise to the inner borders of the superuniverses lie seven space conditions and motions, one after another. Quiet zones alternate with moving belts, and two belts of dark gravity bodies revolve in opposite directions to balance the gravity of the central universe. (14:1.1, 14:1.15)

Source 14:1.1
OrderSpace condition and motion
1The quiescent midspace zones impinging on Paradise
2The three Paradise and seven Havona circuits, moving clockwise
3A semiquiet zone separating Havona from the dark gravity bodies
4The inner belt of dark gravity bodies, counterclockwise and tubular
5A unique space zone between the two belts, with up-and-down wave movements
6The outer belt of dark gravity bodies, clockwise and arranged perpendicularly
7A semiquiet zone separating the outer belt from the innermost superuniverse circuits

The billion worlds of Havona are arranged in seven concentric circuits: upwards of thirty-five million in the innermost and over two hundred and forty-five million in the outermost. Each circuit is pervaded by one of the Seven Spirits of the Circuits, a specialized representation of the Infinite Spirit. The dark gravity bodies neither reflect nor absorb light, and they hide Havona from the view of even the nearby inhabited universes. (14:1.9, 14:1.14)

The reckoning of time is also interesting. Time is not reckoned on Paradise, but each Havona world has its own local time determined by its circuit. The time required for the first Havona circuit to complete one revolution around Paradise is the Paradise-Havona standard day, and this is the standard time measurement for the seven superuniverses. (14:1.11, 14:1.12, 14:1.13)

You have unwittingly read the truth when your eyes rested on the statement “A day is as a thousand years with God, as but a watch in the night.” (14:1.12)

II. Constitution of Havona — Threefold Energy and a Universe Without Sin

Spirit beings do not dwell in nebulous space or on ethereal worlds. They live on material spheres just as real as the worlds of mortals. Havona’s substance, however, has an energy organization radically different from that of the evolutionary universes. (14:2.1, 14:2.2)

That is why a Urantia mortal transported to Havona would be deaf and blind there, utterly lacking in sense reactions, able to function only as a limited self-conscious being. Here we see why we must be long transformed through the mansion worlds and the superuniverse before we reach Havona. (14:2.4)

The urge toward God is stronger in Havona, not because spirit gravity is stronger there, but because the beings who have reached it are more fully spiritualized and so more responsive to the ever-present pull of the Son. The same drawing power is at work on us now. (14:2.7, 14:2.8)

There has never been an instance of misconduct by any creature of any group of personalities ever created in, or admitted to, the central Havona universe. (14:2.9)

III. The Havona Worlds — Perfect Self-Government Without Government

Havona has no government. There are no courts and no legislative assemblies, only administrative direction. The natives are perfect by nature, and the evolutionary creatures among them long ago passed the scrutiny of the supreme tribunals of the superuniverses. Each sphere is directed by one Eternal of Days, a Trinity-origin personality who is not a creator but a perfect administrator and teacher. (14:3.1, 14:3.2, 14:3.3)

Here may be observed the height of the ideals of true self-government. (14:3.1)

The pilgrims of time land on the receiving worlds of the outer seventh circuit and advance inward, planet by planet and circle by circle. That only about one per cent of planetary capacity is now used for mortal ascension shows that this universe is not for us alone. There are real rivers and lakes there, but its beauty, we are told, must be seen to be understood. (14:3.4, 14:3.5, 14:3.7)

IV. Creatures of the Central Universe — Life Without Decay or Death

Life in Havona exists in seven basic forms: material, morontial, spiritual, absonite, ultimate, coabsolute, and absolute. Decay and death are no part of its cycle; even the lower living things do not decay but change form. The Havona natives are all offspring of the Paradise Trinity; they have no creature parents and do not reproduce. (14:4.1, 14:4.9, 14:4.10)

Havoners help the ascenders, yet they also live lives of their own that have meaning in themselves. The paper sets them side by side with us. (14:4.12, 14:4.13)

Man rejoices in the goodness of God, Havoners exult in the divine beauty, while you both enjoy the ministry of the liberty of living truth. (14:4.13)

Havona is also home to pattern beings for every group of beings in creation. All beings in all universes are fashioned along the lines of some order of pattern creature living on one of the billion worlds of Havona, and even the mortals of time have their goal and ideals of creature existence on its outer circuits. What we are becoming already exists there. (14:4.18)

On every Havona world there are also attainment candidates, those who have physically reached the central universe but have not yet grown spiritually enough to claim Paradise residence. The Infinite Spirit teaches and trains them through a host of personalities of grace and glory. That learning and waiting continue even so near the destination reminds us that spiritual growth depends not on a change of place but on inner maturing. (14:4.19, 14:4.20)

V. Life in Havona — A University of Endless Surprises

Each stage of the ascent has its own character. On Urantia we pass through a short and intense test; on the mansion worlds and in the local universe, the morontia phases; in the superuniverse, the true spirit stages; and on the seven circuits of Havona, intellectual, spiritual, and experiential attainment. The rules of Havona are not arbitrary, and when we arrive there we will naturally enjoy doing things the way they should be done. (14:5.1, 14:5.3)

This arrangement of circuits is also the source of the name “circles of progress” for the human mind. And no two of the billion worlds are alike, because each has been developed and embellished according to the plans of its resident Eternal of Days. (14:5.4, 14:5.6)

Monotony is not a part of the Havona career. (14:5.9)

The most comforting passage in this section interprets human curiosity. Love of adventure, curiosity, and dread of monotony were not given to annoy us during our short time on earth. They suggest that death is only the beginning of an endless adventure, and they are a divine endowment that, though often restrained and disappointed now, will be fully gratified in the ages to come. (14:5.10, 14:5.11)

death is only the beginning of an endless career of adventure, an everlasting life of anticipation, an eternal voyage of discovery. (14:5.10)

VI. The Purpose of the Central Universe — What It Gives Seven Orders

The line in this list that comes closest to mortals concerns the Father. The eternal Father views Havona with never-ending satisfaction as the alluring goal for the ascension candidates of time, his mortal grandchildren of space who are coming to his home. Our ascent is not only our own hope; it is a homecoming the Father awaits. (14:6.11)

Havona stands before every will creature as the portal to Paradise and God attainment. (14:6.38)
Paradise is the home, and Havona the workshop and playground, of the finaliters. (14:6.39)

Havona is not only the established destiny of mortals but also the starting place of the finaliters’ new adventure of exploring the infinity of the Father. The presenter foresees that Havona’s potentials are really unlimited and that it will serve forever as an experiential training universe for all types of created beings, past, present, and future. (14:6.40, 14:6.41)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 14 shows us ahead of time the destination of our journey. Havona is a universe made perfect rather than evolved, a place where everyone gladly walks the right way, so that no government is needed. And it is not a static paradise but a university of endless surprises. The curiosity and longing to grow within us now are seeds of that life.

Paper 15 steps out of Havona to explain the organization and administration of the seven superuniverses, the creations of time to which we actually belong. Having first seen the perfect pattern, we can better understand why the unfinished evolutionary universes grow toward it.
