Introduction — The Corps That Awaits at the End of the Ascent

Paper 30 traced the seven stages from planet to Paradise; Paper 31 describes the Mortal Corps of the Finality, which the ascender enters at the end of that journey. This corps is the present known destination of the Adjuster-fused mortals of time, yet it is not a body of mortals alone. Six groups make up this unique association. (31:0.1, 31:0.8)

All who are admitted to the corps are received as equals, but that equality never removes individuality or personal identity. In communicating with a finaliter, we are told, one can at once discern whether he is an ascendant mortal or a seraphim. That oneness does not mean sameness is a principle that applies to every relationship that forms a community. (31:0.9)

this exalted equality in no way abrogates individuality or destroys personal identity. (31:0.9)

The finaliters now return to serve in the universes of time, and only after working in all six other superuniverses are they assigned to their native superuniverse; in this way they gain a sevenfold concept of the Supreme Being. At least one company is always serving on Urantia. They are a self-governing body that chooses its own leaders, and their oath of allegiance is given only to the Paradise Trinity. (31:0.10, 31:0.11, 31:0.12)

They are indeed the accumulating tried and true souls of time and space—the evolutionary salt of the universe—and they are forever proof against evil and secure against sin. (31:0.13)

I–II. The Havona Natives and the Gravity Messengers — The Perfect and the Perfected

Many Havona natives who teach the pilgrims become so attached to ascending mortals that millions upon millions of them are now on the waiting list. They are perfect beings of direct and divine creation, yet they must achieve a certain experiential development alongside evolutionary beings before they can receive a fragment of the Father’s spirit. (31:1.1, 31:1.2)

The finaliters thus embrace both phases of experiential existence—perfect and perfected. (31:1.1)

The Gravity Messengers are modified and personalized Adjusters from Divinington, superspirit beings who seem to make use of all energies and circuits, even gravity. Mortal finaliters cannot defy time and space, but these beings, who can, are under their command. Because both are deeply related to fragments of the Father, they hold a profound affection for each other. (31:2.1, 31:2.2, 31:2.4)

Finaliters are mobilized by companies, but the oath of finality is always taken individually. They work together, yet each makes the eternal decision alone. (31:1.3)

III. Glorified Mortals — One Stage Still to Come

Even the beings of Uversa do not know the “finality destiny” of ascending mortals. They believe, however, that so long a training must be meant to prepare them for greater trust and responsibility. A telling fact is offered in support: finaliters are recorded only as spirits of the sixth stage. (31:3.2, 31:3.3)

The mortal finaliters have fully obeyed the command “Be you perfect,” but the paper carefully distinguishes their achievement in three ways. They have reached the present limit of spirit progression, creature perfection, and Deity worship, but not the finality of ultimate status, service, and experiential Deity attainment. This distinction—that completion is not the end—offers a humble view of growth. (31:3.6)

They have achieved the present limit of creature perfection but not finality of creature service. (31:3.6)

The author says that human beings are entitled to share in speculating about this mystery, and compares the present assignments to postgraduate courses in universe administration. Then he leaves us with a question. (31:3.8)

“Why should the Gods be so concerned in so thoroughly training surviving mortals in the technique of universe management?” (31:3.8)

IV–VII. Companions in the Ascent and the Vacant Place

Many faithful seraphic guardians pass through the ascending career with their mortal subjects, and after the mortal’s Father fusion they take the finaliter oath together, accepting the mortal’s destiny forever. The angel who guarded us on earth may become a companion to the very end. (31:4.1)

Material Sons also enter this path: Adamic citizens whose planetary assignment is long delayed may ascend by petition, and planetary Adams and Eves may choose to ascend in the later ages of light and life. Those who default in their mission, like Urantia’s Adam, take the same natural course as their people—receiving Adjusters, passing through death, and ascending by faith. It is moving that the way stays open even after failure. Midway creatures likewise begin their ascent once their native world is settled in light and life, and all secondary midwayers eventually fuse with Adjusters. (31:5.1, 31:5.2, 31:6.1, 31:6.2)

At present every company has 999 permanent members, and the one vacancy is filled by the chief of a temporary group, the Evangels of Light. There are also only six classes of permanent members for now. Even the authors on Uversa differ about who the seventh group will be; they expect it will be known only when the finaliters enter the seventh stage of spirit attainment. (31:7.1, 31:7.3, 31:7.5)

VIII–IX. The Transcendentalers and the Architects of the Master Universe — Neither Creators nor Creatures

On Paradise the finaliter seeks to understand the Transcendentalers, more than a thousand groups of absonite beings who neither create nor are created but simply eventuate. They live in the western area of Paradise and have nothing to do with the affairs of Havona or the superuniverses; they are concerned only with the superadministration of the master universe. Man’s first serious contact with them comes when he takes the finaliter oath, which is administered by the chief of the Transcendentalers. (31:8.1, 31:8.2, 31:8.3, 31:8.4)

These Transcendentalers create no beings, neither were they ever created. (31:8.3)

The Architects of the Master Universe are the governing corps of the Transcendentalers, numbering 28,011 personalities in all. The mandate for these narratives forbids fully revealing their origin, nature, and destiny, though their existence may be disclosed. We are told, however, that they exist on seven levels of the absonite. (31:9.1, 31:9.2)

The Architects give technical approval to the space locations where Creator Sons will organize local universes, and in association with the Supreme Creators of the grand universe and the evolving Supreme Being they form the Trinity Ultimate. (31:9.10, 31:9.14)

X. The Ultimate Adventure — Toward the Outer Universes

The senior Master Architect oversees the seven Corps of the Finality, whose heads form the Supreme Council of Destiny; in this age it is presided over by Grandfanda, the first mortal ascender. The mobilization of these seven corps is the first such event since the near-eternal days when the superuniverse rulers were commissioned, and it is understood as the grand universe’s response to the future needs of the outer universes. (31:10.9, 31:10.10)

The outer universes will come into being lacking one thing: the experience of taking part in the evolution of the Supreme Being. We now face difficulties because the sovereignty of God the Supreme is incompletely actualized, yet for that very reason we share in the experience of his evolution. The paradox is that our present imperfection is a privilege that will not come again. (31:10.11, 31:10.12)

We evolve in him and he evolves in us. (31:10.12)
These deficiencies are inevitable on all levels of universe existence. (31:10.14)

So the authors conjecture that the Corps of the Finality may be destined to administer the outer universes, compensating for their experiential deficiency, just as higher spiritual beings now come down to minister to the mortals of the evolutionary universes. At the same time they honestly state that of only three things are they certain. (31:10.13, 31:10.14, 31:10.15)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 31 completes the thirty-one papers of Part I, which began with the nature of Deity and passed through the central universe, the superuniverses, and the personalities of the grand universe. The papers state that they were sponsored and formulated in 1934 by a high commission of twenty-four Orvonton administrators, under a mandate of the Ancients of Days of Uversa. The final scene of that long account is a mortal born on a planet who finds God and waits on Paradise for the next assignment of service. (31:10.20, 31:10.22)
