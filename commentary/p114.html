Introduction — The Unseen Hand in the Kingdoms of Men

Paper 113 described the guardian angel at one person’s side; Paper 114 shows the superhuman government that cares for a whole planet. The author, the Chief of Seraphim stationed on Urantia, even gives the result of that day’s noon roll call: hundreds of millions of pairs of seraphim are at work on this world. Ably assisted by the midwayers, the angels carry out the mandates of the resident governor general and his subordinates. (114:0.2, 114:0.3, 114:7.18)

The Most Highs rule in the kingdoms of men through many celestial forces and agencies but chiefly through the ministry of seraphim. (114:0.1)

The planetary government of Urantia is unlike that of any other world in Satania, or even in all Nebadon. The author traces this uniqueness to seven unusual circumstances. (114:0.4)

I. The Sovereignty of Urantia — Michael and the Vicegerent Prince

The sovereignty of Urantia was first held in trust by the sovereign of the Satania system, and after the downfall of Caligastia the planet had no settled relationship with the local universe. When Michael completed his bestowal in the flesh, the Union of Days proclaimed him Planetary Prince of Urantia, and this forever settled the status of our world. (114:1.1)

Michael, however, does not administer the planet personally; he entrusted it to a Jerusem commission of twenty-four former Urantians. Machiventa Melchizedek has recently been designated vicegerent Prince but has not yet taken up his duties. Celestial beings differ on when he will come, and the author himself looks for his appearance any day or hour. (114:1.2, 114:1.3, 114:1.4)

II–III. The Board of Planetary Supervisors; The Resident Governor General

The twenty-four counselors on Jerusem were all real leaders during their lives on earth, and their leadership has been deepened by mansion world experience. When the council now sits, its chairman is John, known to us as “the Baptist.” They also advise the thirty-six other rebellion-isolated worlds of Satania. (114:2.1, 114:2.3, 114:2.4)

Every hundred years the council sends one of its members to Urantia to serve as resident governor general. While these narratives were being prepared, the nineteenth was succeeded by the twentieth. The governor general has no personal authority, yet each day he hands down scores of rulings that all concerned accept as final. (114:3.1, 114:3.3)

He is the co-ordinating director recognized by every order of angelic host, and since the departure of 1-2-3 the first, the United Midwayers have looked upon the successive governors general as their planetary fathers. The council keeps in close touch with Michael through the Brilliant Evening Star who attended Jesus throughout his mortal bestowal. No one knows how long the counselors will serve as they do now; it will likely continue until some change such as the end of a dispensation, the final adjudication of the rebellion, or Michael’s return. (114:2.2, 114:2.6, 114:3.2)

The name of the current planetary supervisor is withheld from you only because mortal man is so prone to venerate, even to deify, his extraordinary compatriots and superhuman superiors. (114:3.1)
He is much more of a fatherly adviser than a technical ruler. (114:3.3)

The reason for withholding the name holds a lesson for us today. Respect for outstanding people slides easily into veneration, and then we end up looking at a person instead of at God. (114:3.1)

IV–V. The Most High Observer; The Planetary Government

Shortly after the rebellion the Most Highs of Edentia seized certain planetary authority, and so a Vorondadek Son still resides here as the Most High observer. In grave crises he may take over the planetary government, and this has happened thirty-three times in the history of Urantia. In purely spiritual matters, though, the supreme authority seems to rest with the commanding archangel at the divisional headquarters of the archangels. (114:4.1, 114:4.3, 114:4.4)

The Most High observer keeps close advisory relations with the planetary supervisors and also serves as advisory director of the race commissioners, who are very active on Urantia. Such Vorondadek regencies are not limited to rebel worlds: the Most Highs may intervene on any inhabited planet to bring the superior wisdom of the constellation rulers into the kingdoms of men. Unseen wisdom stands behind the great currents of history. (114:4.2, 114:4.5)

The actual administration of Urantia is hard to describe in terms of a formal separation of powers. The structure the text describes can be summarized like this. (114:5.1)

This loosely organized government works better than expected thanks to the circuit of the archangels, which can be used in an emergency to get around the planet’s spiritual isolation. And above all, isolation matters little to the individual. (114:5.3, 114:5.4)

Planetary isolation is, of course, of little concern to individual mortals since the pouring out of the Spirit of Truth upon all flesh nineteen hundred years ago. (114:5.4)

Each administrative day on Urantia begins with a consultative conference attended by the governor general, the planetary chief of archangels, the Most High observer, the supervising supernaphim, the chief of resident Life Carriers, and invited guests. Most problems involving seraphim and midwayers are settled by mutual consent, and even the governor general’s rulings may be appealed to conciliating commissions or to the System Sovereign. The unseen government, too, follows the fair processes of consultation and appeal. (114:5.2, 114:5.5)

VI. The Master Seraphim of Planetary Supervision — Twelve Corps for Every Sphere of Life

When the Spirit of Truth was poured out, the first governor general arrived accompanied by twelve corps of special seraphim, graduates of Seraphington. These are the master seraphim of planetary supervision, and the twelve acting chiefs of their groups form the governor general’s cabinet. The author, a supernaphim of the primary order, presides over this council. (114:5.6, 114:6.1, 114:6.3)

The progress angels and the religious guardians hold each other in check. One labors to make things what they ought to be; the other carries the imperishable values of old and passing forms into new patterns. The tension between change and conservation is a condition of healthy civilization. (114:6.6, 114:6.7)

These angels do contend for spiritual forms, but they are not the source of ultrasectarianism and meaningless controversial divisions of professed religionists. (114:6.7)

None of these angelic groups controls its domain arbitrarily. They manipulate conditions and associate circumstances so as to exert a favorable influence. They act as ideational clearinghouses, mind focalizers, and project promoters, but they do not push into the human mind ideas that are not there. (114:6.18, 114:6.19)

While unable to inject new and higher conceptions into human minds, they often act to intensify some higher ideal which has already appeared within a human intellect. (114:6.19)

VII. The Reserve Corps of Destiny — Unknown Servants

The reserve corps of destiny consists of living men and women admitted to the service of the superhuman administration of world affairs. As soon as someone appears with sufficient mental capacity, adequate moral status, and requisite spirituality, that person is assigned as a mortal assistant to a celestial group and given personal destiny guardians. Urantia has twelve reserve corps, one for each of the twelve groups of seraphic supervision. (114:7.1, 114:7.2, 114:7.7)

The corps now numbers 962 persons, and apart from fewer than a score of contact personalities, none of them knows they are being prepared for planetary crises. They are trained in the deep mind by the combined technique of Adjuster and seraphic ministry and emerge only in rare emergencies, to prevent the breakdown of culture or the extinction of the light of living truth. The text adds that it was through just such a combination of cosmic adjustments that these revelations were materialized in English. (114:7.8, 114:7.9)

Reservists also act, unknowingly, as conservators of essential planetary information. When a reservist dies, vital data are often passed to a younger successor through a liaison of the two Adjusters. The corps has no permanent head but has permanent councils, among them the judiciary council, the historicity council, and the council on political sovereignty, and from time to time titular heads are commissioned for a task lasting a few hours. Membership was largest in the days of the Adamites and Andites, lowest around Pentecost, and has grown steadily since. (114:7.10, 114:7.11, 114:7.12)

There is operative on the planet a very definite and effective superhuman supervision of world affairs and human destinies. (114:7.14)

The author admits that Urantia is far from an ideal planetary government. Compared with the other isolated worlds, though, it is not greatly inferior, and sweeping changes are expected when Michael comes again, if not before. (114:7.15, 114:7.16)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 114 ends in comfort. Urantia was isolated by rebellion, but it is not a cosmic orphan. From Uversa to Paradise they all know we are here, and the people of this world are cherished as lovingly as if it had never been betrayed—even more so. (114:7.17)

Your isolated world is not forgotten in the counsels of the universe. (114:7.17)
