Introduction — The Many Mansions in the Father’s Universe

Paper 47 turns the “many mansions” Jesus spoke of into concrete places. All fifty-six worlds around the system capital, Jerusem, are devoted to the transitional culture of ascending mortals, but the seven satellites of the first transition world are known in particular as the mansion worlds. This is where people first awaken, learn, and grow after death. (47:0.1)

In a certain sense, all fifty-six of the encircling worlds of Jerusem are devoted to the transitional culture of ascending mortals, but the seven satellites of world number one are more specifically known as the mansion worlds. (47:0.1)

These worlds do not run by themselves. Many orders of beings share in their administration, and when a system is settled in light and life, the mansion worlds one by one stop serving as training stations and are taken over by finaliters. The mansion worlds, in other words, are not an eternal destination but a school for the needs of an age. (47:0.3, 47:0.4)

I. The Finaliters’ World — Parenthood No One Can Escape

The first transition world is the headquarters of the finaliter corps assigned to Satania. The temple of the finaliters is not visible even to early morontia vision, but with the help of the energy transformers students can now and then actually perceive their activities. The nearness of brethren who have attained Paradise is a quiet encouragement throughout the ascent. (47:0.2, 47:1.2, 47:1.3)

The most striking feature of this section is the parental commission. On the first mansion world every survivor must pass the requirements of the parental commission from his or her native planet. The present Urantia commission consists of twelve couples who have reared three or more children to the pubescent age. Those who do not satisfy it qualify further by serving in the homes of the Material Sons on Jerusem or in the probationary nursery. (47:1.4)

Children need fathers as well as mothers, and fathers need this parental experience as much as do mothers. (47:1.6)

The idea that child culture is largely the task of mothers is called an unfortunate and mistaken notion. Raising children is an essential part of completing personality, and if it is missed on earth, it must be made up in the heavens. This section reminds us that the time spent with children at home today is part of eternal growth. (47:1.6)

II. The Probationary Nursery — Children Who Left Early

Children who die before acquiring individual status on the universe records are not forgotten. If either parent survives, cherubim deputized by the guardian of destiny take custody of the child’s potential identity and deliver it to the probationary nursery on the finaliter world. The child awakens at the exact time of the parent’s arrival on the first mansion world, in the exact physical status it had at death. (47:2.1, 47:2.2)

These children, too, have freedom of choice. Those who reject the ascent simply become as though they had not been, without adjudication; those who choose the Paradise path go at once to the mansion worlds, and many arrive in time to join their parents in the Havona ascent. Because they were deprived of evolutionary experience, they do not enter the Corps of the Finality but become the permanent ascendant citizenship of Paradise. (47:2.7, 47:2.8)

III. The First Mansion World — Starting Again Where We Left Off

At the very center of the first mansion world stands the resurrection hall, the temple of personality assembly. Resurrection is the reuniting of what two custodians have kept. When the morontia soul entrusted to the seraphic destiny guardian is reunited with the spirit-mind held by the Adjuster, the sleeping survivor awakens. (47:3.2, 47:3.3)

You begin over there right where you leave off down here. (47:3.7)

Death lets no one skip growth. Between death and resurrection a person gains nothing but the fact of survival. That is why almost the entire work of the first world is deficiency ministry, the correction and cure of defects left by life in the flesh. After awakening, each person has ten days of liberty to explore the new home and to look up loved ones who arrived earlier. (47:3.6, 47:3.7, 47:3.8)

On this world ascenders meet their Morontia Companions, one assigned to each company of a thousand. Offspring of the Mother Spirit, they are excursion guides and leisure associates who accompany ascenders even on visits to Jerusem. It is moving that, for those taking their first steps in a strange world, friends are provided first of all. (47:3.11, 47:3.12)

IV. The Second Mansion World — Healing for Community and Intellect

On the second world, working groups and social organizations take shape, and the sea of glass appears for the first time. A new morontia body is acquired at each advance, but now the Adjuster does not leave during the transit sleep, and personality continues unbroken. The main task here is the removal of intellectual conflict and the cure of mental disharmony. (47:4.1, 47:4.3, 47:4.4, 47:4.8)

In all the worlds of ascension you will find nothing comparable to human intolerance and the discriminations of inconsiderate caste systems. (47:4.2)

The account of memory is also a comfort. Purely animal associations perish with the brain, but everything with survival value is counterparted by the Adjuster and retained. No worth-while experience we have had on earth is ever thrown away. (47:4.5)

Even with morontia bodies, ascenders continue to eat, drink, and rest through all seven worlds, though morontia food is fully utilized and leaves no waste. The first world is still very material, but with each sphere one becomes less material, more intellectual, and slightly more spiritual. Spiritual progress is greatest on the last three worlds. (47:4.6)

V–VI. The Third and Fourth Mansion Worlds — From Remedy to Progress, From I to We

The third world is the headquarters of the Mansion World Teachers, advanced and glorified cherubim. The training of the first two worlds is mostly remedial, supplementing the life in the flesh; here truly progressive morontia culture begins. Morontia mota is co-ordinated with human philosophy, and ascenders are introduced for the first time to the intelligent comprehension of cosmic meanings. (47:5.1, 47:5.3)

On the fourth world ascenders are first introduced to the true social life of morontia creatures. Social activity based neither on personal aggrandizement nor on self-seeking conquest is an entirely new experience for evolutionary creatures. Those used to competition on earth learn here an order built on mutual appreciation and service. (47:6.2, 47:6.3)

Ascenders are all becoming self-conscious of God-knowing, God-revealing, God-seeking, and God-finding. (47:6.3)

VII. The Fifth Mansion World — The Birth of Cosmic Consciousness

The move to the fifth world is a tremendous step forward. Experience here is a real foretaste of Jerusem life, and having mastered the local universe language, ascenders perfect the tongue of Uversa and become bilingual. Here, too, they meet the first instructors who prepare them for the constellation study worlds. (47:7.1, 47:7.2, 47:7.4)

Study is becoming voluntary, unselfish service natural, and worship spontaneous. (47:7.5)

This sentence shows the mark of growth. Study, service, and worship that began as duties come at some point to well up from within. This is how a real morontia character begins to bud, and we can rehearse the same change here on earth. (47:7.5)

VIII. The Sixth Mansion World — Adjuster Fusion

The sixth world usually witnesses the brilliant event of perfect fusion between the human mind and the divine Adjuster. When fusion is confirmed, messengers speak before the survivor’s associates, and the new being, introduced by a new name, is granted forty days of spiritual retirement in which to choose a route to Havona. (47:8.3, 47:8.4, 47:8.5)

This is a beloved son in whom I am well pleased. (47:8.4)

Even so, these beings are far from being true spirits, still a little lower than the angels. Yet as the worlds are ascended, the shadow of mortal nature grows less, and those who have come up through great tribulation become very kind and understanding, sympathetic and tolerant. Hardship is being turned into loveliness. (47:8.6, 47:8.7)

IX. The Seventh Mansion World — The Last Traces Washed Away

The seventh world is the crowning achievement of the immediate postmortal career. Differences between those from retarded worlds and those from advanced ones are virtually obliterated here, and the remnants of unfortunate heredity and unwholesome environment are purged. On the seventh transition world ascenders begin a new and more spiritual worship of the unseen Father. (47:9.1, 47:9.2)

Until now ascenders have moved from world to world as individuals, but they leave for Jerusem together, in classes. That one may choose to tarry on the seventh world so that a slower member of one’s group can catch up shows that the ascent is not a race to get ahead alone. (47:9.3)

X. Jerusem Citizenship — After the Last Awakening

The arrival of a class of graduates is the signal for all Jerusem to assemble in welcome. Mortals who pass through the whole mansion world career experience the adjustment sleep and resurrection awakening seven times, but the last awakening chamber was left behind on the seventh world. From now on, changes of form never again interrupt consciousness or memory. (47:10.1, 47:10.4, 47:10.5)

Mortal death is a technique of escape from the material life in the flesh (47:10.7)

The end of the paper gathers the whole picture. Death is a technique of escape; the seven mansion worlds are the introduction to the morontia career; and the morontia career is the transition life between material existence and spirit attainment. Death is not an end but the door to the next grade of the school. (47:10.7)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 47 portrays the afterlife not as a vague paradise but as a school with stages. That school resumes our lessons where we left off, lets us make up what we missed on earth, and leads us at last to social and spiritual maturity. What we learn in this world, then, is never wasted. However far we get here, that is how far ahead we start there. (47:3.7, 47:5.3)
