Introduction — Angels, Authorities, and Powers

Paper 38 introduces the angels of the local universe. The author begins by noting that the personalities of the Infinite Spirit fall into three distinct orders, and takes a New Testament verse as his clue. The apostle who wrote that angels and authorities and powers had been made subject to Jesus had already captured this division. (38:0.1)

Source 38:0.1
Biblical termOrder of the Infinite Spirit’s personalities
AngelsThe ministering spirits of time
AuthoritiesThe messenger hosts of space
PowersThe higher personalities of the Infinite Spirit

As the supernaphim serve in the central universe and the seconaphim in a superuniverse, so the angelic corps of a local universe consists of seraphim, cherubim, and sanobim. Seraphim are almost uniform in design throughout all seven superuniverses, the most nearly standard of all personal spirit beings. They are the skilled and common ministers of the local creations. (38:0.2, 38:0.3)

I. Origin of Seraphim — The Mother Spirit’s First Solitary Creation

Seraphim are created by the Universe Mother Spirit. After the Creator Son and the Mother Spirit have together created many Sons, the Son turns to creating the Material Sons, the first sex creatures, while the Mother Spirit undertakes her first solitary effort at spirit reproduction. So begins the seraphic host, projected in units of 41,472 at a time. (38:1.1)

The creation of seraphim coincides with the planning for the evolution of mortal will creatures. Before that, the seraphim serving in Nebadon were on loan from a neighboring universe. And their creation still goes on, because the universe is still in the making. (38:1.2, 38:1.3)

The Universe Mother Spirit never ceases creative activity in a growing and perfecting universe. (38:1.3)

II. Angelic Natures — Fellow Servants Who Know and Love Us

Angels have no material bodies, but they are definite and discrete beings. We cannot see them, yet they perceive us as we are in the flesh. They understand the mode of mortal life, share our nonsensuous emotions and sentiments, and greatly enjoy our music, art, and real humor. They are fully aware of our moral struggles and spiritual difficulties. (38:2.1)

They love human beings, and only good can result from your efforts to understand and love them. (38:2.1)

Seraphim are affectionate and sympathetic but are not sex-emotion creatures. In many powers they far surpass us; we would regard one as a “mathematical prodigy,” knowing such things as the number of our hairs without effort. Yet in personal status they are not far removed from human beings, and once delivered from the flesh we become very much like them. (38:2.2, 38:2.3, 38:2.6)

The two most practical teachings of this section concern judgment and worship. Angels are above us in spiritual status, but they are not our judges or accusers. And angels are to be loved, not adored. As the seraphim Loyalatia told the seer, they are fellow servants who, like us, are enjoined to worship God. (38:2.4, 38:2.5)

Angels do not sit in judgment on mankind, neither should individual mortals prejudge their fellow creatures. (38:2.4)
You do well to love them, but you should not adore them (38:2.5)

III–IV. Unrevealed Angels; The Seraphic Worlds — A Home Shared in Pairs

In this paper the word “angel” is limited to the seraphim and their associated offspring, who are so largely concerned with the plans of mortal survival. The local universe also has six other orders of unrevealed angels, but they are never called seraphim and are occupied with administrative affairs unrelated to man’s ascent. (38:3.1)

The ninth group of seven primary spheres in the Salvington circuit, each with six satellites, are the seraphic worlds. The seraphim exclusively occupy the first cluster, while the six unrevealed angelic orders occupy the others. The seraphic estates are marked by beauty and vastness, and a home there is the dwelling of two seraphim. Though not male and female, seraphim are negative and positive, and most assignments require two angels. (38:4.1, 38:4.2, 38:4.3)

Here each seraphim has a real home, and “home” means the domicile of two seraphim (38:4.2)

Seraphim gather for reunions every millennium. One whose duties forbid absence alternates with her complement, so that every partner is present at least every other reunion. It is a wise way to keep both the work and the bonds of community. (38:4.4)

V. Seraphic Training — From Observer to Ministering Spirit

Seraphim are not sent straight into service. The first Nebadon seraphim were trained by a corps of one thousand seraphim from Avalon, and since then the seniors have taught those who came after. The Melchizedeks also take a large part in the education of all local universe angels. (38:5.1)

Once commissioned, seraphim may range all Nebadon and even Orvonton, always linking the beings of the spirit world with the mortals of the material realms. The process mirrors the principle of universe education: responsibility is given only after experience has been gained. (38:5.4)

Their work in the universe is without bounds and limitations (38:5.4)

After training, seraphim are organized in units built on twelve. The legions Jesus spoke of in Gethsemane, when he said the Father would give him “more than twelve legions of angels,” are one of these organizational units. (38:6.1)

Above all these armies stands Gabriel, the “supreme commander of the armies of heaven.” Seraphim serve under the supervision of the Infinite Spirit as personalized on Salvington, but since Michael’s bestowal on Urantia all local universe orders have become subject to the Master Son’s sovereignty. When Michael was born in the flesh, a broadcast went out to all Nebadon: “And let all the angels worship him.” (38:6.2, 38:6.3)

VII. Cherubim and Sanobim — The Angels Closest to Us

Cherubim and sanobim share the origin and essential endowments of the seraphim, but they are the lowest order of angels. For that very reason they are the nearest of kin to the more progressive human beings of the evolutionary worlds. They are wonderfully intelligent, marvelously efficient, touchingly affectionate, and almost human. (38:7.1)

The cherubim is the positively charged senior personality; the sanobim is the negatively charged complement. They are inherently associated and functionally united, so they usually serve in pairs. They are the routine spirit workers on the individual worlds, assisting the seraphim, but they never serve as attending angels to human beings; that is an exclusive seraphic privilege. (38:7.2, 38:7.3, 38:7.4)

Cherubim assigned to a planet begin by studying its usages and languages. All ministering spirits speak two languages, that of their local universe and that of their superuniverse, and they acquire more in the schools of the realms. Every fourth cherubim is a quasi-material “fourth creature,” doing borderland work on the morontia training worlds much as the midway creatures do on the evolutionary planets. (38:7.5, 38:7.6, 38:7.7)

all creatures having actual or potential personality volition seek new achievements. (38:7.5)

VIII. Evolution of Cherubim and Sanobim — Two Embraces

Cherubim and sanobim, too, have open avenues of service that lead to higher status. Not all have the same potential, however; with regard to evolution they fall into three classes. (38:8.1)

Experienced cherubim attached to guardians of destiny are left behind when their seraphic seniors depart with their wards. They are embraced by the Mother Spirit and become Mansion World Teachers; after long service they are re-embraced in groups of seven thousand and emerge as full-fledged seraphim. From then on they may serve as guardians of destiny, with a path open all the way to Paradise and the Corps of the Finality. Being left behind proves to be not an end but the door to a new beginning. (38:8.5, 38:8.6)

IX. The Midway Creatures — A Bridge Between Matter and Spirit

The midway creatures have a threefold classification. They belong properly with the ascending Sons of God, factually with the orders of permanent citizenship, and functionally with the ministering spirits, since they serve mortals together with the angels. They appear on most inhabited worlds and are always found on decimal planets such as Urantia. (38:9.1, 38:9.2)

The two orders can establish perfect synchrony with each other, so together they can use the whole range of energy, from the gross physical power of the material worlds up to the higher spirit forces. The paper then says that the gap between matter and spirit is bridged without a break by a chain of beings, and that within a single person these levels are unified by the mysterious work of the Thought Adjuster. (38:9.8, 38:9.9)

The gap between the material and spiritual worlds is perfectly bridged by the serial association of mortal man, secondary midwayer, primary midwayer, morontia cherubim, mid-phase cherubim, and seraphim. (38:9.9)

On a world like Urantia, where the Planetary Prince defected and the Material Son failed, the midwayers become wards of the System Sovereign and work under the acting planetary custodian. If faithful to their trust, they will one day be mustered into the ranks of the ascending Sons and begin the Paradise adventure together with the very earth brethren they have served so long. (38:9.10, 38:9.13)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

The angels of Paper 38 are not distant or fearsome beings. They are fellow servants who know us as we are and love us, who do not accuse us, and who refuse our worship. They are commissioned only after long training, they work in pairs, and even when left behind they find a new path of growth. And every gap between matter and spirit is bridged by beings such as these. (38:2.1, 38:2.4, 38:9.9)
