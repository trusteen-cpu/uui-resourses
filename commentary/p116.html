Introduction — The Religious Paradox of Inequality

Paper 116 deals with the power aspect of the Supreme Being, the Almighty Supreme. The author begins with an old question: if God is perfect, why is the world so imperfect and unequal? The perfect spheres of Havona point to a perfect Creator, but the turmoil of Urantia leads honest reason to conclude that this world was made and is managed by Creators who are subabsolute and other than perfect. (116:0.2, 116:7.7)

Once we recognize that our immediate Creators are divine yet finite, and that the God of time and space is an evolving Deity, inequality ceases to be a profound religious paradox. Then, says the text, faith will no longer be used to justify inequality. (116:0.1)

No longer would religious faith be prostituted to the promotion of social smugness in the fortunate while serving only to encourage stoical resignation in the unfortunate victims of social deprivation. (116:0.1)

Havona does not grow; like the eternal Gods, it simply is. But growth characterizes the grand universe, and growth means partnership between creature and Creator. The Almighty Supreme is a living, evolving Deity of power and personality whose destiny is perfection but whose present experience includes growth and incompleteness. (116:0.3, 116:0.4)

Growth is the earmark of experiential Deity (116:0.3)

The Supreme Being functions first in the central universe as a spirit personality, and second in the grand universe as God the Almighty, a personality of power. His third function, in the master universe, is still latent, and the authors admit that none of them really knows what it will disclose. (116:0.5)

I. The Supreme Mind — Progress That Never Stops

The experience of every evolving creature is a phase of the experience of the Almighty Supreme. Joining power and personality is the function of the Supreme Mind, and the completed Almighty Supreme will be one unified, personal Deity, not a loose association of attributes. During the evolutionary ages the physical power potential rests with the Seven Supreme Power Directors and the mind potential with the Seven Master Spirits. (116:1.1, 116:1.2, 116:1.3)

The author confesses to knowing less about the mind of Supremacy than about any other aspect of this Deity. But one thing is certain: the body may complete its growth and spirit may reach perfection, but mind never stops progressing. (116:1.5)

it is the experiential technique of endless progress (116:1.5)

II. The Almighty and God the Sevenfold — The Stairway by Which Man Meets God

Unqualified Paradise Deity is incomprehensible to the creatures of time and space, who grasp the meaning of divinity only through origins, relativities, and destinies. So Paradise Deity attenuates and qualifies itself, bringing the Supreme Creators into being, and its light finds its most distant and beautiful expression in the earth lives of the bestowal Sons. This is the origin of God the Sevenfold. (116:2.3, 116:2.4)

One might infer that the first three levels together would equal Paradise Deity, but that is not so. Paradise Deity is existential, while the Supreme Creators express a new power potential of experiential Deity. God the Supreme originates in the Trinity, yet he becomes manifest to creatures as a personality of power only through the first three levels of God the Sevenfold. (116:2.13, 116:2.14)

III. The Almighty and Paradise Deity — Three Kinds of Participation

The Paradise Deities act directly through their gravity circuits, but they also participate in the evolutions of time through various agencies. The text sets out side by side how each of the three Sources and Centers takes part. (116:3.1, 116:3.6)

Michaels and Avonals live the actual creature life on the evolutionary worlds, adding bona fide creature natures to their divine natures. Here is revealed how the Supreme can be both creature and creator. (116:3.3)

When divinity becomes like humanity, inherent in this relationship is the possibility that humanity can become divine. (116:3.3)

The Adjusters are to human beings what the Trinity is to the Supreme Being: absolute foundations. On those foundations free choice evolves an eternal nature—a finaliter nature in man, a Deity nature in God the Supreme. (116:3.4)

IV. The Almighty and the Supreme Creators — The Local Universe as Laboratory

The unity of the Supreme Whole depends on the progressive unification of the finite parts. In this age the almighty power of the Supreme depends on the acts of God the Sevenfold, and Master Spirit Number Seven, who speaks for the Supreme, knows and is in personal contact with him. The Ancients of Days stand midway between Paradise and the evolving worlds, seeing both ways, knowing both ways, and co-ordinating both ways. (116:4.1, 116:4.2, 116:4.6)

Early in the superuniverse plan, when the Master Spirits joined the Trinity in creating the forty-nine Reflective Spirits, the Supreme Being also functioned creatively, and Majeston appeared. The collective creations of the Master Spirits are ordinarily quasi-material, but when they produced the Seven Circuit Spirits in response to the will of the Supreme, the offspring were spiritual. The purpose of the Supreme changed the very character of the creation. (116:4.3, 116:4.5)

The real laboratories, though, are the local universes. There even the Creators evolve: the presence of the Conjoint Actor grows into a Universe Mother Spirit, and the Creator Son into the nature of experiential supreme sovereignty. Because the personalities farthest from God start there, the greatest spiritual ascent and the fullest experience of cocreating oneself are possible there. (116:4.7, 116:4.8, 116:4.11)

The local universes are the starting points of true evolution, the spawning grounds of bona fide imperfect personalities endowed with the freewill choice of becoming cocreators of themselves as they are to be. (116:4.8)

When a bestowal Son shows a new way to find God, he does not create the path; he lights up an everlasting highway that was always there. The text even says that mortal man appears to be necessary to the full function of God the Sevenfold. (116:4.10, 116:4.12)

rather are they illuminating the everlasting highways of progression which lead through the presence of the Supreme to the person of the Paradise Father (116:4.10)

God the Sevenfold includes not only the creators but also the controllers of the grand universe. These seven groups form the physical-control level of this Deity association, and the Almighty Supreme is evolving as the overcontroller of the physical power of the grand universe. (116:5.1, 116:5.9, 116:5.11)

The struggle for perfection belongs not only to the levels of intellect and spirit but also to the level of energy and mass. Because new energy and new mass keep appearing, a growing universe is always unsettled. In the final analysis all energy responds to mind, and the total evolution of the universe is the personality unification of energy-controlling mind with spirit. (116:5.12, 116:5.14, 116:5.15, 116:5.16)

In the early days of universe building even the Paradise Creators are chiefly concerned with material equilibrium, and the Creator Son does not leave his capital planet until the gross equilibrium of his local universe has been established. In universes settled in light and life there are no major unexpected physical events. Stability does not come by itself; it is won through long labor. (116:5.13, 116:5.17)

The time universes are not perfect; that is their destiny. (116:5.12)

VI. Spirit Dominance — The Wider the Gulf, the Greater the Opportunity

In the evolutionary superuniverses energy-matter dominates everywhere except in personality, where spirit, through mind, struggles for mastery. In relation to personality, physical systems are subordinate, mind systems co-ordinate, and spirit systems directive. This dominance of spirit grows through the freewill acts of Creators and creatures. (116:6.1, 116:6.2)

Here is why our world is difficult. Where divinity is more attenuated, the problems are harder, but the opportunity to gain experience in solving them is larger too. When in a person mind guides the body and spirit leads the mind, a new child of the Supreme is born; and the growth of the parts gathers into the growth of the whole. (116:6.5, 116:6.6, 116:6.7)

Man’s urge for Paradise perfection, his striving for God-attainment, creates a genuine divinity tension in the living cosmos which can only be resolved by the evolution of an immortal soul (116:7.6)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 116 invites us to read an imperfect world not as God’s failure but as a stage for growth. The confusion and inequality we meet are tasks that finite Creators and creatures must work out together, and in the process the Supreme Being himself grows. The way of faith, then, is neither resignation nor complacency in the face of inequality, but achieving our share of mastery. (116:0.1, 116:6.8)
