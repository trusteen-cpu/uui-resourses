Introduction — Life Does Not Arise by Itself

The fourth order of Sons that Paper 35 set aside is the subject of this paper. Its first sentence is plain: life is constructed according to plans formulated by unrevealed Architects of Being, and it appears on the planets either by direct importation or through the work of the Life Carriers. The Life Carriers design life, carry it to the worlds, and stay on for long ages after planting it to foster its development. (36:0.1)

LIFE does not originate spontaneously. (36:0.1)

I. Origin and Nature of Life Carriers

The Life Carriers are the only group of intelligent life in a local universe in whose creation the rulers of a superuniverse take part. Their parents are the Creator Son, the Mother Spirit, and one of the Ancients of Days of the superuniverse. It is fitting that those who alone can decree the extinction of intelligent life should share in creating the beings who establish life. (36:1.1)

The three divisions were sorted out by tests the Melchizedeks conducted, and ever since, the Melchizedeks have been closely associated with the Life Carriers, always going with them when they establish life on a new planet. The Life Carriers are not a truly self-governing group, but they govern themselves in the administration of their own divisions. (36:1.2, 36:1.3)

II. The Life Carrier Worlds — Seven Spheres for the Study of Life

The fourth group of seven worlds in the Salvington circuit belongs to the Life Carriers and is under the general oversight of the Melchizedeks. Each primary sphere is surrounded by six satellites, and on the first world are the central emplacements of the seven adjutant mind-spirits. (36:2.1, 36:2.9, 36:2.10)

The original life designs are provided by the Creator Son, but their outworking is entrusted to the Life Carriers, and any plan that departs from accepted formulas must be endorsed by the Creator Son. Because the Life Carriers constantly work to improve the vital formulas, life is never exactly the same on any two planets, even within one family of worlds. (36:2.12, 36:2.13)

One fact about Urantia stands out. Urantia is a decimal planet, a life-experiment world: on one world in ten a greater variance from the standard life designs is permitted, and the life now evolving on Urantia was planned and partly worked out in the laboratories of the second world. The domain of life is marked by three, seven, and twelve and their multiples, and the human sex cells on Urantia carry forty-eight trait determiners. (36:2.11, 36:2.15)

The studies of the third world lead to the early establishment of a life-conservation commission on each new world. Urantia had twenty-four custodian commissioners, two for each parent pattern of life material. The paper adds that on such planets the highest form of life is reproduced by a bundle of twenty-four pattern units, and on that physical foundation arise the twenty-four basic orders of psychic organization. (36:2.16)

The research of the fourth world explains the apparent waste of evolution. Because the original life plasm must hold the potential for every future variation, many seemingly useless forms of plant and animal life may appear and vanish. Yet through the whole long process runs the thread of the designers’ wisdom, and those by-products are all essential to the full function of the higher intelligent life. (36:2.17)

III. Life Transplantation — Hands Off When Will Appears

A corps commissioned to plant life usually consists of one hundred senior carriers, one hundred assistants, and one thousand custodians. Sometimes they bring life plasm with them; sometimes, as on Urantia, they organize the life patterns after arriving, according to approved formulas. Then they catalyze the lifeless material, imparting the vital spirit spark, and the inert patterns become living matter. (36:3.2, 36:3.3)

The vital spark—the mystery of life—is bestowed through the Life Carriers, not by them. (36:3.4)

From then on, evolution proceeds according to the nature and tendencies already planted in the patterns. Two senior carriers who volunteered to remain, with their twelve associates, are now serving on Urantia. (36:3.8, 36:3.9)

The Life Carriers are not permitted to experiment or to interfere with will (36:3.8)

IV. Melchizedek Life Carriers — The Mystery of the Midsoniters

Each local system has a single sphere, the midsonite world, on which a Melchizedek has served as life carrier. There a materially modified Melchizedek Son mates with a Mother Eve chosen from the Material Daughters, and their offspring are the midsoniters. These beings reproduce until they are one thousand standard years old; then they are translated by seraphic transport and never reproduce again. (36:4.1, 36:4.2, 36:4.3)

no midsoniter has experienced death. (36:4.4)

Without indwelling Adjusters they are hardly immortal, yet they do not seem mortal either. Every midsoniter ever born in Nebadon is still alive, on its native world, on some intervening sphere, or on the Salvington midsonite sphere, the seventh of the finaliters’ worlds, where they seem to be gathering for some future eventuality. Their purpose is unknown, and the finaliters always decline to discuss their destiny. Passages like this, where the author honestly admits what he does not know, show that this revelation does not pretend to know everything. (36:4.4, 36:4.6, 36:4.8)

V. The Seven Adjutant Mind-Spirits — Why Evolution Is Not Accidental

What conditions organic evolution on the primitive worlds is the presence of the seven adjutant mind-spirits, and this is why evolution is purposeful rather than accidental. The adjutants are the children of the Mother Spirit and her personal ministry to material minds. Yet they are not separate personalities; they are more like circuits, in fact a level of consciousness of the Mother Spirit herself. (36:5.1, 36:5.4)

Their central emplacements on the Life Carrier headquarters world indicate the mind function of the adjutants on any world and in any organism. The first five are measured there perfectly, but for worship and wisdom only a qualitative function is recorded; their quantitative activity is registered as the personal experience of the Mother Spirit on Salvington. In other words, the two highest functions of mind are reckoned not in numbers but in the Mother Spirit’s own experience. (36:5.3)

The levels of mind fall into three. Before the capacity to learn from experience appears, mind is the domain of the Master Physical Controllers; before it can recognize and worship Deity, it is the exclusive domain of the adjutants. When spiritual response appears, the mind becomes supermind at once and enters the spirit circuits of the Mother Spirit. The paper also notes that animals are in some measure indispensable to man’s intellectual as well as physical evolution. (36:5.13, 36:5.15)

Mind is a divinity bestowal, but it is not immortal when it functions without spirit insight, and when it is devoid of the ability to worship and crave survival. (36:5.17)

VI. Living Forces — Life Only from Life

Life is both mechanistic and vitalistic. Physicists and chemists will go on understanding protoplasm better, but they will never produce living organisms. Even the experimental life the Life Carriers organize on Salvington lacks mind and the power of reproduction. Mind is the Mother Spirit’s gift through the adjutants, and reproductive ability is likewise her personal impartation. (36:6.1, 36:6.3)

Things material may enjoy an independent existence, but life springs only from life. (36:6.2)

We often speak of life as energy or force, but according to this paper it is really neither. Force-energy responds to gravity; life does not. Life is the animation of a pattern-configured system of energy, whether material, mindal, or spiritual. (36:6.6)

At death the life of plants and animals survives only as part of the cosmic forces, without identity; it does not survive individually. The survival of mortal creatures depends wholly on the evolution of an immortal soul within the mortal mind. The author admits he does not fully understand the nature and source of the life-activating spark, and sums up briefly what is known. (36:6.5, 36:6.7)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 36 shows that life is not the product of chance but the fruit of design, care, and a divine spark. Yet even the designers of life step back the moment will appears. The seven adjutants within us grow the mind from instinct to wisdom, but for that mind to reach immortality it must choose to worship and to crave survival.
