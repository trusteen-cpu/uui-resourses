Introduction — Adjusters Are Children of the Universe Career

Paper 109 presents the Adjusters not as beings standing still in finished perfection but as beings who grow. Virgin Adjusters gain experience while their mortal creatures grow and develop. Just as a child learns adult skills through play, the indwelling Adjuster gains skill for the next stage by planning and rehearsing the activities of the morontia career in advance. (109:0.1)

Human existence constitutes a period of practice which is effectively utilized by the Adjuster in preparing for the increased responsibilities and the greater opportunities of a future life. (109:0.1)

From this point of view an ordinary day takes on cosmic meaning. What the Adjuster labors at within us has less to do with the passing affairs of planetary existence than with the realities of the universe career. Each of our choices becomes, for the Adjuster, material for rehearsing eternity. (109:0.1)

I. Development of Adjusters — Experience Has No Substitute

There must be an elaborate plan on Divinington for training virgin Adjusters, and a system for retraining experienced ones. According to Personalized Adjusters, whenever an Adjuster returns because its subject failed to survive, it always undergoes an extended course of training before being sent out again. (109:1.1, 109:1.2)

Experience is inseparable from a living existence (109:1.3)

Even divine perfection cannot take the place of experience. So Adjusters too must evolve from the lower, inexperienced groups to the higher, more experienced ones. They gain skill and ability from every contact, whether or not their subjects survive, and they are equal partners with the human mind in fostering the evolution of a soul of survival capacity. (109:1.3, 109:1.4)

II. Self-Acting Adjusters — A Functional Classification

Besides the classification by experience—virgin, advanced, and supreme—there is a functional classification. A self-acting Adjuster shows a marked degree of will in matters not involving the personality it indwells and takes part in many activities of the realm. The following six cases qualify. (109:2.1, 109:2.8)

Source 109:2.1
How an Adjuster becomes self-actingIn brief
1. Requisite experienceTemporary indwelling on a loan world, or on a fusion world where the human failed to survive — an advanced or supreme Adjuster
2. The balance of spiritual powerIts subject has made the third psychic circle and been assigned a personal seraphic guardian
3. A solemn betrothalIts subject has made the supreme decision — the Adjuster reckons fusion as an event of fact
4. The reserve corps of destinyIts subject has been mustered into one of the reserve corps
5. Service during sleepIt has been temporarily detached from the mind to serve the spiritual administration of the world
6. Service in a crisisIt has served in a crisis within a human who was the material complement of a spirit personality

Such Adjusters can communicate with Adjusters in other realms, but only on the level of their mutual work and to preserve the custodial data needed for their ministry; in times of crisis they have even functioned in interplanetary matters. Yet most often they work quietly, as undetected indwellers of the earthly tabernacles they have chosen. (109:2.8, 109:2.9)

Supreme and self-acting Adjusters can leave the body at will, because Adjusters are not a biologic part of mortal life but a divine superimposition on it. Even so, once they take up their indwelling they very rarely leave, even temporarily. Adjusters that have conquered their entrusted tasks and only await dissolution or translation are called superacting Adjusters. (109:2.10, 109:2.11)

The indwellers are not an organic or biologic part of mortal life; they are divine superimpositions thereon. (109:2.10)

III. Relation of Adjusters to Mortal Types — Three Series of Worlds

The labors of the Mystery Monitors are remarkably uniform, yet they differ according to whether the assignment is one of liaison or of fusion. The text explains this by dividing the worlds into three series. (109:3.1)

Source 109:3.2
Type of worldHow the Adjuster indwellsHow mortals survive
Series one (certain primitive worlds)Mainly as experiential training for the Adjuster itselfMany who fail of Adjuster fusion survive by Spirit fusion
Series twoLoaned for a single life span — giving far more help than on UrantiaEternal life through Spirit fusion
Series three (worlds like Urantia)A real betrothal, a life-and-death engagementIf they survive, everlasting fusion with the Adjuster
Nothing of survival value is ever lost in all the wide universe. (109:3.2)

The type of brain also makes a difference. In three-brained mortals the Adjuster gains far more actual contact during the temporal life than in one- and two-brained types, but after death all proceed in the same way. On two-brained worlds, after a bestowal Son has come, virgin Adjusters are seldom assigned to persons of unquestioned survival capacity. (109:3.5, 109:3.6)

Among the early races of Urantia there were those wholly lacking Adjuster capacity, those who promptly received Adjusters at the age of moral responsibility, and a borderline group who could receive them only on personal petition. Even virgin Adjusters that served beings virtually disqualified for survival gained valuable preliminary experience and became better qualified for higher minds on other worlds. (109:3.7, 109:3.8)

IV. Adjusters and Human Personality — A Real Potential of Civilization

Animals share feelings, but they do not communicate ideas and ideals. High intellectual intercourse and spiritual communion between people are made possible with the Adjusters’ help, and evolutionary beings who develop speech are on the highroad to receiving Adjusters. Adjusters are not personality, but they come from the source of personality, and their presence enhances the quality of human personality. (109:4.1, 109:4.2)

Many of the great intellectual and spiritual leaders of Urantia exerted their influence chiefly because of the superiority and previous experience of their indwelling Adjusters. If the Adjusters were withdrawn, this world would slowly return to many of the practices of primitive times. (109:4.3, 109:4.4)

the divine Monitors are one of the real potentials of advancing civilization. (109:4.4)

The author testifies that an Adjuster which had previously indwelt fifteen minds in Orvonton is now in a person on Urantia. What others lost by refusing survival, this person and the whole world now gain. Adjusters, however, are seldom given two indwellings on the same planet, and none now serving on Urantia has been here before. (109:4.5, 109:4.6)

V. Material Handicaps to Adjuster Indwelling — The Art of Serving Two Masters

When the mind flows freely in the controlled channels of creative imagination, and sometimes during sleep, the Adjuster can arrest the mental currents and divert the idea procession, effecting deep spiritual transformations in the superconsciousness. But unsteady mental attitudes often thwart its plans. (109:5.1, 109:5.3)

It is sometimes possible to have the mind illuminated, to hear the divine voice that continually speaks within you, so that you may become partially conscious of the wisdom, truth, goodness, and beauty of the potential personality constantly indwelling you. (109:5.2)

The great problem of life is adjusting the tendencies we inherit from our ancestors to the spiritual urges initiated by the Adjuster. Many grow weary and falter under this strain, but heredity only slows the rate of personality conquest; it does not prevent the final completion of the ascendant adventure. This is a clear comfort to anyone discouraged by an inborn temperament. (109:5.4, 109:5.5)

VI. The Persistence of True Values — No Experience Is Wasted

Adjusters never fail, and nothing worth surviving is ever lost. Even when a mortal rejects survival, the worth-while elements of that life are carried by the Adjuster to a higher mind on another world. An Adjuster that is personalized after repeated failures to attain fusion becomes a composite assembly of the survival traits of all its former hosts. (109:6.1, 109:6.2)

No worth-while experience ever happens in vain; no true meaning or real value ever perishes. (109:6.1)

At the center of this section stands the Personalized Adjuster of Michael of Nebadon. This Monitor guided Michael step by step when he lived as Joshua ben Joseph, and before that had served with Machiventa Melchizedek in the days of Abraham. Today he directs the activities of Adjusters in Nebadon. (109:6.4)

Such decisive consecration constitutes the true passport from the limitations of human nature to the finality of divine attainment. (109:6.5)

This Adjuster still reflects the prebaptismal humanity of Joshua ben Joseph—a living transcript of the values created in the humble circumstances of a commonplace life. The permanent values entrusted to an Adjuster are either bestowed on a mind of future indwelling or held in trust for future service with the Architects of the Master Universe. (109:6.6, 109:6.7)

VII. Destiny of Personalized Adjusters — Omnipersonal Beings

Father fragments of the Adjuster type seem to attain personality only through service-ministry to a personal being. Personalized Adjusters make their home on Divinington, where they instruct their prepersonal associates, and they serve as the powerful executives of the Architects of the Master Universe. They alone are before personality, are personality, and are after personality. (109:7.1, 109:7.3, 109:7.4)

When the emergency regency began on Urantia, three Personalized Adjusters appeared, uncalled, the instant the Most High regent assumed authority. He assigned them no duties, yet they were among the most active beings then serving on this world. No one dares to predict what their future missions may be. (109:7.7, 109:7.8)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 109 comes down to two points. Because experience has no substitute, every day we live is material for growth, both for the Adjuster and for us. And true values are never lost. As Jesus’ Adjuster shows, consecration to the Father’s will opens the way to all such growth. (109:1.3, 109:6.5)
