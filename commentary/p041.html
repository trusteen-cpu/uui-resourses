Introduction — As Far as the Mother Spirit Reaches

The papers before this one introduced the personalities of the local universe; Paper 41 turns to the physical stage on which those personalities live and work. Strikingly, it defines the boundary of a local universe not by astronomy but by a spiritual presence. What sets Nebadon apart from every other local universe is the space presence of the Divine Minister of Salvington, the Creative Spirit. (41:0.1)

That which is pervaded by our local universe Mother Spirit is Nebadon; that which extends beyond her space presence is outside Nebadon (41:0.1)

The superuniverses are clearly separated in space, but no such sharp lines of physical demarcation set off the local universes. The reason is that local universes are organized on administrative principles, while their suns and planets take origin chiefly from nebulae. Nebadon itself was assembled from the progeny of Andronover and other nebulae; the power directors adjusted their motions until they traveled together as one unit, and today this local star cloud swings in an increasingly settled orbit about the Sagittarius center. (41:0.2, 41:0.3, 41:0.4)

I. The Nebadon Power Centers — A Living System of Energy

Nebulae are initiated by Paradise force organizers, but once they respond to gravity, the power centers and physical controllers take over and direct the physical evolution of the stars and planets that follow. This supervision was co-ordinated with the Creator Son’s plan as soon as he arrived, and a vast complex of communication lines, energy circuits, and power lanes came to bind the bodies of Nebadon into one integrated administrative unit. (41:1.1)

II. The Satania Physical Controllers — Intelligence That Handles Energy

Satania is one of the one hundred systems of the constellation of Norlatiadek and is made up of over seven thousand astronomical groups. Its energies are supervised by a Master Physical Controller headquartered on Jerusem and by five hundred thousand living and intelligent energy manipulators scattered throughout the system. Together they can mobilize, transform, and transmit nearly all of the physical energies of organized space. (41:2.2, 41:2.3, 41:2.4)

The paper explains this work by comparing it with the life we know. Plants turn the energy of light into vegetable growth, and animals turn that energy into activity; life itself has the power to change energy from one form into another. The physical controllers are related to the preliving manifestations of material energy much as the adjutant mind-spirits are concerned with the prespiritual functions of material mind. (41:2.5, 41:2.6)

Life has inherent capacity for the mobilization and transmutation of universal energy. (41:2.5)

Urantia is a small planet lying in the lines of tremendous energies, in the circuit of enormous masses. Not every energy disturbance here is the controllers’ responsibility, and they themselves have trouble insulating against the powerful Norlatiadek currents. The order of the universe, it turns out, is maintained less like a perfect machine than by the unceasing labor of living beings. (41:2.8)

III–IV. Our Starry Associates; Sun Density

Upward of two thousand suns shine in Satania, and our own sun is an average blazing orb. The material makeup of all suns, planets, and even meteors is quite identical. Antares is four hundred and fifty times the diameter of our sun, yet space has abundant room for such giants. Suns that are too large soon break up or form double stars, and the light that reached Urantia in A.D. 1572 came from the most recent major double star explosion in Orvonton. (41:3.1, 41:3.2, 41:3.4, 41:3.5)

What the paper says about solar density overturns common sense. Our sun is gaseous, yet it has about one and one-half times the density of water. Gaseous, liquid, and solid states are matters of atomic-molecular relationship, whereas density is a relationship of space and mass. So suns that are equally gaseous can differ enormously in density. V–VI. Solar Radiation; Calcium — The Wanderer of Space

The steady stream of escaping light proves that suns are not very dense. Fifteen feet of surface with the density of Urantia would trap all the X rays and light within. The interior of a sun is a vast X-ray generator, and an electron starting at its very center needs more than half a million years to work its way to the surface. The author underlines how real sunlight is by pricing it in the energy values of our own world. (41:5.1, 41:5.2, 41:5.3, 41:5.4)

Light is real. (41:5.2)

Energy in its flight through space moves straight forward. Light seems to travel in waves only because other forces act on it at the same time, just as rain falling straight down in a heavy wind looks like sheets and waves of water. To understand the behavior of light fully, the paper adds, we would need to grasp the activities of the Conjoint Actor and the Unqualified Absolute as well. (41:5.6, 41:5.7, 41:5.8)

Space is not empty. Atoms shattered in the solar elemental battles drift through it, especially sodium and calcium. Calcium is the chief element permeating the space of Orvonton, and it keeps its identity even after ionization and the battering of X rays. By juggling its nineteenth electron between two orbits more than twenty-five thousand times a second, a calcium atom grasps and lets go of the sunbeam and so rides it out of the sun. (41:6.1, 41:6.2, 41:6.4)

Stone is literally the basic building matter for the planets and spheres of space. (41:6.2)
Calcium is the most expert solar-prison escaper. (41:6.4)

The section also warns that spectral analysis shows only the composition of a sun’s surface. Solar spectra show many iron lines not because iron is the sun’s chief element, but because the present surface temperature favors the registry of the iron spectrum. It is a lesson in scientific humility: do not judge the whole by what happens to be visible. (41:6.7)

VII. Sources of Solar Energy — Seven Supplies

The internal temperature of the suns is much higher than is commonly believed. Our sun’s surface is almost 6,000 degrees, but its central regions reach about 35,000,000 degrees Fahrenheit, where practically no whole atoms exist. The paper accounts for this enormous energy expenditure by naming seven sources in the order of their importance. (41:7.1, 41:7.2, 41:7.3)

X ray is the great leveler of atomic existence. (41:7.1)

Whether a sun shines forever depends on where it stands. A sun in the main streams of universe energy can replenish its losses and blaze on indefinitely, while stars far from those channels gradually cool off and burn out. Yet even dying suns can be revived by collision or recharging, and those that finally explode become a rare form of energy that feeds other, better-placed suns. (41:7.14, 41:7.15)

Only those suns which function in the direct channels of the main streams of universe energy can shine on forever. (41:7.14)

VIII–IX. Solar-Energy Reactions; Sun Stability — The Balance of Gravity and Heat

Suns connected to the energy channels release energy through various nuclear-reaction chains, the most common being the hydrogen-carbon-helium reaction, in which carbon acts as a catalyst. When hydrogen is exhausted, luminosity peaks; afterward gravity contraction sustains the light until the star becomes a white dwarf. Large suns may collapse suddenly through the escape of “runaway particles,” and the Crab nebula is an irregular nebula that arose in this way about nine hundred years ago. (41:8.1, 41:8.2, 41:8.3, 41:8.4)

Sun stability is wholly dependent on the equilibrium between gravity-heat contention—tremendous pressures counterbalanced by unimagined temperatures. (41:9.4)

Ultimatons are not subject to local gravity, yet they are fully obedient to absolute or Paradise gravity and swing true to the circuit of the great ellipse of creation. That even the smallest unit of energy is tied to the center of all things echoes the picture of the gravity circuits drawn in Paper 12. (41:9.2)

X. Origin of Inhabited Worlds — The Least Becomes Greatest

Our solar system was born when the sun, then in a state of mighty pulsation, erupted matter as the massive Angona system swung near. Most solar systems had a quite different origin, but whatever the technique, gravity always produces the same pattern: a central sun with planets, satellites, and meteors. A world’s features are shaped by its mode of origin, its situation, its environment, its age and size; and even a planet of dual origin like Urantia passed through an early phase of volcanoes, earthquakes, and floods. (41:10.1, 41:10.2, 41:10.3, 41:10.4)

The paper ends by giving our cosmic address. Urantia lies on the outskirts of Satania, Satania is next to the outermost system of Norlatiadek, and Norlatiadek is now traversing the outer fringe of Nebadon. Until Michael’s bestowal we were truly among the least of all creation. (41:10.5)

Sometimes the last is first, while truly the least becomes greatest. (41:10.5)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 41 reads like a lecture in astronomy, yet beneath it lies a consistent vision of faith. The bounds of the universe are set by the presence of the Mother Spirit; the flow of energy is tended by living intelligences; the stability of a sun comes from a balance between gravity and heat. And a small planet on the edge of things became, through Michael’s bestowal, a place of great universe interest. To know the material world is bound up with knowing the order of the One who made it. (41:0.1, 41:9.4, 41:10.5)
