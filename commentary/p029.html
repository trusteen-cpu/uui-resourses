Introduction — The Least Understood Universe Personalities

The papers so far have dealt with angels and spirit personalities. Paper 29 enters a quite different realm: the beings who watch over stars, planets, and energy circuits, the controllers of the physical domain. The author begins by noting how little these beings are known to mankind. (29:0.1)

OF ALL the universe personalities concerned in the regulation of interplanetary and interuniverse affairs, the power directors and their associates have been the least understood on Urantia. (29:0.1)

Three groups of living beings handle force and energy in the master universe: the Primary Eventuated Master Force Organizers, the Associate Transcendental Master Force Organizers, and the Universe Power Directors. The author is permitted to disclose fully only the last of these, which in turn has four divisions. (29:0.1, 29:0.5)

I. The Seven Supreme Power Directors — Semimaterial Beings Born of Spirit

The Seven Supreme Power Directors are the regulators of physical energy in the grand universe. Their creation by the Seven Master Spirits is the first record of semimaterial offspring coming from truly spirit ancestry. When the Master Spirits create individually they produce spirit personalities of the angelic orders; when they create collectively, they sometimes produce such semimaterial beings. (29:1.1)

The seven directors are identical in appearance and function, and no one but the Master Spirit with whom each is associated can tell them apart. The same director is always paired with the same Spirit, and their collaboration is a unique association of physical and spiritual energy. Spirit and matter, we see here, are not distant realms but were designed from the start to work together. (29:1.2)

The physical creation is fundamentally uniform in administration. (29:1.4)

II. The Supreme Power Centers — The Intelligence of the Grand Universe Power System

The Supreme Power Directors cannot reproduce themselves alone, but collectively, in union with the Master Spirits, they create the power centers. These are beings of high will and exquisite intelligence, with personality of the Third Source, and they are the secret of the mind control of the vast network of physical controllers. They function in seven groups. (29:2.1, 29:2.9)

In Havona there is a perfection of energy control found nowhere else, and the section presents it as the goal of every power center and physical controller. Even the imperfect energy systems of time and space are moving forward with the perfect central universe as their pattern. (29:2.12)

Perfection of energy regulation is the ultimate goal of all the power centers and the physical controllers of space. (29:2.12)

The power charge of a superuniverse consists of three energy streams of ten segregations each, like a vast ocean of energy that bathes all seven supercreations. Within that ocean the specialized currents sent out by the power centers are sharply defined local movements of energy, like the Gulf Stream in the midst of the Atlantic. (29:2.14, 29:2.15)

The local universe centers can lay down energy lanes, reinforced channels for communication between important inhabited points. These are individualized currents running from one power center to another, unlike undifferentiated energy moving through free space. Local astronomic catastrophes in space are only of passing interest to them; they concentrate on the orderly delivery of available energy to the subordinate constellations and systems. Their steadiness, keeping their currents flowing amid great upheaval, is striking. (29:2.16)

III. The Domain of Power Centers — Tireless Workers

The power centers and their subordinates number more than ten billion, all perfectly synchronized with the Seven Supreme Power Directors on Paradise. They are exempt from the restraint of any tribunal and are not subject to the direction of the superuniverse governments or the local universe administrations. Yet in some way they are closely related to the cosmic overcontrol of the Supreme Being. (29:3.1, 29:3.2, 29:3.3)

The universal scheme makes no provision for interrupting the physical lines of energy, so these beings can never for a moment relinquish their supervision. Knowing that such unceasing faithfulness lies behind the stability of the physical world we take for granted gives us reason for gratitude. (29:3.5)

The forms of energy beyond their control represent the unpredictable realm of the Unqualified Absolute. The centers and controllers exercise almost supreme authority in local situations, yet they are always conscious of the presence of the Unqualified Absolute. The author frankly says that explaining how they work is beyond his ability. (29:3.11, 29:3.12, 29:3.8)

They are both living and “personal,” but they are beyond your comprehension. (29:3.8)

IV. The Master Physical Controllers — Intelligence Without Choice

The Master Physical Controllers are the mobile subordinates of the Supreme Power Centers and serve throughout the grand universe. Up to the superuniverse headquarters they are governed by the Seven Supreme Power Directors; from there on they are directed by the equilibrium commissions. They deal chiefly with a fundamental energy not yet discovered on Urantia, one that is essential to interplanetary transport and communication. (29:4.1, 29:4.2, 29:4.14)

The philosophical heart of this section is the distinction between mind and personality. The last four orders in particular respond automatically to the impulses of their superiors, yet this is not mere mechanism; all disclose the differential function of intelligence. Still, they have no will or independence of decision. By contrast, this shows how remarkable it is for a being to receive a personality able to choose. (29:4.12, 29:4.13)

Personality is not necessarily a concomitant of mind. (29:4.13)

The energy transformers insulate planets from powerful energy currents and maintain power equilibrium, but they do not function directly in the realms of life or change the forms of living beings. And three of the thirty energies are beyond their control. The physical order of the universe and the order of life interlock, yet they are not entrusted to the same hands. (29:4.24, 29:4.25)

These controllers are close to the life of our own world. The ten mechanical controllers now stationed on Urantia act as one to assist the departure of the seraphic transports, and the energy transmitters provide emergency lines of communication outside the regular circuits. Transmitters and transformers are indispensable to mortal existence on worlds of meager atmosphere, and on nonbreathing planets they are an essential part of the life technique. Many unseen hands uphold the conditions of life. (29:4.19, 29:4.30, 29:4.31)

They are truly the alchemists of space and the wonder-workers of time. (29:4.35)

Yet in all their wonders the dissociators never transgress the mandates of Cosmic Supremacy. Of the frandalanks the author says that, though intelligent, they cannot be classified as anything but living machines, and he invites us to picture them by comparison with mankind’s own precision instruments. (29:4.35, 29:4.38)

V. The Master Force Organizers — Pioneers of Unorganized Space

The Master Force Organizers are resident on Paradise but work chiefly in the domains of unorganized space. They are neither creators nor creatures, and they work only under the supervision of the Architects of the Master Universe. The power directors take over the work they begin, in the following sequence. (29:5.1, 29:5.4)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 29 tells us that the physical world beneath our feet is not a machine running by itself but is held in the unceasing care of intelligent beings. A lineage that begins in spirit reaches through semimaterial beings to every single energy circuit. At the same time, by distinguishing intelligence from personality, the paper reminds us what a precious gift the ability to choose and take responsibility really is. (29:1.1, 29:4.13)
