Introduction — The Middle Ranks of the Spirit’s Family

Paper 24 dealt with the higher personalities of the Infinite Spirit; Paper 25 introduces the intermediate ranks of that same family, the Messenger Hosts of Space. These versatile beings serve as the connecting links between the higher personalities and the ministering spirits. Of the seven groups, three are created as such, while the other four represent levels of attainment reached by the angelic orders. (25:0.1, 25:0.9)

These beings do many kinds of work according to their nature and status, but they always serve under the direction of those who rule the realms of their assignment. One question runs through the whole paper: in a universe that is not yet perfect, how are very different beings helped to live together? (25:0.9)

I. The Havona Servitals — A World Without Menial Work

Though called servitals, these “midway creatures” of the central universe are not servants in any lowly sense. They are the joint creation of the Master Spirits and the Supreme Power Directors, and this collaboration is the nearest pattern for the long line of dual-order reproduction that reaches from a Creator Son–Creative Spirit liaison all the way down to sex procreation on worlds like Urantia. They appear a thousand at a time, in a ratio of three spirit beings to one semiphysical. (25:1.1, 25:1.2, 25:1.4)

In the spiritual world there is no such thing as menial work; all service is sacred and exhilarating (25:1.1)

The servitals and the Graduate Guides are bound by deep affection, and there is divine pathos when they part. Yet the servitals go with joy, not sorrow, for the satisfying joy of high duty outshines every other feeling. Here the author adds that when an ascending soul stands before the Supreme Judge, the verdict will rest not on material success or quantitative achievement but on faithfulness in the essentials. (25:1.6)

Sorrow cannot exist in the face of the consciousness of divine duty faithfully performed. (25:1.6)

In Orvonton almost 138 billion servitals minister on the 490 educational satellites that surround the superuniverse capital. They have come down from our next career to study us and to assure us that our graduation from time to eternity is real and certain. This experience serves them well later in Havona, where they work as associates of the Graduate Guides or, once translated, as Graduate Guides themselves. (25:1.7)

II–III. The Universal Conciliators — From Judges to Teachers

Each time a Havona Servital is created, seven conciliators appear, one in each superuniverse, because the Reflective Spirits at the superuniverse headquarters respond reflectively to the creative event on Paradise. Each superuniverse mirrors the character of the Master Spirit who presides over its destiny, so the conciliators as a whole display forty-nine partial but complementary viewpoints. (25:2.1, 25:2.2, 25:2.4)

Source 25:2.5
Conciliating commissionRole
1. The Judge-ArbiterJudicial head, designated unanimously by the other three

3. The Divine ExecutionerA quasi-material being who contacts material beings and carries out the decisions
4. The RecorderClerk of the tribunal; prepares records for the superuniverse and local universe (and, when needed, the system)

Because the advocate is detached while a case is being judged, a commission in session functions as three, and so these groups are sometimes called referee trios. Traveling at the seraphic rate of triple velocity, they are the traveling courts of the worlds, devoted to the quick settlement of minor difficulties. They never pass on matters of eternal import, so the soul’s eternal prospects are never placed in jeopardy by their acts; yet once they accept a case, their rulings are final and always unanimous. (25:2.10, 25:2.11, 25:2.12)

Why are such commissions needed? The moment the Creators bring into being creatures with the power of choice, misunderstanding becomes unavoidable. The Creators could have made the local universes as perfect as Havona. They chose instead to make universes rich in differences and difficulties, and with them the means of harmonizing those differences. (25:3.7)

misunderstandings are certain to arise, and provision for the fair adjustment of these honest differences of viewpoint must be made. (25:3.7)

The four members remain together forever just as they were first associated, embodying the supreme justice of time and space. (25:3.12, 25:3.17)

The higher a creature’s education, the more respect he has for the knowledge, experience, and opinions of others. (25:3.12)
They are eternally associated as the embodiment of the supreme justice of time and space. (25:3.17)

IV. Technical Advisers — A Living Library of Applied Law

Technical Advisers are not created as such. The Infinite Spirit chose as their nucleus one million of the most orderly minds among the early supernaphim and omniaphim, and since then actual experience has been required. Their ranks are drawn from supernaphim, seconaphim, tertiaphim, omniaphim, and seraphim, and some ascending mortals and midwayers serve with them for a time. They are organized in groups of seven, at least five of whom must be of permanent status. (25:4.1, 25:4.2, 25:4.11)

In speaking of the mortals who serve with them transiently, the paper states a principle of the entire ascending career. All the way in to Havona we are pupil-teachers, rising by passing on our newly gained knowledge to those just below us. (25:4.12)

In the universal regime you are not reckoned as having possessed yourself of knowledge and truth until you have demonstrated your ability and your willingness to impart this knowledge and truth to others. (25:4.12)

Their training begins in the Melchizedek colleges, continues in the courts of the Ancients of Days and the “schools of the seven circles” in Havona, and is completed in the Paradise “college of the ethics of law and the technique of Supremacy.” A living library of applied law cannot be created; it must be evolved by experience. These advisers prevent delay, facilitate progress, and guide us toward the better way that exists in everything. (25:4.14, 25:4.16, 25:4.17)

There is always a best and right way to do things; there is always the technique of perfection, a divine method (25:4.17)

V–VI. The Custodians of Records and the Celestial Recorders — A Universe That Does Not Forget

Everything of importance is recorded, and records move upward from local to higher levels according to the scope of their significance. The recording angels of the inhabited planets are the source of all individual records, and on Paradise the digests of superuniverse and Havona data are kept by the Custodians of Records, who are senior tertiary supernaphim. The Celestial Recorders are ascendant seraphim who can handle spiritual and material energy at once, producing an original spirit record together with a semimaterial copy. (25:5.2, 25:5.3, 25:6.1)

Source 25:5.2
WhereKind of record
A local universeMore written records, fewer living ones
UversaBoth equally available
ParadiseMore living records, fewer formal ones — the “living library” of the Custodians of Knowledge

Thanks to this dual recording, every class of being, from the material order to the high spirits of light, can consult the records. As we ascend we will always be able to learn the history and traditions of our status sphere. No Celestial Recorder has ever been known to defect or falsify a record, and their work is doubly inspected, including by the Mighty Messengers. (25:6.2, 25:6.3)

VII–VIII. The Morontia Companions and the Paradise Companions — The Ministry of Presence

The Morontia Companions are children of the local universe Mother Spirits. They are gracious hosts to those just beginning the long ascent, and skillful sponsors of play. The paper promises that there will always be time for rest and spirit play along the way. Because they serve only in the local universes, we will truly regret parting from them when we leave the morontia life behind. (25:7.1, 25:7.2, 25:7.3)

Throughout the journey to Paradise there will always be time for rest and spirit play (25:7.2)

The Paradise Companions are recruited from several angelic orders and serve for a season; aside from permanent status on Paradise, this is the highest honor ever conferred on ministering spirits. They have nothing in particular to accomplish. When nearly every other being we meet has something definite to do with us or for us, these companions are assigned simply to be with us as personality associates. (25:8.1, 25:8.2, 25:8.3)

they are simply companions. (25:8.3)
The Creators well know that it is “not good for man to be alone,” and provision is accordingly made for companionship, even on Paradise. (25:8.4)

This care, which makes sure that someone always accompanies a defeated pilgrim, shows that failure in Havona is not an end but a temporary detour. The companion remains with him until he returns to the central universe to resume the Paradise ascent. (25:8.9)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 25 can look like a list of administrative departments, but it holds wisdom for life in an imperfect world. Misunderstandings will come, and so fair conciliation is needed. What we learn becomes truly ours only when we share it. And the universe refuses to leave anyone alone. (25:3.7, 25:4.12, 25:8.4)

Paper 26 returns to the central universe and introduces the Ministering Spirits of the Central Universe, the supernaphim who teach and guide ascenders on the circuits of Havona. Keeping in mind the places of the Havona Servitals and the Paradise Companions met here will make that Havona pilgrimage all the more vivid.
