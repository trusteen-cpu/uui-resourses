Introduction — A Chain of Spirit from Top to Bottom

If Paper 16 introduced the Seven Master Spirits, Paper 17 widens the view to the larger family they head. The seven Supreme Spirit groups described here are the co-ordinating directors of the seven-segmented administration of the grand universe. All belong to the functional family of the Infinite Spirit, but by origin they fall into two branches. The first three groups are usually classed as children of the Paradise Trinity; the remaining four are brought into being by the creative acts of the Infinite Spirit and his associates. (17:0.1, 17:0.5)

The reach of these seven orders is astonishing. It begins with the Master Spirits on the periphery of the eternal Isle and runs through the Paradise satellites of the Spirit, the Havona circuits, the superuniverse governments, and the local universes, all the way down to the lowly service of the adjutants bestowed upon evolutionary minds. This paper therefore tells us that even the smallest stirrings deep within our own minds touch the outermost edge of a vast spiritual order that stretches back to Paradise. (17:0.10)

The Seven Master Spirits are the co-ordinating directors of this far-flung administrative realm. (17:0.11)

The Master Spirits do some things personally, but much of their work they carry out through associates. In executive matters they act in the persons of the Supreme Executives; in the central universe they work through the Spirits of the Circuits; in the superuniverses they reveal themselves through the Reflective Spirits and the Image Aids. Because they do not directly contact universe administration below the courts of the Ancients of Days, the function of the Master Spirit of Orvonton in our local universe is discharged by the Creative Mother Spirit resident on Salvington. (17:0.11, 17:0.12)

I. The Seven Supreme Executives — The Board of Managing Directors

The Supreme Executives are a group of seven beings trinitized by the Father, Son, and Spirit. They direct the executive headquarters of the Master Spirits on the seven Paradise satellites of the Infinite Spirit. Executive Number One looks after superuniverse number one alone, and so on to Executive Number Seven, who devotes himself to the seventh superuniverse. The name of that seventh sphere is Orvonton; in fact, the superuniverses were named after these satellites. (17:1.1, 17:1.5)

they might be termed the board of managing directors of the post-Havona creation. (17:1.3)

Yet this board does not make policy. Its members carry out the plans of divinity promulgated by the Master Spirits, and they do not interfere with the rule of the Ancients of Days or the sovereignty of the Creator Sons. They have no presiding head either; each time they meet, they choose one of their number to preside. That beings of perfect divinity should co-operate so humbly shows that true authority lies less in domination than in service and co-ordination. (17:1.2, 17:1.4)

They are the co-ordinating executives whose function it is to carry out the combined policies of all duly constituted rulers in the grand universe. (17:1.4)

Each executive has two advisory cabinets. Their make-up shows that ascending mortals are never left out of universe management: in all matters affecting the ascenders of time, mortals who have attained Paradise give their counsel directly. (17:1.8)

It is striking that a great center of administration is at the same time a place of remembrance and celebration. (17:1.9, 17:1.10)

II. Majeston — Chief of Reflectivity

The Reflective Spirits number forty-nine. They were created seven at a time, each creative episode effected by a liaison of the Paradise Trinity and one of the Seven Master Spirits. When the forty-ninth Reflective Spirit had personalized and the cycle was complete, a new and far-reaching reaction occurred in the Deity Absolute, which culminated in the personalization of Majeston, the reflectivity chief. (17:2.1, 17:2.2)

Majeston is a true person, the personal and infallible center of reflectivity phenomena in all seven superuniverses of time and space. (17:2.3)

The creation of Majeston was the first supreme creative act of the Supreme Being. The Supreme willed it, but the response of the Deity Absolute went far beyond what was intended or foreseen. On this basis the revelators confess that they stand in awe of the possibility that, as the Supreme and the Ultimate reach new levels, undreamed of beings may yet appear. The universe is not a finished machine; it remains a place open to newness that surprises even Deity. (17:2.5, 17:2.6)

III–IV. The Reflective Spirits and the Reflective Image Aids — The Living Memory of the Universe

Each of the seven creative episodes produced a type of being resembling its coancestral Master Spirit. The Reflective Spirits therefore reflect in various ways the seven possible combinations of the divinity characteristics of the three Deities, and one of each type is assigned to every superuniverse headquarters. Their names are part of one of the seven universal mysteries of the secret spheres of Paradise. (17:3.1, 17:3.2)

Curiously, neither the Master Spirits nor the Paradise Deities disclose the powers of reflectivity just as these spirits manifest them; yet they are the creators of these gifted beings. The passage shows that creatures can bring to flower, in a new form, what they have received from their Creator. (17:3.3)

Divine heredity does sometimes disclose in the creature certain attributes which are not discernible in the Creator. (17:3.3)

The Reflective Spirits are not merely transmitting agents; they are retentive personalities as well. Everything of true spiritual value is registered in duplicate. This means that our good deeds and sincere strivings do not vanish; they are kept in the minds of living personalities. (17:3.5)

These are the live records in contrast with the formal and dead records of the universe, and they are perfectly preserved in the living minds of the recording personalities of the Infinite Spirit. (17:3.6)

Reflectivity is also the news-gathering and decree-disseminating mechanism of all creation, and it works constantly rather than periodically like the broadcasts. It may look automatic, but the revelator insists that it is all very personal and intelligent, the fruit of perfect personality co-operation. Behind the precision of the universe stands not a machine but persons. (17:3.7, 17:3.8)

In each superuniverse the Reflective Spirits created their own Reflective Image Aids, true likenesses of themselves. An Image Aid reproduces its Reflective Mother Spirit perfectly, minus the attribute of reflectivity, and serves as the Spirit’s personal voice to the courts of the Ancients of Days. But the Image Aids are devoid of will and do not choose, and so ascending mortals will not personally contact them even in the schools of Uversa. (17:3.4, 17:4.1, 17:4.3)

they are images, and they are true to their name. (17:4.1)

V. The Seven Spirits of the Circuits — Impersonal Companions of Havona

The Seven Spirits of the Circuits are the collective offspring of the Seven Master Spirits, impersonal representatives who each permeate one of the seven circuits of Havona. Where the Master Spirits give diversified individuality to the superuniverses, through these uniform Circuit Spirits they provide unified supervision for the central universe. The Circuit Spirits contact Havona sojourners through their personal offspring, the tertiary supernaphim. (17:5.1, 17:5.2, 17:5.3)

The paper sets the Circuit Spirits beside the Thought Adjusters. Both are impersonal and both consort with mind, but the Circuit Spirits never become a permanent part of Havona personalities, whereas the Adjuster is destined to become one with the mortal. The comparison throws into relief how remarkable a gift the mortals of time have received. (17:5.5)

VI–VII. The Local Universe Creative Spirits and the Adjutant Mind-Spirits

The sixth group, the local universe Creative Spirits, has the most moving career in the paper. The revelators know six phases in the life of a Mother Spirit and speculate about a seventh. A potential that arises within the Infinite Spirit when a Creator Son is born is differentiated through the Master Spirit’s “prayer of identification,” becomes a true person at the time of life creation, and at last is raised to cosovereignty. (17:6.2, 17:6.5, 17:6.7)

This story is a model of divine partnership. The union the Father acknowledges carries the two through the long and arduous period of material organization, and after victory it flowers into the recognition of equality. The picture of two co-operating persons growing together is worth holding up to our own relationships. (17:6.5, 17:6.6)

The seventh and last group, the adjutant mind-spirits, is the sevenfold mind bestowal that a Mother Spirit gives to living creatures once she attains the status of personality prerogatives. The detailed account is left for the story of Nebadon, but within the design of this paper the point is clear: the very foundation of our minds is already linked to the last place in the line of the seven Supreme Spirit groups. (17:7.1)

VIII. Functions of the Supreme Spirits — Unifying the Descending Administration

The seven Supreme Spirit groups form the nucleus of the functional family of the Third Source and Center. Their domain extends from the presence of the Trinity on Paradise to the functioning of mind on the planets of space, and so they unify the descending levels of administration. Together with the Infinite Mother Spirit they are also the immediate creators of nearly every order of ministering spirits. (17:8.1, 17:8.2)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 17 is an administrative paper full of unfamiliar names, but a clear message runs beneath it. The grand universe is joined from top to bottom by an unbroken chain of spirit. Each link of that chain moves by co-ordination and service rather than domination. And everything of spiritual value is preserved as a living record.

Paper 18 turns next to the trinitized beings glimpsed in this paper, the Supreme Trinity Personalities. If we keep in mind the picture of the Infinite Spirit’s family drawn here, it becomes much easier to follow how the government of the grand universe is organized in the papers that follow.
