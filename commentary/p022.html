Introduction — The Third Group of the Sons of God

If Papers 20 and 21 dealt with the descending Sons, Paper 22 introduces the third group, the Trinitized Sons of God. By origin they fall into three divisions: Deity-trinitized Sons, Trinity-embraced Sons, and Creature-trinitized Sons. The Deity-trinitized Sons are not revealed in these narratives, so the paper is devoted to the other two, and especially to the Trinity-embraced sons. (22:0.1, 22:0.5)

What the three divisions share is the experience of trinitization. Some came into being through it; others received the Trinity embrace at the end of their ascent. The author himself is a Mighty Messenger, a being who began as a mortal and received this embrace, and so this paper touches our own future in a special way. (22:0.5, 22:2.6)

I. The Trinity-Embraced Sons — Seven Orders, Three Divisions

After the embrace, the Trinity-embraced sons are forever devoted to Trinity service, and for superuniverse service they are organized into seven orders of personalities. By origin, nature, and function these seven are grouped again into three divisions: Sons of Attainment, Sons of Selection, and Sons of Perfection. (22:1.1, 22:1.9)

When the Sons of Attainment are embraced, their names are removed from the finaliter roll call. And while their Trinity-origin associates, the Perfectors of Wisdom, Divine Counselors, and Universal Censors, are of fixed numbers, the Trinity-embraced sons keep increasing. Each superuniverse receives exactly the same number, and not one has ever been lost. (22:1.10, 22:1.13)

Trinity-embraced beings have never gone astray (22:1.13)

The author adds frankly, however, that the Sons of Perfection have sometimes erred in judgment and caused transient confusion. This small qualification leads later into the paper’s great theme: the value of experience. (22:1.13)

II. The Mighty Messengers — Loyalty That Was Tested

The Mighty Messengers are perfected mortals who have been rebellion tested or otherwise proved in their personal loyalty. At some point in their ascent they stood firm in the face of the disloyalty of their superiors. Yet this paper values action that prevents rebellion even more highly than loyalty in the face of it. (22:2.1, 22:2.3)

action designed to prevent rebellion or to effect higher types of loyalty in a universe crisis is regarded as of even greater value than loyalty in the face of actual rebellion. (22:2.3)

The author tells us that the companion of his own mortal experience also triumphed in the great test; after long separations during the ascent, they were embraced in the same group of seven hundred thousand and are now often sent out together. The Messengers act as defenders of individuals and planets in the superuniverse courts and are stationed on many worlds as official observers of the Ancients of Days. Because they remain fully conscious of their whole ascendant careers, they are sympathetic ministers to any creature. (22:2.6, 22:2.8, 22:2.9)

As soon as you are delivered from the flesh, you will communicate freely and understandingly with us (22:2.9)

III–IV. Those High in Authority and Those without Name and Number — Administrative Genius and Souls of Worship

Those High in Authority are mortals who showed superior administrative ability and executive genius throughout their ascent, and they present the cause of justice in the superuniverse tribunals. Because our prosecutors would be onetime ascenders who know every step of our path, the paper says we are hardly likely to suffer injustice. Those without Name and Number are souls whose ability to worship has developed beyond that of all others, giving them a spiritual concept above name and number; they serve as the supreme jurors of Orvonton. (22:3.1, 22:3.4, 22:4.1, 22:4.3)

Adjudication is the highest function of any government, and those who are intrusted with verdict rendering should be chosen from the highest and most noble types of the most experienced and understanding individuals. (22:4.3)

The selection of candidates for these three orders is not arbitrary but inherent and automatic: personal experience and spiritual values determine who they are. So although they are equal in authority and status, they differ as much as their ascendant careers differed. When the viewpoint of a mortal creature is in doubt, an ascendant commission of a Mighty Messenger, One High in Authority, and One without Name and Number settles the question. (22:4.4, 22:4.6)

they are not standardized beings. (22:4.4)

V–VI. The Trinitized Custodians and Ambassadors — A Share for Those Who Walked Beside Us

The story of the Sons of Selection shows that ascension is not the work of mortals alone. Our seraphic guardians and midway associates, too, may traverse Havona, attain Paradise, and receive the same Trinity recognition. Seraphim are so honored because of their valiant co-operation with an ascendant mortal who was later trinitized; the author’s own seraphic guardian now serves on Uversa as a Trinitized Custodian. (22:5.1, 22:5.3)

The Custodians are officers of the superuniverse who handle not individuals but group affairs: records, plans, and institutions. The Ambassadors are chosen from Spirit-fused and Son-fused mortals on the advice of their Havona teachers; as emissaries of the Ancients of Days and their emergency reserve corps, they carry out countless duties that human minds cannot picture. (22:5.6, 22:6.2, 22:6.3)

VII. Technique of Trinitization — The Adventure of Two Becoming One

The techniques of trinitization belong to the secrets of Vicegerington and Solitarington and can be understood only by those who have experienced them. Apart from the Deities, only Paradise-Havona personalities and certain finaliters engage in it; mortal finaliters may attempt it as one of seven ways to spend the recess granted every millennium of Havona time. A creature may take part in such an experience only once. (22:7.1, 22:7.3, 22:7.4)

If two finaliters show that they have independently chosen the same concept, the Architects may extend their recess, and Master Spirit Number Seven authorizes the undertaking. Unbelievably long ages may follow, and the effort may fail through no discoverable error. Those who fail are admitted to a special group of finaliters. (22:7.5, 22:7.6)

Candidates for trinitization who thus fail are admitted to a special group of finaliters who are designated as beings who have made the supreme effort and sustained the supreme disappointment. (22:7.6)

If they succeed, the two parents become one functionally and spiritually. They are still listed as two personalities, but during the present universe age they are inseparable in assignment and function. The experience is on the order of the Father and the Son becoming one in eternalizing the Infinite Spirit, yet this union is not eternal; it will end with the completed factualization of the experiential Deities. (22:7.8, 22:7.9)

where one goes the other goes, what one does the other does. (22:7.9)

VIII. The Creature-Trinitized Sons — Many Paths of Destiny

All creature-trinitized beings, revealed or not, receive personality from the Universal Father. Young ascender-trinitized and Paradise-Havona-trinitized sons first serve under the Supreme Executives on the seven Paradise spheres of the Infinite Spirit, and may then be adopted by Trinity Teacher Sons for training as apprentices in the local universes. When the Teacher Sons nominate them for the Trinity embrace, they emerge as Trinitized Sons of Perfection. (22:8.1, 22:8.2, 22:8.4)

Many are never embraced. They become associates of the Master Spirits, the Reflective Spirits, and the Mother Spirits, take special assignments on Paradise and the secret worlds, or join the conjoint corps on the inner Havona circuit. The paper says that the supreme destiny of most creature-trinitized sons appears to be entrance into the Corps of Trinitized Finaliters. (22:8.5, 22:8.6)

IX–X. The Celestial Guardians and High Son Assistants — Perfection Without Experience

Creature-trinitized sons are embraced in classes of seven thousand; on the advice of the Teacher Sons, those of more acceptable service become High Son Assistants and those of less distinguished performance become Celestial Guardians. The Guardians are officers of the courts of the Ancients of Days, bearing summonses and bringing in those whose presence is required. They have never been indwelt by Adjusters nor fused with Spirit or Son; the Trinity embrace compensates for this nonfused status. (22:9.1, 22:9.4, 22:9.5)

The author says that these twice-trinitized sons are neither as versatile nor as dependable as their ascendant associates, because they lack the experience of actually climbing up to glory from the dark domains of space. On the mansion worlds he has seen them look longingly at even new arrivals, and their willingness to acknowledge their deficiencies is transcendently beautiful. (22:9.6, 22:9.8)

There is simply nothing in all universal existence which can take the place of actual personal experience (22:9.7)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 22 may look like a staff roster of superuniverse government, but beneath it runs one conviction: nothing can replace experience. Even beings who embody perfect ideas envy those who climb up from humble origins. And the author tells his mortal readers that they, too, may one day receive the Trinity embrace and be sent to enlarge the revelation of truth to some evolving planet. (22:9.6, 22:4.7)
