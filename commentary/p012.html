Introduction — What We Can Learn Before Immensity

If Paper 11 describes the eternal Isle of Paradise, Paper 12 surveys the whole master universe that spreads out around it. Even the presenter, a Perfector of Wisdom, confesses that its enormousness staggers the concept of his order of being. Yet he says that the mortal mind can be taught much about the plan and arrangement of the universes, their physical organization, and their administration. (12:0.1)

The paper begins with a tension. Because the Father is infinite, we may conceive of creation as infinite in principle. But the material creation we actually observe is limited at any given moment. Much of the potential of the Infinite is still unrevealed, and the revelation of the eternal purpose is still in progress. (12:0.2, 12:0.3)

To created beings the master universe might appear to be almost infinite, but it is far from finished (12:0.3)

To say that the universe is not yet finished also describes our own situation. As creation grows, so do creatures. The paper begins with stars and galaxies and ends with the Father who lives in each person and with the brotherhood of all.

I. Space Levels of the Master Universe — Six Concentric Ellipses

The universe of universes is not an infinite plane or a boundless cube; it is an organized whole with definite dimensions. As evidence, the paper points out that all basic energy forever swings around the curved paths of the space levels in obedience to Paradise gravity. If the universe were open, some energy would shoot off in a straight line into trackless space, but such behavior is never found. (12:1.1, 12:1.2)

The Paradise-Havona System is the perfect and eternal nucleus of the master universe, around which the superuniverses and all regions of outer space revolve. The seven superuniverses are geographic clusterings, each holding roughly one seventh of the post-Havona creation, and together they have a potential of around seven trillion inhabited planets. Our local universe, Nebadon, is one of the newer creations in Orvonton, the seventh superuniverse, near the outer border of the grand universe. (12:1.11, 12:1.12, 12:1.13)

The central universe is the creation of eternity; the seven superuniverses are the creations of time (12:1.16)

II. The Domains of the Unqualified Absolute — A Future Creation in the Making

The space beyond the borders of the seven superuniverses is generally recognized as the domains of the Unqualified Absolute. The millions of universes in formation that our telescopes reveal are the outworking of the plans of the Architects of the Master Universe. The energy and matter of these outer regions already equal many times that of all seven superuniverses, yet as far as is known no mortals or angels live there, and they lie beyond the jurisdiction of the superuniverse governments. (12:2.1, 12:2.4, 12:2.5)

What, then, is all this vast preparation for? The presenter shares the belief that it will become the scene of the future activities of the assembling Corps of the Finality. It is a hint that the end of the ascent may be not rest but the beginning of new service. (12:2.6)

the endless future may hold for all of you the same enthralling spectacles that the endless past has held for your seniors and predecessors. (12:2.6)

III. Universal Gravity — Four Absolute Circuits

All forms of force-energy, whether material, mindal, or spiritual, are alike subject to the universal presences we call gravity. The paper names four absolute-gravity circuits in the master universe. They are not circuits of force or energy but absolute presence circuits, and like God they are independent of time and space. (12:3.1, 12:3.6)

Personality Gravity is noncomputable. (12:3.12)

IV. Space and Motion — A Universe That Never Rests

Nothing in the master universe is absolutely stationary except the eternal Isle of Paradise; everything else revolves along the circuits of the space levels. On the origin of motion the presenter is cautious. He thinks the Conjoint Actor initiates motion in space but cannot prove it, and the Universal Absolute does not originate motion but equalizes the tensions it produces. (12:4.1, 12:4.3, 12:4.5)

Source 12:4.7

The section also points out a trap in astronomical observation. The apparent speed at which distant outer universes seem to be receding is not real. It arises from angles of observation, time-space distortions, and above all from the fact that the neighboring outer universes revolve in the opposite direction from the grand universe: the superuniverses turn counterclockwise, while the first outer level turns clockwise. (12:4.14, 12:4.15)

V. Space and Time — Beings Who Look Ahead

Time, like space, is a bestowal of Paradise, but only indirectly. Time comes by virtue of motion and because mind is inherently aware of sequence. Time and space are inseparable only in the seven superuniverses, and the only truly nontemporal place is Paradise. This is why Paradise pilgrims become unconscious during the transit from time to eternity. (12:5.1, 12:5.3, 12:5.4)

Source 12:5.6

The practical conclusion of this section is short but strong. Animals know only the past and live in the present, but spirit-indwelt man can visualize the future. To remain in static ethics and traditional morality is only slightly above the animal level; only an attitude that moves forward is personally real. (12:5.10)

Only forward-looking and progressive attitudes are personally real. (12:5.10)

VI. Universal Overcontrol — The Predictable and the Unpredictable

The stability of the universe does not come from inertia. It is the product of balanced energies, co-operative minds, co-ordinated morontias, spirit overcontrol, and personality unification, and it is always proportional to divinity. The Father exercises physical control through the Isle of Paradise and spiritual administration through the Eternal Son, while in the domains of mind the Father and the Son function together in the Conjoint Actor. (12:6.1, 12:6.2)

Mind alone can interassociate the physical forces and energies of the material level with the spiritual powers and beings of the spirit level. (12:6.3)

The universe is highly predictable only in the quantitative, gravity-measured sense. When matter, mind, and spirit are unified in a creature personality, the decisions of that freewill being cannot be fully predicted. The presenter surmises that the unexpected reactions observed in outer space disclose the presence of the Ultimate and the Absolutes. He also sets out that the appearance of the post-Havona universes depends on the Absolutes in potential, the Ultimate in direction, the Supreme in evolutionary co-ordination, and the Architects of the Master Universe in administration before specific rulers appear. (12:6.5, 12:6.6, 12:6.8)

VII. The Part and the Whole — Mercy and Impartiality

This is the warmest section of the paper. Mercy characterizes God’s attitude toward the individual; impartiality, his attitude toward the whole. God’s will does not always prevail in one person’s heart, but it does rule the universe of universes. And God’s laws are not arbitrary commands but God’s habits, his way of always doing things in the best way. (12:7.1, 12:7.2)

The laws of God are merely the habits of God, his way of repeatedly doing things; and he ever does all things well. (12:7.2)

This does not make God a machine bound by law. If a situation called for a better way, God would act in that better way, and this would be the expression of a higher law, not the reversal of a lower one. He is not a mathematical equation but a freewill and primal personality, so his steadfastness is a personal and volitional faithfulness. (12:7.3, 12:7.6)

On the level of personality, the paradox of the part and the whole appears as the Fatherhood of God and the brotherhood of man. The Father’s love makes each person a unique child without duplicate, and because that same love goes out to everyone, universal brotherhood comes into being. No one, therefore, can escape the benefits or the penalties that come from relationship with others. (12:7.8, 12:7.9, 12:7.11)

The section ends by urging us not to be overawed by immensity. The Father dwells on Paradise, but a fragment of him lives within us to be our real and eternal self. Neither the spirit of the Son nor the seraphim can come as near to us as the Father does. (12:7.12, 12:7.13, 12:7.14)

VIII. Matter, Mind, and Spirit

The eighth section connects the three kinds of drawing power with the three persons of Deity. Cosmic gravity is the domain of the Isle of Paradise, spiritual gravity is the realm of the Eternal Son, and the realms of mind belong to the Conjoint Actor. Physical energy is the one reality that is steadfast in its obedience to universal law; deviation from the divine plans has occurred only in the realm of creature volition. (12:8.2, 12:8.3, 12:8.6)

Source 12:8.9
Level of finite realityDefinition
MatterOrganized energy subject to linear gravity, except as modified by motion and conditioned by mind
MindOrganized consciousness not wholly subject to material gravity, truly liberated when modified by spirit
SpiritThe highest personal reality, not subject to physical gravity

The more spiritual a mind becomes, the less it responds to material gravity. Physical gravity measures the quantity of nonspirit energy; spirit gravity measures the quality of divinity. Mind, matter, and spirit are equally real, but they are not of equal value in the attainment of divinity. At death, mind (identity) and spirit survive; the body does not. (12:8.5, 12:8.15, 12:8.16)

Mind is the technique whereby spirit realities become experiential to creature personalities. (12:8.8)

IX. Personal Realities — What Analysis Cannot Reveal

The last section is about how persons are known. Love is the secret of good association between persons; we cannot know someone from a single contact, any more than a telephone number tells us anything about a person’s character. Just as no analysis of water reveals that it puts out fire, so no mathematics or chemistry could have predicted that hydrogen and oxygen would combine to form water. The whole can be more than, or different from, the sum of its parts. (12:9.2, 12:9.3, 12:9.4)

Love is the secret of beneficial association between personalities. (12:9.2)

Mortal man has a spirit nucleus, and the mind is a personal-energy system existing around that nucleus in a material environment. Real defeat and inescapable death come only when self-concepts presume to displace fully the governing power of that spirit nucleus. To whom we yield the center within us finally decides the destiny of our personality. (12:9.6)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 12 begins like astronomy and ends as a story of faith. The universe is not yet finished and keeps growing, and that growth is held together as one whole by four gravity circuits. Within that immense whole each person is loved as a child without duplicate and at the same time shares a common destiny with everyone. And the Father is nearer to us than any other being, for he lives within us.

Papers 13 and 14 turn to the innermost part of this map: the sacred spheres of Paradise and the perfect central universe of Havona. With the six levels of this paper in mind, it is easy to follow which part of the map each of those papers illuminates.
