Introduction — A Chain of Administration That Never Fails

If Paper 17 showed us the functional family of the Infinite Spirit, Paper 18 introduces the administrators made directly by the Paradise Trinity. The Supreme Trinity Personalities are all designed for specific service, and they serve with perfection of technique and finality of devotion. Their numbers are definite and final; no more are being personalized. (18:0.1, 18:0.9)

These seven orders form an interrelated line of administrative perfection reaching from the Paradise spheres of the Father to the capitals of the local universe constellations. They represent the policies and the justice of the Trinity, and they are at the same time its executive judgment. (18:0.10)

There is never any danger of default or risk of rebellion with Trinity-origin beings. (18:0.11)

For us who live amid the confusion of an evolutionary world, this is a great comfort. Above the universes of time, where rebellion and error do occur, rests a framework of justice and wisdom that never wavers. Yet even these beings, in the realms of experience, have gained added equipment for cosmic service with the passing of time. (18:0.11)

I. The Trinitized Secrets of Supremacy — The Privacy of Personality Respected

The seven worlds of the innermost circuit of the Paradise satellites, the personal circuit of the Universal Father, are governed by seventy Secrets of Supremacy, ten to each world. The work of each world is divided into seven major departments, each headed by one of the ten; the remaining three represent the Father, the Son, and the Spirit. Each group resembles the Deity or Deity association characteristic of its world. (18:1.1, 18:1.2, 18:1.3)

Why is the work of these worlds secret? Because it concerns the intimate and personal contact of the Deities with the seven basic groupings of created beings. There are no arbitrary secrets in the approach to God, but the personal relationship of Deity with each creature is held sacred. (18:1.4, 18:1.5)

The Deities are an open book to all who attain divine perfection, but all the Secrets of Supremacy can never be fully attained. (18:1.4)
The Paradise Creators respect the privacy and sanctity of personality even in their lowly creatures. (18:1.5)

This teaching sheds light on how we treat one another. The relationship between God and a single person is holy ground that no one else may casually judge or pry into. The revelator adds that these secret worlds ever remain a test of loyalty, even to beings of high universe attainment. (18:1.6)

II. The Eternals of Days — Original Rulers of Havona’s Billion Worlds

Each of the billion worlds of Havona has one Eternal of Days. They have ruled their worlds forever, without rotation or reassignment, and they are visible to all will creatures in their domains. They are close of kin to the Ancients of Days and are their divine equals. (18:2.1, 18:2.2)

What stands out is their creativity. Each has developed his sphere according to his own personal ideas and ideals, and though they visit one another, they never imitate. So every Havona world is a place of everlasting beauty, wholly unlike any other. It is a fine example of how perfection does not mean uniformity. (18:2.3, 18:2.4)

They visit each other’s planets, but they do not copy or imitate; they are always and wholly original. (18:2.3)

This section also offers a small correction about the direction of ascent. We speak of going “up” to Paradise, but it would be more correct to say that the goal of ascension lies “inward.” The journey of faith, too, is in the end less a climb to outward heights than a journey of depth toward the center. (18:2.4)

III. The Ancients of Days — Rulers of the Superuniverses

The twenty-one Ancients of Days were all trinitized at the same time, and they stand at the very beginning of the personality records of the universe of universes; hence their name. They always govern the superuniverses in groups of three. (18:3.4, 18:3.5)

They never personally leave their residential worlds, and they do not need to, for these worlds are the superuniverse focal points of the far-flung reflectivity system. The reflectivity service we met in Paper 17 thus becomes the practical instrument of superuniverse government. Their abodes lie at the point of spiritual polarity on the headquarters sphere, which is divided into seventy administrative sectors. (18:3.5, 18:3.6)

They are the most powerful of all the direct rulers of the time-space creations. They alone are invested with final executive judgment concerning the eternal extinction of will creatures, and all three must participate in such decrees. That the eternal destiny of a single being is handled with such care shows how fair and solemn God’s justice is. (18:3.7)

Yet they have not earned their right to rule through experience. They are therefore destined one day to be superseded by the Supreme Being, the experiential sovereign, and to become his vicegerents. Just as a Creator Son earns the sovereignty of his local universe by experience, so the Supreme is achieving the sovereignty of the seven superuniverses through experiential service. (18:3.8, 18:3.9)

the wisdom of originality and the initiative of individuality characterize all the decrees and rulings of the Ancients of Days. (18:3.9)

IV–V. The Perfections of Days and the Recents of Days — Teachers of the Ascenders

The two hundred and ten Perfections of Days preside, three at a time, over the ten major sectors of each superuniverse. They are peculiarly perfect in the mastery of administrative details, hence their name. The revelator admits how hard it is to translate the names of spiritual beings into our language, and says they try to choose names that are clear to us and yet somewhat representative of the original. (18:4.1, 18:4.3)

The twenty-one thousand Recents of Days are the youngest of the superuniverse directors and govern the minor sectors in groups of three. They are co-ordinate in nature with the Perfections of Days but subordinate in authority. The minor sector governments deal largely with physical problems, and their spheres are the headquarters of the Master Physical Controllers. (18:5.1, 18:5.3)

These two orders are very close to ascending mortals. We will pass through the hands of the three Recents of Days of Ensa, our minor sector, advance to the major sector of Splandon, and see all thirty Perfections of Days of Orvonton before we reach Uversa. Each stage of training has a distinct character. (18:4.7, 18:4.9, 18:5.5)

This sequence, from matter to intellect and from intellect to spirit, traces the natural path of growth. Moreover, nearly all the administrators of these governments served apprenticeships under the Eternals of Days in Havona. That even perfect beings learn and are trained reminds us that learning is the basic pattern of an eternal career. (18:4.6)

They are all seasoned, tried, and experienced administrators. (18:4.6)

The three Recents of Days are seldom together on their capital. One may be conferring with his major sector superiors or attending a Paradise conclave, and another may be away inspecting local universe headquarters. Yet at least one always remains on duty. This sense of responsibility, never leaving the post empty however busy things become, is a good example for anyone entrusted with even a small community. (18:5.4)

VI–VII. The Unions of Days and the Faithfuls of Days — Counselors to the Local Universe

The Trinity personalities of the order of “Days” do not administer below the level of the superuniverse governments. In the local universes they serve only as counselors. The Union of Days is the Trinity ambassador assigned to each local universe, and apart from his duties as observer he acts only at the request of the local authorities. He is an ex officio member of all primary councils, but he takes no part in the technical details of administration. (18:6.1, 18:6.5)

He is called a “Union” because he co-ordinates the activities of all branches of universal government. He makes a threefold report, according to the nature of the matter. (18:6.3)

Source 18:6.3
Content of the reportSuperiors who receive it
Data of a physical and semi-intellectual natureThe Recents of Days of the minor sector
Intellectual and quasi-spiritual happeningsThe Perfections of Days of the major sector
Spiritual and semiparadisiacal mattersThe Ancients of Days at the superuniverse capital

A Creator Son of dual Deity origin rules the local universe, but at his side there is always a Paradise brother of Trinity origin. When the Creator Son is temporarily absent, the acting rulers are largely guided by the counsel of their Union of Days in their major decisions. The greater the authority, the more the universe provides a wise counselor close at hand. (18:6.7)

The Faithfuls of Days fill the same role for the Vorondadek Sons who rule the constellations. Their name comes from their supreme devotion and divine faithfulness to the constellations they serve. Their abodes are indeed modest compared with the homes of the Vorondadek rulers. (18:7.1, 18:7.2, 18:7.4)

They are supremely devoted and divinely faithful to the welfare of their constellations of assignment (18:7.2)

The Trinity-origin regime stops with the constellations. The administration of the systems and inhabited worlds below them belongs wholly to beings native to the local universe. In other words, the affairs of our world are entrusted to beings who are close to us. (18:7.5)

These latter administrative units are wholly under the jurisdiction of beings native to the local universes. (18:7.5)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 18 shows how the order of the universe is joined from top to bottom by justice and wisdom. The beings of that order are perfect yet not uniform, powerful yet willing to step back as counselors, and so respectful of personality that they guard the relationship between God and each creature as a secret. And along our path of ascent we will actually meet them.

Paper 19 continues with other beings who also take origin in the Trinity, the Co-ordinate Trinity-Origin Beings. If we remember the system of the “Days” met in this paper, the larger picture of grand universe government will become much clearer.
