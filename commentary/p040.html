Introduction — An Author Who Was Once a Mortal

The Urantia Papers began with the Deities and have descended the scale of universe beings until reaching the lowest life endowed with the potential of immortality. Paper 40 marks a turning point in that course. Its author is a Mighty Messenger who was once a mortal on an evolutionary world, and he now sets out the purpose of the Gods concerning the ascending Sons of God. (40:0.10)

I–IV. The Nonmortal Ascending Sons

Before treating the three orders of mortals in detail, the paper briefly introduces the nonmortal ascending sons. What they share is this: even beings who descended or who served can enter the path of ascent through experience. (40:0.11)

Source 40:1.1
BeingHow it becomes an ascending son
Evolutionary seraphimThrough experience and service guarding the mortals of time — many mustered into the Corps of Mortal Finality
Ascending Material SonsWhen their mission succeeds and the world is settled in light and life, they resign and register as perfected Material Sons
Translated midwayersReleased around the intermediate epochs of light and life — all secondary midwayers go to the Mortal Corps of Finality
Personalized AdjustersPersonalized by the Father after repeated service or unusual experience such as indwelling a bestowal Son

For an angel, reaching finaliter sonship is said to be a far greater achievement than mortal survival with the help of an indwelling Adjuster. Personalized Adjusters, for their part, are classed as ascending sons because they have become experiential by sharing in mortal lives. The paper also makes clear that when a mortal fails to survive, it is never due to any neglect on the part of the Adjuster. (40:1.2, 40:4.1, 40:4.2)

V. Mortals of Time and Space — The Farthest Comes Nearest

Mortals are the last link in the chain of beings called sons of God. The touch of the Eternal Son reaches down through ever less divine and more human personalizations to a being we can see and touch, and the line of the Infinite Spirit reaches its limit in the angels. The Father cannot step himself down in that way, yet as the Adjuster within us he comes nearest of all. (40:5.1, 40:5.2, 40:5.3)

you are not without the divine presence. (40:5.3)

Mortals are therefore classified chiefly by their relation to the Adjuster, since the Adjuster is the only spirit with potential for fusion during life in the flesh. (40:5.4)

Spirit identification constitutes the secret of personal survival and determines the destiny of spiritual ascension. (40:5.4)
Source 40:5.4
Mortal seriesRelation to the AdjusterPath of survival
Series oneIn primitive times the Adjuster sojourns transiently and experientially — group custodians instead of personal guardiansEternal life through the embrace of the Mother Spirit — many pre-Adamic Urantians

Series threeAdjuster-fusion potential — one-, two-, and three-brained types; Urantians are two-brainedFusion with the Adjuster and on to Paradise

The author frankly says he does not know why the second series cannot fuse with Adjusters. But on the chances of survival he is emphatic: what decides survival is not the type of brain or the conditions of a planet, but the desire to find God and the willingness to co-operate with the Adjuster. (40:5.16, 40:5.17)

All souls of every possible phase of mortal existence will survive provided they manifest willingness to co-operate with their indwelling Adjusters and exhibit a desire to find God and to attain divine perfection, even though these desires be but the first faint flickers of the primitive comprehension of that “true light which lights every man who comes into the world.” (40:5.19)

VI. The Faith Sons of God — Five Reasons We Are Sons

Mortals are the lowest order of intelligent creation and are not yet divine by nature. They are reckoned ascending sons the instant fusion occurs, but until then they hold the status of faith sons. The paper insists that this is no small status but a solemn and supernal fact, and it gives five grounds on which Urantia mortals may regard themselves as sons of God. (40:6.1, 40:6.2, 40:6.3)

Source 40:6.4
Ground of sonshipWhat it means
1. Promise and faithBy accepting sonship and believing in its reality, our sonship becomes eternally real
2. Michael our elder brotherA Creator Son became one of us and is our elder brother in fact
3. The spirit of a SonA spirit freely poured out on all races draws us to the Son and the Father
4. Creature personalityThe divine spontaneity of freewill action given by the Father
5. The indwelling fragmentA fragment of the Father lives within us, relating us directly to him

This list shows that sonship is a gift, not something earned, and yet it becomes real by being received. Only the first ground is our response; the other four are things God has already done. (40:6.4, 40:6.8)

VII. Father-Fused Mortals — When Two Become One

The indwelling of Adjusters is one of the unfathomable mysteries of God the Father. We are hardly yet ascending sons, but we may reach that status by faith and by freewill co-operation with the Adjuster. When we and our Adjusters are finally fused and made one, as in Christ Michael the Son of God and the Son of Man are one, we have in fact become ascending sons of God. (40:7.1, 40:7.2)

Adjusters are immortal spirits, and union with them confers eternal life upon the soul of the fused mortal. (40:7.1)

Adjuster-fused mortals are the only class of human beings who all traverse the Havona circuits and find God on Paradise. Because the Father has bestowed himself upon us, he also gives the command, “Be you perfect.” The author asks those of us who trudge through the so-called “vale of tears” whether we grasp the grandeur of the destiny that lies before us. (40:7.4, 40:7.5)

VIII–IX. Son-Fused and Spirit-Fused Mortals — Different Paths, the Same Love

Almost all survivors fuse with their Adjusters on the mansion worlds or soon after, but a few never do. They were judged worthy of survival and their Adjusters concurred, yet some synchronizing difficulty kept them from becoming one. When a court of inquiry finds the mortal not at fault, the Adjuster returns to Divinington and the mortal is fused with an individualized gift of the Creator Son’s spirit. There are fewer than a million such beings in Orvonton. (40:8.1, 40:8.3, 40:8.5)

they have enjoyed the “seventy times seven” opportunities for fusion and still have been unable to attain oneness with their Adjusters. (40:8.2)

Spirit-fused mortals are indwelt by Adjusters during life in the flesh but are not fusion candidates; at death the Adjuster departs, and on the mansion worlds they fuse with an individualization of the Mother Spirit’s spirit. Because the transcript of human memory belongs to the Adjuster, they awaken without consciousness of their former existence. Yet when seraphim retell their story and they consult the records, a residual experiential-recognition-response in the soul knows it to be true. (40:9.2, 40:9.3, 40:9.4, 40:9.6)

This passage also offers an intriguing insight into memory and personality. Only experiences of spiritual value survive with the Adjuster, and ascenders from the same planet can pool their memories to reconstruct a shared past. And at a level deeper than memory, personality recognizes personality. (40:9.7, 40:9.8)

Personalities of onetime association mutually respond quite independently of the operation of memory (40:9.8)

X. Ascendant Destinies — Different Does Not Mean Lesser

A fused spirit always ascends to its level of origin, so each of the three fusion types has its own range of destiny. (40:10.1)

The spirits of mortal fusion always ascend to the level of origin (40:10.1)
Source 40:10.1
Type of fusionFused spiritUsual range of destiny
Spirit fusionAn individualization of the spirit of the Divine MinisterThe local universe — home on the eighth group of Salvington worlds
Son fusionAn individualization of the spirit of the Creator SonThe superuniverse — residence on Uversa
Father fusionThe Thought AdjusterThrough the universe of universes to Paradise and the Corps of the Finality

This difference may look like a flaw in the ascension plan, but the author does not see it so. If every mortal left for Paradise, the local universes and superuniverses would depend wholly on beings in transit. Ascendant citizens who stay share in a universe’s long struggle to the end and gain a wisdom of focalized experience that finaliters do not acquire. And some Son-fused and Spirit-fused mortals, after transformation, actually do reach Paradise. (40:10.3, 40:10.4, 40:10.6, 40:10.10)

There is no favoritism, nothing arbitrary, in the selective operation of the divine plan of mortal survival. (40:10.5)

The author looks farther ahead as well. When finaliters are pioneering the frontiers of divinity in outer space, Spirit- and Son-fused mortals may be maintaining the experiential balance of the perfected superuniverses and preparing to welcome the new pilgrims who will stream in from the galaxies of outer space. He also notes that Father-fused finaliters are, in the present universe age, still unfinished creatures, sixth-stage spirits, while those embraced by the Trinity are for now finished creatures. Destinies cannot simply be ranked high and low. (40:10.8, 40:10.11, 40:10.12)

In the end, the words “greater” and “lesser” hardly fit when comparing ascendant destinies. Every son shares the fatherhood of God, and the Father loves each one individually and personally. That love utterly eclipses all other facts. (40:10.13)

Sonship is the supreme relationship of the creature to the Creator. (40:10.13)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 40 traces the many branches of sonship and arrives at a single conclusion. Sonship is a gift God gives first, and it becomes real when we receive it in faith and co-operate with the Adjuster. The paths differ, but the Father’s love makes no distinctions. What remains for us is to sense the obligation to make use of the plan already so freely provided. (40:6.4, 40:10.13, 40:10.14)
