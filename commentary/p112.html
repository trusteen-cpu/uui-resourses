Introduction — What Does Not Change amid Change

In Paper 112 a Solitary Messenger of Orvonton explains how personality survives death. Urantia is the starting point of the ascending career, the place where we and our Thought Adjusters are joined in temporary union. Those who sincerely run the race of time will be eternally united with their Adjusters and will discover that the present mortal state was only the vestibule of real life. (112:0.1, 112:7.20)

One axis runs through this whole long journey. Body, mind, and soul change constantly, yet amid all those changes one part of us remains absolutely unaltered. The author calls it personality, and rather than attempt a definition, he begins by recounting what is known about it. (112:0.1, 112:0.2)

Personality is changeless in the presence of change. (112:0.9)

I. Personality and Reality — A System, Not an Aggregation

Personality is a potentially eternal endowment, designed to function from the lowly finite to the borders of the absolute. Finite personality operates in three dimensions—length, depth, and breadth—and the personality of Urantia mortals has a potential of seven dimensions in all. These dimensions are greatly enhanced on the morontia level. (112:1.1, 112:1.5, 112:1.9)

Source 112:1.10

DepthOrganismal drives and attitudes, levels of self-realization, reaction to environmentValue
BreadthCo-ordination, association, and selfhood organizationInsight

Life is a process taking place between the organism and its environment, and personality gives that relationship the value of identity and the meaning of continuity. So stimulus and response are not a mere mechanical process. Nor can personality function well in isolation, for man is a social creature dominated by the craving to belong. (112:1.13, 112:1.16)

It is ever true that mechanisms are innately passive; organisms, inherently active. (112:1.13)

The author distinguishes relationships from systems. In aggregations parts are added; in systems parts are arranged and gain positional value. In a good system every factor is in its place; in a bad one something is missing or displaced. In the human being it is personality that unifies all these activities. (112:1.17, 112:1.19)

II. The Self — The One Who Observes the Observation

In studying the self, an order is worth remembering: physical systems are subordinate, intellectual systems are co-ordinate, personality is superordinate, and the indwelling spirit is potentially directive. And the fact of life comes first, its interpretation later. The child first lives and only afterward thinks about his living. (112:2.1, 112:2.6)

The very possession of personality marks a person as a spiritual being. Even the materialist’s ability to deny supermaterial reality shows that spirit synthesis is at work in his mind. Science observes the material world, philosophy observes that observation, and religion experientially realizes the reality of the one who observes all this observing. (112:2.9, 112:2.12)

The observer cannot be the thing observed (112:2.12)
In time, thinking leads to wisdom and wisdom leads to worship (112:2.13)

The purpose of cosmic evolution is increasing spirit dominance—the unification of personality through volitional response to the Adjuster’s leading. The author calls this “the evolution of dominance.” Before becoming finaliters, ascending personalities have the God-seeking experience; afterward, they have the God-revealing experience of showing God to those still seeking him. The seeker at last becomes the one who reveals. (112:2.15, 112:2.17, 112:2.18)

A self that can survive does not arise by itself. The transfer of the seat of identity from the material body to the immortal morontia soul is effected by the sincerity, persistence, and steadfastness of our God-seeking decisions. In the end, the key to survival lies in the quality of the decisions we make today. (112:2.20)

III–IV. The Phenomenon of Death; Adjusters after Death

Most people recognize only one kind of death, the cessation of physical life, but with regard to personality survival there are really three. In every case the Adjuster leaves the person for Divinington and is registered out by the same number that recorded its entry into the domains of time. (112:3.1, 112:4.1)

Source 112:3.1
Kind of deathWhat happens
Spiritual (soul) deathFinal rejection of survival — even if the body lives on, from the cosmic standpoint the person is already dead
Intellectual (mind) deathThe mind circuits of will-action are irreparably destroyed — by prior choice the soul may still survive
Physical (body and mind) deathThe Adjuster stays until the brain’s vital pulsations cease, then departs unceremoniously

After death the body returns to the elemental world, but two nonmaterial factors persist. The Adjuster goes to Divinington carrying the memory transcription of the mortal career, and the morontia soul remains in the keeping of the destiny guardian. At death the Adjuster loses personality but not identity, while the human loses identity but not personality. (112:3.5, 112:3.7)

Those who go to the mansion worlds are not permitted to send messages back to their loved ones. (112:3.7)

If a person has reached the third circle or higher, has been assigned a personal guardian, and the records of Adjuster and seraphim agree, the soul goes immediately to the resurrection halls of the mansion worlds. Others await a group repersonalization at the end of the dispensation, and meanwhile their Adjusters choose a temporary assignment, such as the schools of Divinington. (112:4.4, 112:4.12)

V. Survival of the Human Self — One True Opportunity

Personality comes from the Father and is therefore eternal, but with regard to identity it is a conditioned eternal reality. An energy system must continue; it has no choice. Man, however, has everything to do with determining his own destiny. The Adjuster is the path to Paradise, but walking that path is a matter of free choice. (112:5.2, 112:5.3)

The universe governments handle this choice with great generosity. Those who, through no fault of their own, could not master the circles on earth because of the accidents of time are given extended probation, and when there is doubt about advancing someone, the decision always favors the individual. This does not mean a second chance; it means that everyone enjoys one true opportunity to make a final choice. (112:5.6, 112:5.7, 112:5.9)

We had rather assume the risk of a system rebellion than to court the hazard of depriving one struggling mortal from any evolutionary world of the eternal joy of pursuing the ascending career. (112:5.8)

What survives death is the soul, the conjoint child of the human you and the divine Adjuster. This soul waits, wholly unconscious, in the keeping of the destiny guardian until repersonalization, which takes place in the life-assembly chambers of the resurrection halls. (112:5.12, 112:5.13, 112:5.16)

On awakening, much of earthly life may at first seem like a vague and hazy dream. Memories with no spiritual meaning perish with the material brain, but memories of relationships between persons have cosmic value and persist. On the mansion worlds we will recognize our former associates and be remembered by them. (112:5.21, 112:5.22)

But personality and the relationships between personalities are never scaffolding (112:5.22)

VI. The Morontia Self — Outside and Inside Becoming One

Just as a butterfly emerges from the caterpillar stage, so will the true personalities of human beings emerge on the mansion worlds (112:6.1)

In physical life a person can be outwardly beautiful though inwardly unlovely. In the morontia life, however, the form varies directly with the inner nature, and the nearer one comes to the spirit level, the closer outward form and inner nature approach complete identity. The character we cultivate now will become our face in the future. (112:6.3)

When the material mind is gone, the morontia soul cannot retain self-consciousness without the Adjuster. The memory patterns in the soul come alive when the returning Adjuster energizes them. Leaving the native planet, the ascender leaves the adjutant ministry behind and depends on morontia intellect; leaving the local universe, he reaches the spiritual level. (112:6.7, 112:6.8, 112:6.10)

The persistence of memory is proof that the original self has been retained. That is why those who ascend without Adjusters spend considerable time, with the help of seraphic instructors, rebuilding memory and recapturing the meanings and values of their former lives. That memory is treated with such importance also means that the days we live on earth are not lived in vain. (112:6.8)

VII. Adjuster Fusion — The Eternal Partnership of God and Man

Fusion with the Adjuster imparts to personality eternal actualities that were previously only potential. It usually takes place while the ascender is resident in the local system, and it never occurs until the superuniverse has pronounced the person’s choice final and irrevocable. Once fusion is effected, there is no further danger to that personality’s eternal career. (112:7.1, 112:7.3, 112:7.4, 112:7.5)

Has the Adjuster won personality, or has the human acquired immortality? The author answers: neither. Together they have achieved a new ascending personality. Because the human was the first to enjoy personality, the human outranks the Adjuster in matters of personality recognition, and the Paradise headquarters of this union is Ascendington, not Divinington. (112:7.9, 112:7.12)

they are inseparable, eternally fused. (112:7.10)

With this fusion the Father completes his promise of the gift of himself to his material creatures. The author believes that those who reach the Corps of the Finality will one day serve as understanding and sympathetic administrators in the universes of the first outer space level. (112:7.14, 112:7.17, 112:7.18)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 112 teaches us to see death not as an end but as a transition. Our frame is dust, but if we truly desire it, the heritage of the ages is ours. What decides the path is not some grand experience but the sincerity of our decision to seek God and the relationships we build in loving people. (112:2.20, 112:7.19)

But if you actually will, if you really desire, surely the heritage of the ages is yours (112:7.19)
