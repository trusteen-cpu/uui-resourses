Introduction — The Constellation in Our Address

Urantia is commonly called “606 of Satania in Norlatiadek of Nebadon”: the six hundred sixth inhabited world in the system of Satania, within Norlatiadek, one of the one hundred constellations of the local universe of Nebadon. Paper 43 introduces the middle step of that address, the constellation. Constellations are the primary divisions of a local universe, the link joining the local systems to the universe administration on Salvington. (43:0.1)

Edentia is about one hundred times as large as Urantia. Seventy major spheres circle it, and ten satellites revolve around each of these, making 771 architectural spheres in all. Because the constellation is the midpoint of the morontia-training regime, its morontia life is both typical and ideal. (43:0.2, 43:0.4)

this is really the typical morontia life (43:8.2)

I. The Constellation Headquarters — A World Without Storms or Winter

Edentia has fascinating highlands, tens of thousands of lakes, and countless interconnecting streams, but no rugged mountain ranges and no great oceans. Its atmosphere is a three-gas mixture—the two elements of Urantia’s air plus a morontia gas—and since there are no storms and no seasons, all outdoors can be embellished. Near the administrative center lies the sea of glass, a crystal about one hundred miles in circumference that serves as the receiving field. (43:1.1, 43:1.3, 43:1.10)

On the summit of the seventh highland range stand the resurrection halls, where mortals of the secondary modified order of ascension awaken. The Melchizedeks conduct an emergency school that studies the problems of the Satania rebellion and a bestowal school that deals with the problems arising from Michael’s final bestowal. The bestowal school was founded almost forty thousand years ago, immediately after Michael announced that Urantia had been chosen as his bestowal world. (43:1.5, 43:1.6)

II. The Constellation Government — A Self-Governing Lawmaking Unit

Each constellation is an autonomous unit, administered according to its own legislative enactments. Judicial problems are first reviewed by a council of twelve Melchizedeks, who formulate provisional verdicts and pass them on to the court of the Most High. The mortal division of that court consists of seven judges, all ascendant mortals. (43:2.1, 43:2.3, 43:2.4)

The higher you ascend in the universe, the more certain you are to be judged by those of your own kind. (43:2.4)

It is striking that the voice of mortals is where lawmaking begins. Experience gained by those who rose from the lowest levels is honored as material for the laws of the universe. On Edentia, however, the lower house of ascenders is not yet fully recruited. (43:2.5)

III. The Most Highs of Norlatiadek — Rulers in the Kingdoms of Men

The constellation rulers are Sons of the Vorondadek order, called Most Highs because they embody the highest administrative wisdom and the most intelligent loyalty. No disaffection of a Vorondadek Son has ever occurred in Nebadon. Gabriel commissions at least three for each constellation, and a Constellation Father reigns for ten thousand standard years, about 50,000 Urantia years. (43:3.1, 43:3.2)

The old records of Urantia used the term “Most High” often, yet confused who was meant. Tribal deities were mixed in, and the Hebrews merged the various celestial rulers into a composite Deity. But Daniel, the author says, fully understood these matters. (43:3.4)

The Most High rules in the kingdom of men and gives it to whomsoever he will. (43:3.4)

The Constellation Fathers are little occupied with individuals but are closely concerned with the lawmaking that affects races and nations. At the time of the Lucifer secession they seized certain phases of planetary authority on the rebellious worlds, and during Michael’s bestowal they directly supervised all Urantia affairs connected with it. Urantia therefore stands in a peculiarly close relation to the constellation rulers. (43:3.5, 43:3.7, 43:3.8)

IV. Mount Assembly — The Faithful of Days

The most holy mount of assembly is the dwelling of the Faithful of Days, the representative of the Paradise Trinity. He always stands at the right hand of the Constellation Fathers, yet never offers advice unless it is asked for. His residence is modest compared with the homes of the Most Highs, and here ascending mortals gather to hear the story of the long journey through Havona to Paradise. (43:4.1, 43:4.2, 43:4.4, 43:4.5)

Lucifer coveted this mount. He purposed to exalt his throne above the Sons of God and to sit upon the mount of assembly in the north. Even after the rebellion the archrebels kept coming to the Edentia councils, until Michael assumed unlimited sovereignty; since then they have not been permitted to sit there. Shortly before Michael’s death in the flesh, Satan sought to attend again and was unanimously rejected. (43:4.6, 43:4.7, 43:4.9)

When there exists no open door for the reception of evil, there exists no opportunity for the entertainment of sin. (43:4.9)

This sentence is worth reading at the level of a community. Evil seldom forces its way in; it enters where a door of sympathy has been left open. When the sentiment of a whole community stands firmly with the truth, agitation for evil finds no standing ground. (43:4.9)

V. The Edentia Fathers since the Lucifer Rebellion

The rotation of the Most Highs stopped at the time of the rebellion, and the present government has been expanded to twelve Vorondadek Sons. The current Constellation Father is number 617,318 of the Vorondadek series of Nebadon. Every isolated world has a Vorondadek observer, and one has been stationed on Urantia ever since the Caligastia betrayal; Machiventa Melchizedek paid respectful homage to him. (43:5.1, 43:5.3, 43:5.17)

VI–VII. The Gardens of God; The Univitatia

System capitals are adorned with material constructions and the universe headquarters reflects spiritual glory, but the constellation capitals are the acme of living embellishment. About half of Edentia is given to the gardens of the Most Highs, which is why such worlds are called “the gardens of God,” and why beautiful places on the worlds of Norlatiadek are so often called “the garden of Eden.” At the shrine in the center of the garden, the Most Highs lead all Edentia every tenth day in the worshipful contemplation of God the Supreme. (43:6.1, 43:6.2, 43:6.3)

He who has clean hands and a pure heart, who has not lifted up his soul to vanity nor sworn deceitfully. (43:6.3)

Material life there falls into ten divisions: the first three we would call vegetable and the last three animal, but the middle four are beyond our imagining. There are no carnivorous creatures, and the animals are gentle and intelligent. Celestial artisans express their ideals not in paint and marble but in living materials. (43:6.4, 43:6.5, 43:6.7)

Truly, eye has not seen such glories as await your arrival on these worlds of the mortal-ascension adventure. (43:6.8)
there is nothing in all Edentia to make any living being afraid (43:6.5)

The permanent citizens of the constellation, the univitatia, are children of the Creator Son and the Creative Spirit. They live on a plane between the material and the spiritual, yet they are not morontia creatures. Spiritually they are alike, intellectually they vary, and they function in seventy orders of personality. In artistic skill and social adaptability, almost no being outside Havona can equal them. (43:7.1, 43:7.2, 43:7.4)

VIII–IX. The Edentia Training Worlds; Citizenship on Edentia — True Socialization

The time spent on the seventy training worlds is the most settled period of the whole ascending career. You are re-keyed each time you move to a new world, but you keep the same morontia body and never lose consciousness. The task of this period is group ethics: the secret of pleasant and profitable relationships among very different personalities. (43:8.2, 43:8.3)

After graduating from the seventieth world, ascenders become citizens of Edentia. On the system worlds they were more material than spiritual, and on Salvington they will be more spiritual than material; on Edentia they stand midway between evolutionary animal and ascending spirit. The Most Highs are concerned less with individual advancement than with the welfare of groups and nations. (43:9.1, 43:9.2, 43:9.4)

they rule in the kingdoms of men rather than in the hearts of individuals (43:9.4)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

While Paper 43 describes the administrative structure of the universe, in the end it presents the constellation as the place where we learn how to live together. Law begins with mortal representatives; evil loses its power when the doors of sympathy are closed; and growth multiplies when different beings work together. The friction between people that we meet on Urantia can be seen as first practice for the socialization that will be completed on Edentia. (43:2.5, 43:4.9, 43:8.11)
