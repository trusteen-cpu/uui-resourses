Introduction — The One Beside the Son

Paper 33 introduced Michael and his government; Paper 34 turns to the one who works beside him as his equal, the local universe Mother Spirit. When a Creator Son is personalized, the Infinite Spirit individualizes a new and unique representation of himself to go with that Son into space. She becomes the Son’s companion, first in physical organization and later in creating and ministering to the creatures of the new universe. (34:0.1)

Because both respond to physical and to spiritual realities, they work together as co-ordinate associates. In physical creation the Son provides the pattern, and the Spirit initiates the actual materialization of physical realities. The Son designs; the Spirit shapes the design into substance. (34:0.2, 34:0.3)

I. Personalization of the Creative Spirit — The Primary Eruption

When the physical organization is complete, Michael proclaims that life is next to be projected in the new universe. Upon Paradise recognition there is a reaction of approval in the Trinity, and the Master Spirit of that superuniverse disappears into the spiritual shining of the Deities and then reappears. At that moment comes the “primary eruption,” a tremendous spiritual flash visible as far away as the superuniverse headquarters, and in the very presence of the Creator Son a new personal representation of the Infinite Spirit is personalized. (34:1.1)

The author admits that the secret of this process resides in the bosom of the Trinity. Only one thing is certain: during physical organization the Spirit presence was incompletely differentiated from the Infinite Spirit, but after the eruption it changes completely into a personal being tinged with the nature of that superuniverse’s Master Spirit. It is through this personalization that the Spirit could later become fully subject to the Son. (34:1.3, 34:1.4)

They are instantaneous, inscrutable, and incomprehensible (34:1.3)

II. Nature of the Divine Minister — The Breath of Life

After Michael is elevated to Master Son, the Mother Spirit becomes so augmented in personal qualities that all who contact her recognize her as a person. From the beginning she possesses the physical-control attributes of the Infinite Spirit, including antigravity, and on attaining personal status she exerts full control of mind gravity in the local universe. Her techniques vary according to the Master Spirit through whom she originated, yet in all essential spiritual attributes every Universe Spirit is identical. (34:2.1, 34:2.2, 34:2.3)

Life is ministered and maintained through the Creative Spirit. In creating intelligent beings she first collaborates with the Son to produce the Bright and Morning Star, and her later offspring come ever closer to the beings of the planets. For mortals, the Life Carriers provide the body and the Universe Spirit adds the breath of life. (34:2.4, 34:2.5)

Life is ministered and maintained through the agency of the Creative Spirit. (34:2.4)
the Life Carrier Sons provide the physical body, fabricated out of the existing organized material of the realm, while the Universe Spirit contributes the “breath of life.” (34:2.5)

The presiding Spirit of our superuniverse, Orvonton, is the chief of the Master Spirits, embodying the balanced union of the traits of all three Deities. So although Orvonton now seems tardy and backward, it is expected to evolve into an extraordinarily well-balanced creation. A slow start is no proof of inferiority, and the same holds true of personal growth. (34:2.6)

III. The Son and Spirit in Time and Space — Partners Who Fill Each Other’s Limits

Only absolute beings are independent of time and space in the absolute sense. The Creative Spirit is wholly independent of space and is just as personally present on one world of her universe as on any other, but in her ministry of the universal mind she must reckon with time. The Creator Son, by contrast, is not handicapped by time, but he cannot personally be in two places at once. (34:3.3, 34:3.4, 34:3.5)

Source 34:3.6

When the Son and the Spirit function in administrative union, each always has available the liberation of the other, and so within their creation they are practically independent of both time and space. This partnership, in which each makes up for the other’s limits, shows why true co-operation is more than a mere division of labor. (34:3.6)

One is free to choose and act only within the realm of one’s consciousness. (34:3.8)

She is the universe focus of both the Holy Spirit and the Spirit of Truth. (34:4.6, 34:4.7)

The seven adjutants are the creation of the Divine Minister; similar in character but diverse in power, they are hardly regarded as personalities apart from their Mother Creator. The paper explains John’s vision of the “seven spirits of God,” the four beasts, and the twenty-four elders as a blend of two scenes, one at Salvington and one at Jerusem. It adds that all living creatures have bodily units responsive to directional currents, so that the sense of orientation is fixed in life itself. (34:4.10, 34:4.11, 34:4.13)

Mind is also endowed differently at different levels. Superevolutionary personalities receive the local universe type of the superuniverse pattern of mind, while human and subhuman life receive the adjutant type of mind ministry. The paper notes as well that the Creative Spirit does not bestow mind until she is endowed with personal prerogatives. (34:4.8, 34:4.9)

V. The Ministry of the Spirit — The Threshold of Worship and Wisdom

On the inhabited worlds the Spirit leads the evolution of life from lifeless matter to plants, animals, and the first human orders, working chiefly through the seven adjutants. When animal mind develops capacity to receive the adjutants of worship and wisdom, its evolution crosses the threshold of spiritual ministry, and such a mind is at once included in the circuits of the Holy Spirit. (34:5.2, 34:5.3)

A mind ministered to by the Holy Spirit has the capacity to choose a Thought Adjuster, yet not until a bestowal Son has released the Spirit of Truth are all normal minds automatically prepared to receive Adjusters. The Spirit of Truth depends almost wholly on man’s personal reception, while the Holy Spirit is only partly conditioned by human attitude. (34:5.4, 34:5.5)

VI. The Spirit in Man — The Spirit That Leads but Never Drives

Divinity may be plural in manifestation, but in human experience all spirit influences are one in function. The divine Spirit descends by a long series of steps to meet us as we are and where we are, and it does not stop until it has lifted the soul back to the heights from which it first came forth. (34:6.2, 34:6.3)

Spiritual life, like physical energy, is consumed; spiritual effort brings relative exhaustion. That is why we need daily renewal. And even the highest doctrine, as dead theory, cannot change character. The Spirit must breathe upon the forms of truth. (34:6.5, 34:6.6)

unless and until the divine Spirit breathes upon the forms of truth and quickens the formulas of righteousness. (34:6.6)
The Spirit never drives, only leads. (34:6.11)

When we freely accept the Spirit’s guidance, the assurance of divine contact grows and the fruits of the spirit show in our lives. The words that the Spirit bears witness “with” our spirit point to the fact that the Adjuster has already told us of our kinship with God. (34:6.12, 34:6.13)

the fruits of the spirit are love, joy, peace, long-suffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, and temperance. (34:6.13)

VII. The Spirit and the Flesh — Urantia’s Double Deprivation

Mortals on normal worlds do not suffer an unceasing war between flesh and spirit; for them, rising from the animal level to the spiritual is more like an educational training. There is a reason why the conflict on Urantia is so acute. (34:7.2, 34:7.3)

The mortals of a normal world do not experience constant warfare between their physical and spiritual natures. (34:7.3)

Yet there is comfort. Those born of the Spirit experience no more conflict than the inhabitants of the most normal worlds, and the natural appetites of the body are not in conflict with the highest spiritual attainment except in the minds of the ignorant, the mistaught, or the overconscientious. It is a balanced teaching: hostility to the body is not spirituality. (34:7.6, 34:7.7)

the Spirit of Truth will always speak, saying, “This is the way.” (34:7.8)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 34 explains one pillar of universe administration, yet it ends by bringing us back to our own inner life. The Mother Spirit who gave the breath of life, the Son’s spirit that leads us into truth, and the Father’s Adjuster dwelling deep in the mind all work as one within a single person. Because that Spirit never coerces, a willingness to learn is the key to growth.
