Introduction — The Local Universe as a Great Family

Paper 37 gathers in one place the many personalities who work in our local universe of Nebadon. At their head stand the Creator Son, Michael, and the local universe Mother Spirit, the Divine Minister of Salvington. The two are co-ordinate in divinity and complemental in creative attributes, and the paper describes this relationship in the language of family, as “Father-Son” and “Spirit-Mother.” (37:0.1)

these creators are in a very literal sense the Father-Son and the Spirit-Mother of all the native creatures of Nebadon. (37:0.1)

Earlier papers dealt with the created orders of sonship, and later ones will treat the ministering spirits and the ascending sons; this paper focuses on the group in between, the Universe Aids. It also adds brief notes on the higher spirits stationed in Nebadon and on the permanent citizens of the local universe. The names are many and unfamiliar, but if we follow what each group exists for, we can see how carefully a universe is run. (37:0.2)

I. The Universe Aids — Seven Orders and Gabriel

Many of the orders grouped as Universe Aids are unrevealed, but the paper presents seven. The first, the Bright and Morning Star, is one of a kind in each local universe, the first-born of all its native creatures. In Nebadon he is Gabriel of Salvington. (37:1.1, 37:1.9)

Gabriel functions as the personal representative of the Sovereign Son and as spokesman for his creative consort. In Nebadon’s early days he worked almost alone with Michael and the Creative Spirit, but as the universe grew and its problems multiplied he was given a staff of unrevealed assistants, and in time the corps of Evening Stars was created to help him. When the work grows, co-workers are provided: this is the first picture we get of universe administration. (37:1.9, 37:1.10)

II. The Brilliant Evening Stars — Gabriel’s Hands and Feet

The Evening Stars are superangels planned by the Melchizedeks and brought into being by the Creator Son and the Creative Spirit. Their chief work is as Gabriel’s liaison officers, representing him at the capital of every constellation and system. As many as a thousand conclaves may be in session on Salvington at once, and since Gabriel cannot attend them all, the Evening Stars represent him. (37:2.1, 37:2.2)

he cannot be in two places at the same time, and these superangels compensate for this limitation. (37:2.2)

What makes this order unique is that it is twofold. Some were created with this dignity from the start; others are ascendant spirits who reached it through service, many of them having begun as seraphim. When assigned to a mission, they always work in pairs, one created and one ascendant. (37:2.4, 37:2.7)

On planetary missions Evening Stars have sometimes been known as “the angel of the Lord,” and they accompany Avonal bestowal Sons as cocommanders of those undertakings. On the missions of Trinity Teacher Sons that open a postbestowal age, they serve as liaisons between mortals and the invisible corps of Teacher Sons. The sixth group of Salvington worlds and its forty-two satellites are under their administration, with joint schools for spirit personalities and for ascending mortals. (37:2.3, 37:2.8, 37:2.9, 37:2.10)

III. The Archangels — Servants of Survival and Resurrection

Archangels are the offspring of the Creator Son and the Mother Spirit, the highest type of high spirit being produced in large numbers in a local universe; Nebadon has almost eight hundred thousand. They are normally outside Gabriel’s jurisdiction and take no part in routine administration, being dedicated wholly to creature survival and the ascending career of mortals. (37:3.1, 37:3.2)

Remarkably, in recent times a divisional headquarters of the archangels has been maintained on Urantia. Student visitors from beyond Nebadon are surprised to find archangel activities directed from this small and seemingly insignificant world, and their interest quickens when they learn of Michael’s bestowal here. The author sees in this a sign that more ascendant activities will be concentrated on the bestowal world in the future. (37:3.3, 37:3.4)

This undoubtedly presages the future concentration of other ascendant activities on the bestowal world of Michael and lends a tremendous and solemn import to the Master’s personal promise, “I will come again.” (37:3.4)

A corps of one hundred archangels goes with every Paradise bestowal Son to a planet, and two senior archangels serve as an Avonal’s personal aids. When a dispensation closes, it is literally true that the seraphic guardians of sleeping personalities respond to “the voice of the archangel”; the one who proclaims the roll call is the archangel of the resurrection. On the seventh group of Salvington worlds, the personality record keepers keep each mortal’s record from birth onward and preserve personality records and identification sureties between death and repersonalization. (37:3.5, 37:3.6, 37:3.7, 37:3.8)

IV. Most High Assistants — Volunteers from Beyond

The Most High Assistants are volunteers originating outside the local universe, temporarily assigned as representatives or observers of the central and superuniverses. Their number is always far up in the millions. Among them are Perfectors of Wisdom, Divine Counselors, Universal Censors, Solitary Messengers, supernaphim, seconaphim, tertiaphim, and others, who help bring Nebadon into fuller harmony with the ideas of Orvonton and the ideals of Paradise. (37:4.1, 37:4.2)

Technically they may stand outside local universe jurisdiction, yet when serving on request they often place themselves wholly under the directors of the realm. And there are times when certain advanced plans and complex rulings are wisely withheld for a while from the native personalities; these are better grasped by this more mature and farseeing corps. Here again we see higher beings setting aside their rights to serve a lower order. (37:4.3, 37:4.5)

V. High Commissioners — Speaking for Those Who Cannot

High Commissioners are ascending mortals who are Spirit fused, not Adjuster fused. On certain worlds the Adjusters indwell mortals only transiently, guiding them through life in the flesh and fostering the soul, but taking eternal leave when life ends. The surviving soul then attains immortality by fusion with an individualized fragment of the spirit of the Mother Spirit. Most of these beings stop at Salvington and remain forever in local universe service. (37:5.1, 37:5.2)

They have in spirit obeyed the Father’s command, “Be you perfect.” (37:5.3)

VI–VII. Celestial Overseers; Mansion World Teachers — One Vast School

The Nebadon educational system is jointly administered by the Trinity Teacher Sons and the Melchizedeks, but much of its upkeep falls to more than three million Celestial Overseers. These volunteers, qualified by experience, have their headquarters on the Melchizedek worlds and travel the universe inspecting its school technique. The training runs from the worlds of human origin through the mansion worlds, the seventy realms of Edentia, and the 490 spheres of Salvington. (37:6.1, 37:6.2)

The entire universe is one vast school. (37:6.2)

The keynote of this whole educational system is character acquired by enlightened experience. The teachers provide enlightenment; one’s universe station provides the opportunity for experience. The student is given a definite task and at the same time learns how best to do it. Because work and instruction are always joined, this school is more like a workplace than a lecture hall. (37:6.3, 37:6.4)

We teach you how best to execute the things we command you to do. (37:6.4)

Progress is not spiritual development alone; intellectual acquisition grows alongside it. The ascender no longer has to referee the conflict between spiritual and material natures and enjoys instead the unified urge of a glorified mind. Most Urantia mortals will have the chance to serve as Celestial Overseers before leaving Nebadon. The Mansion World Teachers, for their part, are recruited and glorified cherubim, commissioned by the Melchizedeks, who carry much of the teaching of the morontia life. (37:6.6, 37:6.7, 37:7.1)

VIII. Higher Spirit Orders of Assignment — The Hidden Workers of Administration

Certain higher spirit beings of the Infinite Spirit’s family are permanently assigned to the local universe, and some of them are directly connected with Urantia. Andovontia, who oversees the spirit and morontia circuits, is the one who isolated Urantia after Caligastia’s betrayal during the Lucifer rebellion, and he expresses pleasure in anticipating our eventual restoration to the universe circuits. (37:8.1, 37:8.3)

IX. Permanent Citizens of the Local Universe — Keepers of an Unchanging Place

The local universe also has orders of permanent citizenship, beings who by and large neither ascend nor descend: the susatia, the univitatia, the Material Sons, and the midway creatures. Together with Spirit-fused ascenders and the spironga, they form a relatively permanent citizenry, and their growing experience remains available to the universe on their level of origin. (37:9.1, 37:9.6)

The Material Sons and Daughters are a reproducing order, created when the creative union of the Son and the Mother Spirit has completed its cycle and the Son personalizes his last concept of being in dual form, male and female. Some are commissioned as Planetary Adams to found an Adamic race that will blend with the mortal inhabitants. The univitatia do for ascenders on the constellation spheres what the Havona natives do for pilgrims in the central universe. (37:9.8, 37:9.9, 37:9.10)

The account of the midway creatures is especially meaningful for readers on Urantia. Mortals are born, live, die, and move on to other worlds, and most celestial ministers are assigned only for a time. The midwayers, however, remain on the planet from the arrival of the Planetary Prince to the far-off age of light and life, providing continuity of planetary administration. (37:9.11)

On Urantia the midway ministers are in reality the actual custodians of the planet (37:9.11)

X. Other Local Universe Groups — Ascenders Who Reach Back

Nebadon now has more than three million inhabited worlds, with ten million in prospect, and 647,591 architectural spheres. The paper singles out two orders among the many. The spironga, spirit offspring of the Bright and Morning Star and the Father Melchizedek, carry out routine spirit tasks, while the spornagia, neither spirits nor persons, care for the material side of the headquarters worlds. (37:10.1, 37:10.2, 37:10.3)

Ascending mortals who reach Salvington are also attached to universe service as helpers, students, observers, and teachers. This picture shows that ascension is not a solitary race to the top. (37:10.5)

From each level of achievement these advancing mortals reach back and down to extend a helping hand to their fellows who follow them in the upward climb. (37:10.5)

The author says this is only a brief outline of the many personalities, and that the experience of the ascending career will gradually reveal them. The pilgrims of time advance from life to life and from world to world until they are lovingly dispatched onward to Paradise and the destiny of the finaliters. (37:10.6)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 37 may look like a list of names, yet it holds one coherent picture. The local universe is a great family led by creator parents, and its orders are arranged to make up for one another’s limits, to speak for the weak, and to help those who come after. The purpose of all this administration is to raise the mortals of time into beings of character through experience. (37:0.1, 37:6.3)
