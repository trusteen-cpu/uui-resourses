Introduction — Seeing Evolution from Above

Papers 58 through 64 told, in the order of time, how life appeared and grew on our world. Paper 65 looks at the same history again, this time from the place of those who oversaw it. The author is a Life Carrier resident on Urantia. He explains that basic material life is the work of a threefold creativity: the formulation of the Master Physical Controllers, the life-impartation ministry of the Seven Master Spirits, and the active ministration of the Life Carriers. (65:0.1)

The Life Carriers exercise considerable discretionary control over the environment right up to the appearance of human will—the ability to know God and the power of choosing to worship him. evolution—on Urantia or elsewhere—is always purposeful and never accidental. (65:0.7)

I. Life Carrier Functions — Three Phases of Being

The Life Carriers possess potentials of personality metamorphosis that few orders of creatures have. They ordinarily work as mid-phase Sons, the state of their origin, but in that state they cannot function in the electrochemical domains where energies and material particles are fashioned into living units. So, as their task requires, commissions of other beings change their phase of existence. (65:1.1, 65:1.2)

The Life Carriers look forward to a possible fourth, wholly spiritual stage when the universe is settled in light and life, but he admits frankly that the technique for reaching it has never been revealed to them. It is striking to hear that even such high beings face a future they do not yet understand. (65:1.8, 65:1.9)

II. The Evolutionary Panorama — From Seaweed to Man

The story of man’s ascent from seaweed to the lordship of earthly creation is a romance of biologic struggle and mind survival. Our primordial ancestors were literally the slime and ooze of the ocean bed in the warm bays and lagoons along the shores of the ancient inland seas. This section retraces that long road by picking out only its key links. (65:2.1)

III. The Fostering of Evolution — Caring Without Interfering

The Life Carriers may use every natural resource and any fortuitous circumstance, but they are not permitted to intervene mechanically in evolution or to manipulate it arbitrarily. This puts the dramatic frog story of Paper 62 in a new light. Had that frog perished, the evolution of mankind would not have ended. The Life Carriers were fostering no fewer than a thousand other strains at the same time, and that frog was their third selection. (65:3.2, 65:3.3)

Even if Andon and Fonta had been lost before having children, human evolution would only have been delayed, not prevented; afterward no fewer than seven thousand favorable strains could still have developed into a human type. But the human potentials of animal life are now exhausted, and mankind must solve its problems of development with the stocks it already has. (65:3.4, 65:3.5, 65:3.6)

In a general way, man’s evolutionary destiny is in his own hands, (65:3.6)

Just as the Life Carriers fostered and conserved the life strains before human will appeared, so now man must do this for himself. The author adds that one day his readers may be attached to a corps of Life Carriers and have every opportunity to offer better ideas. “Be patient!” he says. (65:3.6, 65:3.7)

IV. The Urantia Adventure — The Sixtieth Life Experiment

Urantia was assigned as a life-experiment world, the scene of the sixtieth attempt to improve the Satania adaptation of the Nebadon life designs. Here no fewer than twenty-eight features of life modification were worked out. Yet “experiment” does not mean trying things at random. The evolution of life is a progressive and changing technique, but it is never left to chance. (65:4.1, 65:4.2)

The evolution of life is a technique ever progressive, differential, and variable, but never haphazard, uncontrolled, nor wholly experimental, in the accidental sense. (65:4.2)

As evidence the author points to the healing of wounds. An injured cell elaborates certain chemicals that stimulate neighboring normal cells; these secrete substances that help healing, and they multiply to replace the cells that were destroyed. This formula was chosen from over one hundred thousand phases and features of possible chemical reactions, and more than half a million experiments were made before it was settled on. (65:4.3, 65:4.4)

The Life Carriers later refined the technique on another Satania world, giving more pain relief and better control over the proliferation of the surrounding cells. (65:4.5, 65:4.6)

Such modification work left behind many apparently useless forms of transition life and countless strange by-products. The author says he can well understand how puzzling these must look from the limited human viewpoint. (65:4.10, 65:4.12)

V. Life-Evolution Vicissitudes — Disappointment and Confidence

The Life Carriers have their regrets. The Caligastia betrayal and the Adamic default handicapped their efforts to modify intelligent life. Their greatest disappointment was the reversion of certain primitive plant life to parasitic bacteria, which caused many distressful diseases in the higher mammals and in man. They had hoped that the admixture of the Adamic life plasm would make the blended race practically immune, but that hope too was dashed. (65:5.1, 65:5.2)

The wise and all-powerful beings who are responsible for universe management undoubtedly know exactly what they are about; (65:5.3)

So the fitting attitude, for Life Carriers and mortals alike, is patient waiting and hearty co-operation. There are compensations for tribulation, such as Michael’s bestowal, and the celestial supervisors express complete confidence in the ultimate evolutionary triumph of the human race. This refusal to withdraw trust when plans go astray applies just as well to an individual life. (65:5.3, 65:5.4)

VI. Evolutionary Techniques of Life — An Innate Striving for Perfection

The secret of life cannot be captured by analysis. The chemist can explain the chemistry of dead protoplasm, but because he must kill protoplasm in order to analyze it, he can never grasp the organization and performance of living protoplasm. Yet every living thing carries, along with its power of adaptation, an insatiable craving for ever-increasing perfection. (65:6.1, 65:6.2)

These interminable efforts of all living things evidence the existence within them of an innate striving for perfection. (65:6.2)

The laws of physics do not respond to training, and chemical reactions are not modified by education, but mind learns from experience. The ability to learn, memory, and differential response to environment are the endowment of mind. (65:6.8)

Preintelligent organisms react to environmental stimuli, but those organisms which are reactive to mind ministry can adjust and manipulate the environment itself. (65:6.9)

VII. Evolutionary Mind Levels — The Ministry of the Adjutant Spirits

The seven adjutant spirits are more circuitlike than entitylike. They do not touch purely mechanical responses; they begin to function the moment the ability to learn from experience appears. On Urantia, a life-experiment world, they were relatively isolated, and with but a single exception they had greater difficulty contacting evolving minds here than anywhere else in Nebadon. That is why so many border phenomena appeared, confused mixtures of mechanical and teachable responses. (65:7.3, 65:7.4, 65:7.6)

At the level of the spirit of worship, the higher reaches down to co-ordinate with the lower, and still additional spirit ministry accompanies the work of the spirit of wisdom. All such changes are gradual and reciprocal, never abrupt. The author urges us always to keep three domains distinct. (65:7.7, 65:7.8)

VIII. Evolution in Time and Space — A Choice That Delay Cannot Defeat

Why does evolution take so long? The author answers that life cannot unfold faster than the physical changes of the planet allow. If conditions permitted, the whole evolution of life could be completed in well under a million years, but the Life Carriers have no control over geologic evolution. To short-lived creatures evolution seems endless, but on Paradise, where time is nonexistent, all of it is present. (65:8.2, 65:8.3)

Just as mind evolution waits on physical conditions, spiritual progress is delayed by limited mental growth. Yet the evolution of the soul does not depend on education or culture. What it needs is the mind’s choice of survival and its decision to do the will of the Father in heaven. (65:8.4)

Although survival may not depend on the possession of knowledge and wisdom, progression most certainly does. (65:8.4)
none of these obstacles can defeat the whole-souled choice of will. (65:8.5)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 65 presents evolution as a purposeful work of co-operation. Matter is linked to mind and mind to spirit, and the delays and setbacks along the way are not failures but the way things work in time and space. And now that the Life Carriers have withdrawn, mankind’s evolutionary destiny has passed in large measure into our own hands. (65:3.6, 65:8.5)
