Introduction — Is There Art in the Spirit World?

Paper 44, indited by an Archangel of Nebadon, is about the celestial artisans, the master artists and craftsmen of the morontia and lower spirit realms. They are found on headquarters worlds throughout the grand universe, but their chief realm of activity is the constellations, especially the seven hundred seventy worlds surrounding each headquarters sphere. (44:0.1)

it should be understood that the morontia and spirit worlds are not without their high arts and supernal cultures. (44:0.2)

Celestial artisans are not created as such. They are a corps that began with seven thousand Havona instructors assigned by the Infinite Spirit in collaboration with the Seven Master Spirits, joined by volunteer pupils from among ascending mortals and many celestial groups. Any morontia personality or spirit below the rank of inherent divine sonship may join, but no one may enlist for less than a millennium. (44:0.3, 44:0.4)

The Question of Reality — What Is Real, and to Whom?

Before the main subject, the Archangel lays down an important premise. The activities of the morontia and spirit worlds are real. For spirit beings, in fact, the material world is the more unreal, a mere shadow. He admits that he cannot, with spirit vision alone, perceive the very building in which this narrative is being translated and recorded; he discerns it through a spirit counterpart presented by an energy transformer. (44:0.15, 44:0.16)

To spirit beings the spirit world is a reality. (44:0.15)

A warm promise follows. On the path of ascent we will never lose the power to recognize our associates of former stages. Each new translation or resurrection adds new beings to our vision without in the least depriving us of the ability to recognize old friends. This is possible because the Adjusters retain duplicates of our entire life experience. (44:0.18, 44:0.19)

these Adjusters are going through with you, as a part of you, in reality, as you. (44:0.19)

The Archangel is candid about his limits as well. To convey the meaning of these semispirit activities he must constantly pervert thought and distort language, and he presses on knowing he cannot be very successful. All he hopes is to make emphatic the fact that these things are real. The lists in this paper should therefore be read as a “crude parallelism” rather than precise description. (44:0.20, 44:0.21)

I. The Celestial Musicians — Harmony Is the Speech of Havona

Spirit melodies are not material sound waves but spirit pulsations received by the spirits of celestial personalities. The celestial musicians produce harmony by managing the seven spirit forces listed below, and there are over one hundred thousand different modes of sound, color, and energy manipulation. (44:1.1, 44:1.2, 44:1.10)

Musical appreciation on Urantia has improved steadily for more than thirty-five thousand years, yet most people still respond largely with their muscles and only slightly with mind and spirit. The gift of harmony from Adam and Eve has been so diluted that only once in a thousand lives is there any great appreciation of harmonics. But the Archangel says not to be discouraged: one real musician could change the course of a whole nation, even the entire civilized world. (44:1.12, 44:1.15)

He notes that tuneful syncopation, which stirs rhythm without engaging the higher powers of harmony appreciation, appeals more generally to immature or spiritually indolent individuals. Even the best music of Urantia is only a fleeting echo of celestial strains, and reducing them to notes is like trying to reproduce a great orchestra with a single instrument. (44:1.13, 44:1.14)

Forever, music will remain the universal language of men, angels, and spirits. Harmony is the speech of Havona. (44:1.15)

The spirit-morontia world has a thousand and one things worthy of reproduction that are unknown on Urantia. The heavenly reproducers preserve and re-enact them in seven groups. The Archangel tells of a recent presentation in which more than one million actors produced a succession of one thousand scenes. (44:2.1, 44:2.10)

The divine builders construct individual homes, workshops, play buildings, temples of worship, educational headquarters, morontia co-ordination plans, and public assembly halls. Every spirit being may share to some extent in planning its own abode, and the transition worlds have an economy of mutual ministry and division of labor. The temples of worship are the most exquisite creations of the morontia and spirit spheres. (44:3.2, 44:3.3, 44:3.5)

all of these supermaterial creations are actually there, and we clearly discern them and just as fully enjoy them. (44:3.9)
For every material satisfaction which humans are capable of enjoying, we have thousands of spiritual realities that serve to enrich and enlarge our existence. (44:3.1)

IV. The Thought Recorders — Keepers of the Gems of Mentation

The thought recorders preserve the superior thought of the realms in the tongue of Uversa. Each superuniverse and each local universe has its own language, and the higher orders of Nebadon are bilingual. Expert recorders can register the equivalent of over half a million thought symbols in a minute; the concept symbols of Uversa number more than a billion, though the basic alphabet has only seventy. (44:4.2, 44:4.3, 44:4.4)

In the central universe understanding is so complete that language is hardly needed; a chance meeting on Paradise reveals more mutual understanding than a mortal language could convey in a thousand years. In the language of Nebadon, a half-hour address could cover the subject matter of a whole human lifetime, and the Archangel suggests we think of dreams in which we cross years of experience in a few seconds. (44:4.3, 44:4.7)

Even on Salvington we “know as we are known.” (44:4.3)

It is telling that the Archangel had seen the recorded ideation of some of this planet’s great minds before he ever came here. The noble thoughts people hold do not vanish; they are kept as treasures of the universe. What we choose to think lasts longer than we suppose. (44:4.2)

V–VII. The Energy Manipulators; The Designers and Embellishers; The Harmony Workers

The energy manipulators handle physical, mindal, and spiritual energy. Spiritual energy acts according to established laws just as physical energy does. The compound manipulators unite the three energies and seek to discover the universe presence of God the Supreme. Urantia is served by seventy transport advisers and twelve experts of communication. The teachers of rest teach that even spirit beings must periodically enter the divine rest to recuperate their energies. (44:5.4, 44:5.5, 44:5.6, 44:5.7, 44:5.8)

There are just as certain and reliable laws in the spirit world as obtain in the material realms. (44:5.4)

Rest is not idleness. Just as pilgrims who have traversed the circles of Havona enter the long and revivifying rest of Paradise, rest is a necessary technique for replenishing spent energy and storing reserves of spirit power for the next stage. (44:5.9)

The designers and embellishers work in over a thousand subdivisions: craftworkers of color, sound designers, emotion designers, artists of odor, presence embellishers, taste designers, and morontia synthesizers. The work of the harmony workers, the Archangel says, has no basis for comparison at all. Yet what cannot be described can still be implied, and he shows the kinship of truth, beauty, and goodness. (44:6.2, 44:7.1, 44:7.2)

that which cannot be described may still be implied (44:7.1)

Whether in art, science, or philosophy, the real soul of expression is absent unless the realities of truth, the meanings of beauty, and the values of goodness are unified in the life of the one expressing them. These three qualities are perfectly unified in God, and every God-knowing man or angel has the potential of unlimited self-expression by blending them in experience. (44:7.3, 44:7.4)

VIII. Mortal Aspirations and Morontia Achievements

Celestial artisans sometimes come down from the system headquarters to help gifted mortals, working together with the seraphim. The Archangel names three sources of special human ability. (44:8.1, 44:8.2)

Source 44:8.2
Source of special abilityContent

The Adjuster’s leadingAdded when the Adjuster has had real experience in that field on other worlds
Help of spirit artisansWhen both mind and Adjuster are skillful, they harmonize and inspire the talent
Special ability is never an arbitrary gift of the Gods (44:8.2)

The paper closes with deep comfort. There is no caste among spirit artisans; however lowly your origin, if you have ability you will receive due recognition. The handicaps of heredity and the deprivations of environment are made up in the morontia career. If something you longed to do on earth, but circumstances denied you, is still your desire after you gain true mota insight, you will be given every opportunity to fulfill it. (44:8.3, 44:8.4)

There can be no handicap of human heredity or deprivation of mortal environment which the morontia career will not fully compensate and wholly remove. (44:8.3)

This does not mean that everyone becomes the same. It is an equality of satisfaction; individuality of skill and expression is not erased, and selfish ambitions learn to become socialized. (44:8.4, 44:8.5)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 44 shows that the path of ascent is no dry discipline but a journey of culture, full of music and color, story and architecture, play and rest. It also tells us that the roots of this beauty are one with truth and goodness, and that unfulfilled dreams are not thrown away. The small art and care we offer now become first studies for that larger culture. (44:7.4, 44:8.3)
