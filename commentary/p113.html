Introduction — Guardian Angels Are No Myth

The preceding papers told of the Ministering Spirits of Time and the Messenger Hosts of Space. Paper 113 turns to the seraphim devoted to the care of individual mortals, the guardians of destiny. Presented by the Chief of Seraphim stationed on Urantia, it tells us that the traditional angels of heaven really do live near us and do much for us. In past ages these guardians were about the only group of angels this world recognized at all. (113:0.1, 113:0.2, 113:7.9)

The teaching about guardian angels is not a myth (113:1.1)

Jesus himself acknowledged this. Speaking of the children of the heavenly kingdom, he warned his hearers not to despise even one of these little ones, because their angels always look upon the presence of the Father’s spirit. Whoever reads this paper comes to see afresh that no human life is a road walked alone. (113:1.1)

their angels do always behold the presence of the spirit of my Father (113:1.1)

I. The Guardian Angels — Care That Changes with the Circles

Seraphim were at first assigned to the separate races of Urantia, but since the bestowal of Michael they are assigned according to human intelligence, spirituality, and destiny. The subnormal minded have one company of seraphim, with a battalion of cherubim, to see that justice and mercy are extended to them. The supernormal minded, and anyone enrolled in a reserve corps of destiny, are at once given personal seraphim. (113:1.2, 113:1.3, 113:1.5)

The ordinary person starts out in the seventh circle and journeys inward through the task of self-understanding, self-conquest, and self-mastery. The farther inward one moves, the fewer the people each angel is asked to watch over. (113:1.6)

II. The Destiny Guardians — A Volunteer Pair of Angels

Source 113:2.1

A supreme decisionDeciding to become Godlike — a real betrothal with the Adjuster
Entering the third circleBreaking through the inertia of the animal inheritance to reach the third circle
Mustering into a reserve corps of destinyPersonal seraphim are assigned at once, whatever the circle

On Urantia the first mortal to receive a personal guardian was Rantowoc, a wise man of the red race of long ago. Seraphim always volunteer for this service, and only those of long service, experienced and tested, are given it. The text offers an example: when a certain mortal was admitted to the reserve corps of destiny, more than one hundred qualified seraphim sought the assignment. (113:2.2, 113:2.3, 113:2.7)

Guardians serve in pairs, and one of the two is always on duty. The complement of being becomes the recorder; these are the recording angels of the evolutionary worlds. Angels share most of our emotions, but one of them they find hard to grasp. (113:2.5, 113:2.8, 113:2.9)

The only emotion actuating you which is somewhat difficult for them to comprehend is the legacy of animal fear that bulks so large in the mental life of the average inhabitant of Urantia. (113:2.5)

The angels wonder why we let our higher intellectual powers, and even our religious faith, be ruled by fear. The passage puts a quiet question to us: with a guardian always at our side, why do we live so captive to anxiety? (113:2.5)

Every seraphim has a name, yet in world service they are often known by planetary numbers. The paper even gives the long army, legion, and host numbers of the guardian of the human subject used in this revelation. When their wards fail to survive, guardians may serve again and again on the same planet, and in the process they grow attached to particular worlds and their peoples. An angel’s affection is not an abstract concern but the real warmth one feels for those one has long lived beside. (113:2.4, 113:2.6)

III. Relation to Other Spirit Influences — A Personal Co-ordinator

One of the most important things a guardian does is to co-ordinate, in a personal way, the many impersonal spirit influences that touch the human mind and soul. Nonpersonal spirits find it exceedingly difficult to make direct contact with minds so material and so discretely personal. The seraphim first unifies the varied ministries of the Infinite Spirit and then correlates them with the spirit presences of the Father and the Son. (113:3.1, 113:3.2)

The angelic servers are gifted in combining the love of the Father and the mercy of the Son in their ministry to mortal creatures. (113:3.3)
Source 113:3.5

This is why the seraphim becomes the custodian of the mind patterns, memory formulas, and soul realities of the survivor between death and resurrection. How so humble an angel manages such complex work, the author frankly admits, is not really known; it is conjectured that the unrevealed working of the Supreme Being makes it possible. (113:3.4, 113:3.6)

IV–V. Seraphic Domains of Action; Seraphic Ministry to Mortals — From the Outside Inward

Seraphim stimulate the mind to make circle-making decisions. Yet they do not work as the Adjuster does, from within through the soul; they work from the outside inward, through the social, ethical, and moral environment. The impulse of worship comes largely from the adjutants and the Adjuster, but the urge to pray very often arises from seraphic influence. (113:4.1, 113:4.4)

To accept the guidance of a seraphim rarely means attaining a life of ease. (113:4.3)

Although there seems to be no communication between Adjuster and seraphim, the two always work in exquisite accord. The personal ministry of the seraphim, the God presence of the Adjuster, the circuit of the Holy Spirit, and the Son-consciousness of the Spirit of Truth come from different sources and levels, yet all are integrated in the evolving presence of the Supreme Being. Heaven’s help to one person is manifold, but it never works at cross-purposes. (113:4.5, 113:4.6)

Angelic guidance leads, if anything, up the rugged hills of moral choosing and spiritual progress. And the angels never touch human freedom. No universe personality has the authority to curtail a person’s prerogative of choice. (113:4.3, 113:5.1)

Angels do not invade the sanctity of the human mind; they do not manipulate the will of mortals; neither do they directly contact with the indwelling Adjusters. (113:5.1)

Seraphim do not respond directly to our prayers or our passing whims; they carry out the mandates of their superiors. We chart our own course, and the angels help us make the best possible use of it. They seldom step into the routine of daily life except in emergencies, and material intervention is very rare. (113:5.3, 113:5.4, 113:5.5)

They are not overlords or directors; they are simply guardians. (113:5.4)

VI. Guardian Angels after Death — Trustees of the Soul

At death, everything that constitutes the real you, except the identity represented by the departing Adjuster, is entrusted to the guardian. The guardian reports up the chain of command and then goes to the first mansion world to await the awakening of her former ward. In the resurrection halls the Adjuster, the seraphim, and the new morontia form work together, and the personality is reassembled. (113:6.1, 113:6.2, 113:6.5)

The Adjuster will identify you; the guardian seraphim will repersonalize you and then re-present you to the faithful Monitor of your earth days. (113:6.6)

If the ward fails to survive, the guardian is absolved of blame before the tribunals of the archangels and moves on to a new assignment. At the dispensational roll call the seraphim answer on behalf of nonsurvivors but the Adjusters do not; this is the “resurrection of the unjust,” the formal recognition that creature existence has ceased. (113:6.3, 113:6.8)

When those of the lower circles are raised together at the end of an age, it is their group guardians who reassemble them in the resurrection halls. Over age after age such guardians may hold in trust thousands upon thousands of sleeping survivors. Meanwhile the Satania guardians who went astray in the Lucifer rebellion, though many sincerely repented, are detained on Jerusem until the final adjudication, and the souls they had held are kept safe by volunteer seconaphim. (113:6.7, 113:6.9, 113:6.10)

VII. Seraphim and the Ascendant Career — Companions to Paradise

The first awakening on the mansion world is an epoch in the ascending career. There, for the first time, we actually see the angelic companions who were always near us on earth, and become truly conscious of the presence of the Adjuster who indwelt us. (113:7.1)

Such an experience constitutes a glorious awakening, a real resurrection. (113:7.1)

On the morontia spheres the two guardian seraphim become our open companions, helping us progress while they themselves study in the extension schools of the mansion worlds. Because mankind was created a little lower than the simplest angels, our first morontia assignment is to serve as assistants to the seraphim. The one who was cared for now becomes a fellow worker. (113:7.2, 113:7.3)

In Havona, some guardians follow their pilgrims through the central universe; others bid a temporary farewell and achieve the circles of Seraphington on their own. They will be waiting on the shores of Paradise when their mortal associates awaken from the last transit sleep of time. Whether or not man and angel serve together again, the bond between them lasts forever. (113:7.5, 113:7.6)

For seraphim, the surest way to the Paradise Deities is to guide a soul of evolutionary origin to the portals of Paradise. That is why only destiny guardians are mustered into the mortal Corps of the Finality, and some of our angelic associates in time become our finaliter associates in eternity. (113:7.7, 113:7.8)

Therefore is the assignment of guardian of destiny the most highly prized seraphic duty. (113:7.7)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 113 pictures angelic care not as mysterious intervention but as companionship that respects human dignity. Angels never take the place of our will; they help us make the best use of the path we choose. Our part, then, is to stop being driven by fear and to decide our way, step by step, toward the inner circles. (113:5.4, 113:1.6)

The intimate associations and the affectionate attachments of the realms of human origin are never forgotten nor ever completely severed. (113:7.6)
