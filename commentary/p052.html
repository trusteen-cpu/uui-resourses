Introduction — Epochs Marked by the Visits of the Sons

Paper 51 dealt with the Planetary Adams; Paper 52 widens the view to the whole human history of an evolutionary planet. From the time life begins until it flowers in the era of light and life, at least seven epochs appear. What divides these epochs is neither technology nor dynasty but the planetary missions of the divine Sons. (52:0.1)

These successive ages are determined by the planetary missions of the divine Sons (52:0.1)

The span from the initiation of life to the appearance of man is set apart as the prehuman era and comes before the mortal epochs this paper describes. Throughout, the paper also draws a contrast: the order of a normal world, and Urantia’s situation, thrown out of that order by rebellion and isolation. So the paper is both a map of the future and a mirror held up to our own world. (52:0.9, 52:2.4)

I. Primitive Man — A Long, Dark, Yet Heroic Chapter

From the time man emerges from the animal level and can choose to worship the Creator until the arrival of the Planetary Prince, mortals are called primitive men. Six colored races appear in the order of the spectrum, beginning with the red; the law of this age is the physical survival of the fittest, and government is wholly tribal. People live in caves or on cliffs and learn to kindle fire, and on some planets they are helped by enormous birds that can carry a man nonstop for over five hundred miles. (52:1.1, 52:1.3, 52:1.5)

the performances of primitive man represent a splendid, even a heroic, chapter in the annals of an evolutionary world of time and space. (52:1.4)

The religion of this age is largely animal fear mixed with ignorant awe and tribal superstition. Yet the paper does not simply disparage it. Fear religion subdues fiery tempers and becomes the soil in which the Planetary Prince later plants the seeds of revealed religion. Once will appears, the temporary indwelling of Adjusters begins, and within a hundred thousand years of man’s standing erect the Planetary Prince usually arrives. (52:1.6, 52:1.7, 52:1.8)

II. Post-Planetary Prince Man — The Golden Age of the Home Begins

With the arrival of the Planetary Prince, government appears and a new dispensation opens, averaging around five hundred thousand years. The prince and his staff give the first revelations of higher truth and universe organization, and from then on religion grows along two paths together: evolutionary growth and graduated revelation. But the prince is not yet the Prince of Peace, and racial dispersion and intense nationalism reach their peak in this age. (52:2.2, 52:2.3, 52:2.5)

But the great social achievement of the prince’s epoch is the emergence of family life. (52:2.6)

As the idea of family grows, the roving life of the hunter gives way to settled homes and agriculture, and normal worlds establish full equality of the sexes. The paper also says that normal worlds take up the task of race purification in this period, restricting the multiplication of defective strains. At the same time it adds that there is abundant opportunity to show tolerance and altruism toward unfortunate individuals who have not lost their moral heritage. (52:2.7, 52:2.8, 52:2.12)

Religiously, this age is a spiritual dawn. Running through all the racial religions are two strains: the fears of primitive man and the revelations of the Planetary Prince. The paper remarks that in some respects Urantians do not seem to have wholly emerged from this stage. Telling apart faith born of fear and faith born of revelation remains a task for readers today. (52:2.4)

III. Post-Adamic Man — An Age of Invention and Internationalism

When man reaches the highest level biologically possible, a Material Son and Daughter sent by the System Sovereign arrive. The prime purpose of the Adamic regime is to move civilization from the hunter and herder stage to that of the agriculturist and horticulturist. The gift of Adam’s life plasm raises intellectual capacity and quickens spiritual progress, and on an average world this becomes a golden age of invention, energy control, and exploration. (52:3.1, 52:3.3, 52:3.6)

Your world is a full dispensation and more behind the average planetary schedule. (52:3.6)

By the end of the Adamic age the races are practically blended, taking on an olive shade of the violet hue, the racial “white” of the spheres. Nationalism wanes, representative government replaces monarchy, education becomes world-wide, and a common language takes hold. The paper holds that universal peace is seldom attained until the races are blended and speak a common tongue. (52:3.7, 52:3.10)

the brotherhood of man is the goal of its society. (52:3.12)

IV. Post-Magisterial Son Man — A Society That Knows How to Use Leisure

To a world whose races are blended and at peace comes an Avonal Son from Paradise on a magisterial mission. Unlike the Planetary Prince and the Material Sons, who are of local universe origin, the Magisterial Son hails from Paradise, and on his first magisterial mission he is incarnated without experiencing birth or death. If one mission is not enough, a second or a series of Magisterial Sons follows, and each advent marks the end of one dispensation and the beginning of the next. (52:4.2, 52:4.3, 52:4.9)

It is perfectly safe to liberate such ethical and intelligent mortals. (52:4.5)

In this age two and one-half hours of daily work are enough to sustain one’s independence, and self-government becomes established. Interestingly, toward the end of the age society begins to return to simpler ways of living. The physical sciences have already reached their height; art, music, and higher learning flower; and a world-wide spiritual awakening signals the coming of the bestowal Son. The insight here is that leisure becomes liberation only when ethical maturity to use it comes first. (52:4.5, 52:4.6, 52:4.8)

In this epoch the majority of mortals are indwelt by Thought Adjusters, but Adjusters of fusion destiny are not yet bestowed on all; it is still necessary for will creatures to choose the Mystery Monitors. The greater God’s gift becomes, the weightier the human choice to receive it. (52:4.7)

V. Post-Bestowal Son Man — The New and Living Way

When a world becomes ripe for spiritualization, a bestowal Son of the Avonal order arrives. The exception occurs once in each local universe, when the Creator Son makes his terminal bestowal—as when Michael appeared on Urantia. On normal worlds the bestowal Son comes when intellectual and ethical attainment is highest, but on Urantia he appeared at the close of the Adamic dispensation. (52:5.1, 52:5.2)

his life is an incarnation of Paradise truth in mortal flesh (52:5.4)

On Urantia the establishment of this way was a matter of fact as well as truth. After the Lucifer rebellion, the procedure by which mortals pass directly to the mansion worlds at death had been suspended; since Pentecost, Urantia mortals may again proceed directly to the morontia spheres. When the bestowal Son ascends, the Spirit of Truth is poured out upon all flesh, the bestowal edict of the Adjusters is issued, and every normal-minded will creature receives an Adjuster on reaching the age of moral choice. (52:5.5, 52:5.6)

VI. Urantia’s Post-Bestowal Age — Five Paths to Brotherhood

On a normal world the age after the bestowal Son is a dispensation of world-wide peace, but not on Urantia. Jesus himself warned of “wars and rumors of wars.” Because unaided social evolution can hardly achieve brotherhood on an isolated planet, the paper says religious revelation is essential, and it sets out five conditions for social brotherhood. (52:6.1, 52:6.2)

Before they are grand planetary programs, these five conditions are personal tasks. Reading the literature of another culture, choosing emotional maturity over selfish calculation, seeing a neighbor as a child of the same Father—one person can begin these today. The paper says the quickest way is the spiritual transformation of humanity. (52:6.7)

VII. Post-Teacher Son Man — The Threshold of Light and Life

Next the Trinity Teacher Sons come, in groups. A planetary Teacher Son is supported by seventy primary Sons, twelve secondary Sons, and three of the most experienced Daynals, and the corps remains for at least a thousand years. Urantia is out of step here too, because Jesus promised to return, and no one knows when that second coming will take place in relation to the other Sons. (52:7.1, 52:7.2)

In this age more and more Adjusters fuse with their subjects during the life in the flesh, natural death becomes less frequent, and the length of life approaches five hundred years. Representative government vanishes and the rule of individual self-control begins; about an hour a day from each adult is enough for the physical administration of the world. Still, the paper makes clear that the inhabitants remain finite and mortal, and nothing is perfect. (52:7.4, 52:7.5, 52:7.9)

Slowly but surely the world is being won to the joyous service of the Sons of God. (52:7.7)

The greatest comfort is that this final prospect is open to every world. Whether a realm has been wholly loyal, tainted with evil, or cursed by sin, sooner or later the grace of God and the ministry of angels will usher in the day of the Teacher Sons and begin the era of light and life. Backward Urantia is no exception. (52:7.14, 52:7.16)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 52 teaches us to read history as a course of education carried forward by divine visitations. Each epoch opens only on the soil prepared by the one before, and revelation expands only as far as a world can receive it. Urantia has departed from the normal order in many ways, yet the paper’s conclusion is not discouragement but hope. Brotherhood and peace are a story about distant planets, and also work each of us can help with now. (52:2.3, 52:7.14)
