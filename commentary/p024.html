Introduction — The Family of the Infinite Spirit in Three Grand Divisions

On Uversa all the personalities and entities of the Conjoint Creator are divided into three grand divisions: the Higher Personalities of the Infinite Spirit, the Messenger Hosts of Space, and the Ministering Spirits of Time, who teach and minister to ascending will creatures. Paper 24 treats the first of these, introducing seven divisions that include the Solitary Messengers of the previous paper. (24:0.1, 24:0.2)

Most of these higher personalities have stations in the local universes, yet they are not organically attached to the administrations of the evolutionary realms. They are, so to speak, an unseen infrastructure that works alongside local governments without being part of them. (24:0.10)

I. The Universe Circuit Supervisors — Keepers of the Paths of Spirit Energy

The power currents of space and the circuits of spirit energy may seem to run automatically, but in fact they are under intelligent supervision. The Universe Circuit Supervisors deal not with purely physical energy but with the circuits of relative spiritual energy needed to sustain spiritual and morontia beings. They do not originate these circuits; they direct and manipulate all such spirit-energy circuits outside the Isle of Paradise. (24:1.1)

All these stupendous systems of energy are under control; they are subject to intelligent supervision. (24:1.1)

Circuit supervisors serve forever in their original assignment and are never rotated, so they make an agelong study of the problems of their realms. Tertiary circuit supervisor number 572,842 has served on Salvington since the early concept of Nebadon and is a member of Michael’s personal staff. It is they who would isolate a world from the higher spiritual circuits if its Planetary Prince rebelled, though they cannot annul the material currents of the power directors. (24:1.11, 24:1.12)

The circuit supervisors and the Universe Power Directors complement each other, the one overseeing spirit circuits and the other material circuits. Yet they are not co-ordinate; the circuit supervisors are subject to the Supreme Power Directors, and the circuits of pure mind are under the supervision of neither. They possess a unique type of personality, other than that bestowed by the Father, and though ascenders will come to know them, they will have no personal relations with them. (24:1.13, 24:1.14, 24:1.15, 24:1.16)

II. The Census Directors — Those Who Know Where Will Exists

Although the cosmic mind knows of all thinking creatures, there is an independent method of keeping count of will creatures. The Census Directors become aware of the birth of will anywhere in the grand universe the moment it happens and can report the number, nature, and whereabouts of all will creatures. They do not, however, function on Paradise, for there it is not needed. (24:2.1, 24:2.2)

On Paradise knowledge is inherent; the Deities know all things. (24:2.2)

Orvonton has one hundred thousand Census Directors assignable to its local universes, and Usatia, the superuniverse chief, does not register will directly but totals the reports coming in from each local creation. They record only the fact of will function; they are not recorders of anyone’s life and deeds. The partial emergence of will in higher animals lies outside their domain. (24:2.5, 24:2.7, 24:2.8)

The Census Director of Nebadon, number 81,412 of Orvonton, now stationed on Salvington, is at this very moment personally conscious and aware of your living presence here on Urantia (24:2.7)

That we were registered the moment we first exercised will, and that our death will be noted when we perform our last act of will, reminds us how weighty a thing will is in the universe. These directors are flawless in their work; they never default and never falsify. (24:2.8, 24:2.9)

But they are infallible in function (24:2.9)

III. Personal Aids of the Infinite Spirit — Nearly the Only Ones We Will Not Meet

When and how the Personal Aids were created is not known, and their number is estimated to run high into the trillions. They serve only the Paradise presence of the Third Person of Deity, flashing to and fro to the uttermost parts of creation. All are equal and identical, and they do not manifest a spirit presence to other spirit beings. This very lack of a presence-form makes them all the more serviceable to the Infinite Spirit. (24:3.1, 24:3.2, 24:3.3)

Of all the revealed orders of spirit beings taking origin in the Infinite Spirit, the Personal Aids are about the only ones you will not encounter on your inward ascent to Paradise. (24:3.4)

IV–V. The Associate Inspectors and the Assigned Sentinels — Eyes and Ears of the Supreme Executives

The Seven Supreme Executives on the Paradise spheres of the Infinite Spirit are the board of supermanagers for the seven superuniverses. The Associate Inspectors, joint offspring of the Infinite Spirit and the Seven Master Spirits, were personalized seven hundred thousand strong in the near times of eternity, and they represent the authority of the Supreme Executives in each local universe. The Assigned Sentinels, exactly seven billion in number, do the same in each system of a local universe. (24:4.1, 24:4.2, 24:5.1, 24:5.2)

Thus the paper shows a centralized yet far-flung system of advisory and administrative co-ordination for the whole grand universe. (24:5.3, 24:5.4, 24:5.5)

VI. The Graduate Guides — Companions on the Havona Road

The Graduate Guides conduct the high university that is essential to mortal attainment of the goal of the ages: God, rest, and then eternity of perfected service. The author all but despairs of portraying their work to a material mind, but he comforts us that our vision will not always be so limited. (24:6.1, 24:6.2)

You are not always to be so limited in the range of your vision and spiritual comprehension. (24:6.2)

Until the first mortal pilgrim reached the outer circuit of Havona, there was no record of any Graduate Guide in the central universe. The one who greeted that pilgrim, Grandfanda, was Malvorian, the first of the guides. His arrival sent the thrill of all time through the heavenly courts and signaled the start of the universe-wide broadcast, the “space reports of glory.” (24:6.4, 24:6.7, 24:6.8)

did conduct him from the outer circuits of initial experience, step by step and circuit by circuit, until he stood in the very presence of the Source and Destiny of all personality (24:6.6)

The author recalls that he himself was serving on Uversa at that distant time, and that all rejoiced in the assurance that pilgrims from their own superuniverse would one day reach Havona. Grandfanda came from a planet in superuniverse number one. The first proof that an evolutionary creature could actually reach the goal became the joy of the whole universe. (24:6.7, 24:6.8)

And you will sometime meet these noble beings face to face if you do not reject the certain and all-perfected plan designed to effect your survival and ascension. (24:6.9)

VII. Origin of the Graduate Guides — Beings Who Grew Through Service

Evolution is not the order of the central universe, yet the revelators believe that the Graduate Guides are transformed Havona Servitals, the more experienced of them. When a servital returns from superuniverse service, is granted “personal contact” with the Paradise Central Shining, and disappears, a newborn Graduate Guide appears on the outer Havona circuit just three moments later, and the number of guides exactly equals the number of vanished servitals. (24:7.1, 24:7.2, 24:7.6)

knew the Father, entered the divine embrace, and is not. (24:7.5)

The paper reasons that the guides understand ascenders so deeply because they gained that culture by actual service in the superuniverse realms. The inexplicable attachment between guides and servitals is further evidence. And since Grandfanda, Havona itself has changed greatly, in modifications initiated by the Supreme Being, the God of evolutionary creatures, in response to the arrival of his first experiential children. (24:7.1, 24:7.7, 24:7.9)

The paper also cautiously suggests the background of this change. The Havona Servitals are created by the Master Spirits and the Supreme Power Directors, who hold the not-yet-operating mind and power potentials of the Supreme, so they are inherently involved in phases of Supremacy. When a servital is re-created as a guide, the evolutionary potentials of time and space are reflected in the central universe, and the revelators conjecture that experiential Deity is in some way concerned. (24:7.8)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 24 is a tour of the grand universe’s infrastructure. The spirit circuits are intelligently managed, every will is counted, and the state of every region is faithfully reported to the Supreme Executives. And at the far end of the long journey waits a guide who himself grew through service. The universe is not an indifferent machine; it is woven as a web of care. (24:1.1, 24:2.7, 24:6.3)

Paper 25 moves to the second grand division, the Messenger Hosts of Space. If the higher personalities of this paper form the skeleton of the grand universe, the messenger hosts that follow are the beings who move between its parts, assisting the courts, the records, and the journey of the ascenders.
