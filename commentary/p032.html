Introduction — The Story of How Our Universe Grows

Paper 32 is the first paper of Part II, “The Local Universe.” Until now we have looked down from afar on Paradise, Havona, and the seven superuniverses; now our gaze comes down to the creation we actually belong to. A local universe is the handiwork of a Creator Son of the Michael order, and it is organized into one hundred constellations, each with one hundred systems, each system eventually holding about one thousand inhabited spheres. (32:0.1)

The key word of this paper is evolution. All the universes of time and space are evolutionary, and the creative plan of the Michaels follows the path of gradual development of the physical, intellectual, and spiritual capacities of their creatures. The sovereign of our universe is Jesus of Nazareth, Michael of Salvington, and all his plans were approved by the Paradise Trinity before he began his adventure. (32:0.2, 32:0.3)

These universes of time and space are all evolutionary. (32:0.2)

I. Physical Emergence of Universes — Those Who Come Before the Creator Son

A local universe does not begin on the day its Creator Son arrives. Prior to universe existence, space-force is handled by the Paradise force organizers, and once energy responds to local or linear gravity, the superuniverse power directors take over. Until the power directors have assembled enough energy to form suns and material spheres, a Creator Son has no opportunity to begin organizing. (32:1.1, 32:1.2)

Source 32:1.5

3. At some stage of mass materializationThe Creator Son and a Creative Daughter of the Infinite SpiritArrive on the scene and begin building the headquarters world
4. Over long agesThe Son and the power centersGo on creating architectural worlds for constellation headquarters and system capitals

Local universes are roughly equal in energy potential, though they may differ in size and in the amount of visible matter. The energy charge of a local universe is about one one-hundred-thousandth of its superuniverse, and Nebadon has somewhat less mass materialization. Even so, Nebadon possesses every physical endowment found in any local creation of Orvonton. (32:1.3, 32:1.4)

II. Universe Organization — From Salvington to Satania

The Creator Son materializes visible matter from the energy organized in advance, designs living creatures, and in co-operation with the universe presence of the Infinite Spirit creates spirit personalities. The power directors who first put the energy into circuits thereafter share its control with the Son forever, and the very same one hundred power centers who co-operated in forming Nebadon still work at Salvington. (32:2.1, 32:2.2)

The first physical creation completed in Nebadon was the headquarters world, Salvington, with its satellites. A little more than one billion years passed between the first movements of the power centers and the arrival of the living staff on Salvington. Then one hundred constellation headquarters and ten thousand system headquarters were created, architectural worlds designed to accommodate even the morontia stage between physical and spiritual beings. (32:2.3)

What stands out in this order is that man comes last. Only after the house is built and those who will govern and care for its inhabitants are all in place are mortals created. When we came into this world, an order was already prepared to receive us. (32:2.7, 32:2.8)

Nebadon is still a young universe in Orvonton, and its planetary organization continues. At the last registry there were 3,840,101 inhabited planets. The 619 inhabited worlds of our system, Satania, are scattered among more than five hundred physical systems; Satania lies on the fringe of our local universe, and Nebadon itself lies toward the outskirts of Orvonton. (32:2.9, 32:2.10, 32:2.11)

you can adequately comprehend the destiny of the mortals of such a local creation only by a perusal of the narratives of the life and teachings of your Creator Son as he once lived the life of man, in the likeness of mortal flesh, on your own evolutionary world. (32:2.13)

III. The Evolutionary Idea — Perfection Is Something Attained

The only perfectly settled creation is the central universe of Havona. All other universes are finite and evolutionary, and they will not be settled in light and life until their physical possibilities are exhausted and the spiritual status of every inhabited world is settled. Havona holds the pattern of perfection, and every other realm must attain that perfection by its own established methods. (32:3.1, 32:3.2, 32:3.3)

Source 32:3.4

There is a wise compensation in this contrast. To the mortal in a distant universe who cannot see the Father in person, the Father is closest of all as the Adjuster within the mind. Yet the farther down the scale of life we go, the harder it becomes to find the invisible Father by faith, and some grow weary, doubt, and isolate themselves. The paper names the surest safeguard in that season: holding tenaciously to the truth of the Father’s presence in his Sons. (32:3.4, 32:3.6)

He who has seen a Creator Son has seen the Father. (32:3.6)

An evolutionary beginning is no stain. Animal origin is the exclusive method of producing one of the two basic types of finite intelligent will creatures, and those who climb from the very bottom, step by step, come to know every phase of life by experience. The perfection so attained, though grace is lavishly mingled with it, is in the end a genuine personal possession that comes from individual effort and actual living. (32:3.9, 32:3.10)

all the more honor to those who began at the bottom and joyfully climbed the ladder of life, round by round (32:3.10)

The Father could have made every mortal perfect by a single word. But that would have deprived them of the adventure and training of the long inward journey. And inherent perfection and evolved perfection need each other: the superuniverses depend on Havona for the final training of their ascenders, and Havona needs the superuniverses for the development of its descending inhabitants. (32:3.11, 32:3.14, 32:3.15)

IV. God’s Relation to a Local Universe — A Father Who Steps Back yet Takes Part

Because the Father has delegated so much power, he appears to be the least active of the Deities. But the paper warns us not to mistake him for a silent member. The Father refrains only from what someone else can do. He steps back because he knows his creatures need function and experience, yet through designated agencies and personalities he actually and personally takes part in events. (32:4.1, 32:4.2)

The Adjuster is a relatively isolated being, not directly co-ordinated with the local universe administration or with seraphic ministry. Yet it is through this separate channel that the invisible God reveals his presence to each person. And by means of the personality circuit, the Father personally knows the thoughts and acts of every being in all creation. (32:4.6, 32:4.7, 32:4.8)

the “Lord knows his children,” (32:4.8)

The section ends on the Father’s generosity. God is not a self-centered personality; he has divested himself of every function that another being can perform. And whatever he has kept, from that he gives Adjusters to mortals. The climax of the section is the confession that he has given himself to us so that we may become like him. (32:4.10, 32:4.11, 32:4.12)

God has given us himself that we may be like him (32:4.12)

V. The Eternal and Divine Purpose — Eternity Drawn as a Circle

The last section declares that there is a great and glorious purpose in the march of the universes. We are part of a plan too vast to see in a single lifetime, and the events of time and our material struggles are temporary scaffolding for crossing over into the promised land of spiritual reality. (32:5.1, 32:5.2)

All of your mortal struggling is not in vain. (32:5.1)

The author suggests that we think of eternity not as a straight line but as a cycle, an endless circle. Segments of time are born, live, and die, but they reappear in new form in the higher life that moves around the eternal circle. For most human beings who do not attain Adjuster fusion in the flesh, death is the way to escape the fetters of time and fall into step with the procession of eternity. The author frankly admits that he too cannot fully visualize eternity. (32:5.4, 32:5.5, 32:5.6)

The race for perfection is on! (32:5.8)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 32 confirms again and again that the universe is not handed over finished all at once. Energy gathers first, the house is built, those who will govern take their places, and then man comes. And man too is not a finished product but a being who starts at the bottom and gains perfection through experience. The Father seems to stand back from this process, yet in fact he works most closely of all, in his Sons and in the Adjuster within our minds.

Keeping in mind the order of growth sketched in this paper will make it easy to follow at what stage, and why, the many beings of the following papers appear.
