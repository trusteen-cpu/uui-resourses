Introduction — Three Circuits Between Paradise and Havona

If Paper 12 pictures the whole master universe as six concentric ellipses, Paper 13 takes us back to its very center. Between the central Isle of Paradise and the innermost circuit of Havona lie three lesser circuits of seven spheres each. Like Paradise they are eternal, and there is neither record nor tradition of their origin. (13:0.1, 13:0.2)

Source 13:0.1
CircuitSpheresWhat they do
InnermostThe seven secret spheres of the Universal FatherReflect the central shining of the Deities and shed it on Paradise and Havona
MiddleThe seven luminous worlds of the Eternal SonOrigin of the impersonal energies of spirit luminosity; emit light without heat
OutermostThe seven immense spheres of the Infinite SpiritExecutive headquarters of the Seven Master Spirits; send spiritual illumination to the superuniverses

The presenter, a Perfector of Wisdom, makes his limits clear from the start. Thousands of unrevealed activities go on in these twenty-one spheres, and this paper offers only a fleeting glimpse of activities that concern the present age in one of the seven sectors of the grand universe. So the paper tells us a great deal while also honestly acknowledging that there are things it cannot tell. (13:0.7)

I-1. The Seven Sacred Worlds of the Father — Seven “Bosoms”

The Father’s circuit contains the only inherent personality secrets in the universe of universes and is the only forbidden domain concerned with personality in the central universe. These worlds are directed by the highest order of the Stationary Sons of the Trinity, the Trinitized Secrets of Supremacy. Each sphere has a unique presence of Divinity that can be appreciated only by those who reside on, or are admitted to, that sphere. (13:1.1, 13:1.2, 13:1.3)

Divinington is the Paradise rendezvous of the Thought Adjusters. The nature and origin of the Adjusters, and the technique of their contact with the lowly creatures of the evolutionary worlds, are the secret of this sphere. The presenter himself has never landed on Divinington, and he says that its withheld truths, if revealed, would probably only confuse and handicap him in his work. (13:1.4, 13:1.5, 13:1.6)

The secret of Sonarington is the mystery of a Son of God being born of woman and becoming a Son of Man. The paper sets the mysteries of the three divine persons side by side. (13:1.8, 13:1.10)

The Adjusters are a mystery of God the Father. The incarnation of the divine Sons is a mystery of God the Son (13:1.8)
Reflectivity is a secret of God the Spirit. (13:1.10)

Vicegerington keeps the secret of trinitization, which is the secret of authority to represent the Trinity. Seraphington keeps the mystery of seraphic transport, by which seraphim envelop nonmaterial personalities within their spirit forms and carry them on long journeys. The presenter’s reason for saying little about the other secrets of Seraphington is striking: to reveal the secrets of beings so closely related that we can almost comprehend them would be akin to a betrayal of trust. (13:1.12, 13:1.20)

As we read about these seven worlds, we discover how many beings have nothing to do with mortal ascension. Countless orders of spirit personalities unrevealed to us call Solitarington and Seraphington home. The presenter reminds us that every division and level of universe activity is just as fully provided with spirit ministers as is the realm that helps mortals ascend. The universe is not a stage for humans alone, and that very fact shows the fairness and generosity of its administration. (13:1.16, 13:1.17, 13:1.19)

I-2. Ascendington — Where Our Own Secret Is Kept

Of the seven worlds, the one closest to mortals is Ascendington. It is the “bosom of the Father, Son, and Spirit,” the actual Paradise home of the ascendant souls of time and space until they attain Paradise status. Mortals will spend most of their Havona “vacations” there, and there the human self will enter into new relationships with the divine self. (13:1.21)

The secret of Ascendington is our own story: the mystery of the gradual building up, in the material and mortal mind, of a spiritual and potentially immortal counterpart of character and identity. In a word, the evolution of the immortal soul. (13:1.22)

This phenomenon constitutes one of the most perplexing mysteries of the universes—the evolution of an immortal soul within the mind of a mortal and material creature. (13:1.22)

Then comes a remarkable turn. To the presenter, a high Trinity-origin being, one seventh of Ascendington is forbidden, because that sector concerns a secret experienced and possessed only by beings of our type. The secret is eventually revealed to us, yet even then we never reveal it to any other order of beings. That a humble mortal comes to know by experience what a Perfector of Wisdom does not know shows how precious, on a universal scale, is the growth of the soul we are undergoing now. (13:1.23)

We know about the eternal fusion of a divine Adjuster and an immortal soul of human origin, but the ascendant finaliters know this very experience as an absolute reality. (13:1.23)

II. Father-World Relationships — Who May Enter Where

These home worlds are less residential spheres than rendezvous worlds and reunion spheres, serving as permanent cosmic addresses. Even as finaliters living on Paradise and serving in outer space, we will keep Ascendington as our home address and will regard it through all eternity as our home of sentimental memories. The presenter infers that ascending children of future outer universes will also look upon Ascendington as their home. (13:2.1, 13:2.2, 13:2.6)

As finaliters you will be domiciled on Paradise, but Ascendington will be your home address at all times, even when you enter service in outer space. (13:2.1)

Status on these worlds is acquired by service as well as by origin, and successive universe ages may redistribute certain personality groups. The presenter also advises us not to try to reason these questions out, since the more we knew, the more difficulties we would meet until we knew everything. (13:2.5)

Why do some secrets never open? The answer of this section is respect. When the Creator shares a personal experiential secret with a creature, that secret is kept in eternal confidence. We may become perfect, yet we need not know all the experiential secrets of every other order of beings. (13:2.8)

When the Creator has an experiential personality secret with his creature, the Creator preserves that secret in eternal confidence. (13:2.8)

III. The Sacred Worlds of the Eternal Son — Homes of Light Without Personality

The seven luminous spheres of the Eternal Son are the worlds of the seven phases of pure-spirit existence and the source of the threefold light of Paradise and Havona. No personality is present there; they teem with the otherwise-than-personal beings of the Eternal Son. The presenter shares the view of the Paradise philosophers that these entities are being assembled for ministry in the new universes of outer space, and that each Paradise cycle of about two billion years sees more of them created. (13:3.1, 13:3.2)

The presenter has never visited these worlds and says that, as a person, he would no doubt find them empty and deserted even if he were allowed to go. He adds a remark that is useful to us as well: high spirit personalities do not indulge in purposeless curiosity or useless adventure, because meaningful work is always abundant. (13:3.3)

There is at all times altogether too much intriguing and purposeful adventure to permit the development of any great interest in those projects which are either futile or unreal. (13:3.3)

IV. The Worlds of the Infinite Spirit — Executive Headquarters of the Master Spirits

The seven spheres of the outermost circuit are the executive worlds of the Seven Master Spirits and, in reality, the Paradise headquarters of the seven superuniverses. From here the Master Spirits equalize and stabilize the cosmic-mind circuits of the grand universe. Unlike the worlds of the Father or the Son, these administrative planets are open to all who can command the means of transit. The presenter confesses that when released from assignment he goes to these busy worlds to be inspired by those who enjoy doing their work. (13:4.2, 13:4.3, 13:4.6, 13:4.7)

The Seven Master Spirits are, in reality, the mind-spirit balance wheel of the universe of universes (13:4.2)

The heart of this section explains why the presence of God is experienced differently in different places. Physical reactions are always uniform and automatic, but experiential spiritual presence varies with the receptivity of the creature. (13:4.3, 13:4.4)

The Deities bestow more of their presence in response to sincere desire and withdraw when creatures decide adversely. The statement that the spirit of divinity becomes humbly obedient to our choices is an invitation: when God feels far away, look for the cause not on God’s side but in the vessel of your own heart. (13:4.5)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 13 is a paper about secrets. Yet these secrets are not meant to push us away; they honor the unique experience of each order of beings. Just as the mystery of the Adjusters, the mystery of incarnation, and the mystery of reflectivity belong to the Deities, the mystery of the soul’s evolution has been entrusted to us mortals. And how much of God’s presence we experience depends on our own choices.

Paper 14 introduces the billion perfect worlds that circle outside these twenty-one spheres: Havona, the divine central universe. Read through the eyes of a pilgrim who will one day pass through Havona with Ascendington as home, that paper will feel all the closer.
