Introduction — The Father Who Is Farthest and Nearest

With Paper 107 a series of papers on the Thought Adjusters begins. The author, a Solitary Messenger of Orvonton, opens with a paradox. The Universal Father resides on Paradise at the center of all things, yet he is actually present on the worlds of space, in the minds of his children of time, as the Mystery Monitors. The One who is farthest removed is at the same time the most intimately near. (107:0.1)

The Adjusters are the actuality of the Father's love incarnate in the souls of men; they are the veritable promise of man's eternal career imprisoned within the mortal mind (107:0.2)

God commanded us to be perfect as he is perfect, and then came down himself as the partner who helps us fulfill that command. So any mortal who follows the leading of the Adjuster, consciously or unconsciously, is already living by the will of God. The unquenchable longing to be like God is itself something the Adjuster creates in us. (107:0.3, 107:0.4, 107:0.5)

The Adjuster is man's infallible cosmic compass, always and unerringly pointing the soul Godward. (107:0.6)

At the end of the opening, the author adds that the Monitors are called by different names at different stages of mortal life. He makes it clear, however, that this division has nothing to do with the three stages of Adjuster progress in mind duplication and soul evolution. (107:0.7)

From then to the age of discretion (about forty years)Thought Adjuster

I. Origin of Thought Adjusters — God from God

The author begins humbly: no one may speak with authority about the nature and origin of Adjusters. All he can offer are the traditions of Salvington and the beliefs of Uversa. On their origin, however, there is no disagreement. Adjusters are not created beings; they are fragmentized entities of the infinite God, proceeding directly from the First Source and Center. (107:1.1, 107:1.2)

they are of God, and as far as we are able to discern, they are God. (107:1.2)

When Adjusters began their separate existence, and how many there are, is not known. It is presumed that they are constantly being individualized as the universe enlarges and fusion candidates increase, though perhaps even trying to count them is a mistake. The technique of their origin is an unrevealed function of the Father, and none of his other absolute associates seem to have any part in it. (107:1.3, 107:1.4, 107:1.5)

The section closes by comparing the ways in which the three persons of Deity give themselves to mortals. Because the nature of the Eternal Son cannot be fragmented in this way, there are three series of fusion. (107:1.7)

SourceWhat is given to mortalsFusion series

II. Classification of Adjusters — Seven Orders

Adjusters are individuated as virgin entities, and all are destined to become liberated, fused, or personalized. The author lists seven orders while admitting that he does not altogether comprehend these divisions. In divine nature they are all the same; the differences come from actual experience in universe ministry. (107:2.1, 107:2.2)

Source 107:2.1
OrderDescription
1. Virgin AdjustersServing on their initial assignment in the mind of an evolutionary candidate
2. Advanced AdjustersHave served one or more seasons on worlds where spirit fusion takes place
3. Supreme AdjustersReassigned to other mortals after a human partner declined survival — richer in experience
4. Vanished AdjustersA stage where tracking breaks off — the Melchizedeks and the Solitary Messengers hold different views
5. Liberated AdjustersEternally liberated from the service of time for mortals
6. Fused Adjusters (finaliters)One with ascending mortals, eternity partners in the Corps of the Finality
7. Personalized AdjustersServed with incarnated Paradise Sons, or achieved unusual distinction though their subjects rejected survival

The third order in particular gives us something to ponder. When a person rejects survival, the Adjuster’s labor is not thrown away; that experience becomes the ability to help the next mortal. Such an Adjuster is no more divine than a virgin Monitor, but it can do in the human mind what a less experienced one cannot. (107:2.4)

A supreme Adjuster, though no more divine than a virgin Monitor, has had more experience, can do things in the human mind which a less experienced Adjuster could not do. (107:2.4)

The fused Adjusters of the sixth order appear to translate from the absolute existential level of the universe to the finite experiential level. While retaining all of their existential divine nature, they become indissolubly linked with the ascending career of a surviving mortal. (107:2.7)

III. The Divinington Home of Adjusters — The Sphere No One May Visit

The dispatch, management, direction, and return of Adjusters all seem to be centered on Divinington, a secret sphere of Paradise. As far as the author knows, none but Adjusters and other entities of the Father have been there. When Adjusters return to the Father, they go back to Divinington and probably have actual contact with the Father’s Paradise personality. (107:3.1, 107:3.2)

Beings of high spiritual orders receive only three divine injunctions. The first two concern attitudes toward those above and those below, and they translate well into our own human relationships. (107:3.3)

Source 107:3.3
The three divine injunctionsDirected toward
Always to show adequate respect for the experience and endowments of seniors and superiorsRespect upward
Always to be considerate of the limitations and inexperience of juniors and subordinatesConsideration downward
Never to attempt a landing on the shores of DiviningtonReverence before mystery

The Solitary Messenger does not complain about the prohibition. He is sure that if anything on Divinington were essential to his growth, he would not have been forbidden to go there. This attitude—reading a prohibition as grounds for trust—is also a model for anyone of faith who stands before the unknown. (107:3.7)

we are compelled to gather information from a thousand and one different sources, and it is necessary to assemble, associate, and correlate this accumulated data in order that such knowledge may be informative. (107:3.8)

The valor and wisdom of the Adjusters suggest that they have received extensive training on Divinington. Its training schools are staffed by Personalized Adjusters, and the corps is presided over by the Personalized Adjuster of the first Paradise Michael Son to complete his sevenfold bestowal. Personalized Adjusters are always known by name, not by number. (107:3.9, 107:3.10)

IV. Nature and Presence of Adjusters — Qualified Absolute Fragments

Adjusters are not absolutes in the universal sense, but they are probably true absolutes within the potentialities of their fragmented nature. They are limited in universality but not in nature. That is why the author calls them the qualified absolute fragments of the Father. Moreover, not one of them has ever been disloyal. (107:4.2, 107:4.3)

No Adjuster has ever been disloyal to the Paradise Father (107:4.3)

Nonpersonalized Adjusters are visible only to Personalized Adjusters. Even Solitary Messengers and seraphim can only detect their presence through spiritual reactive phenomena or a spirit luminosity. This luminosity goes by different names in different places. (107:4.4, 107:4.5)

Source 107:4.5

The author ends the section with a question: do we truly realize what it means for an absolute fragment of infinite Deity to indwell and fuse with our finite mortal nature? No limit can ever be placed on the destiny of such a partnership. In eternity we will discover not only the infinity of the objective God but also the unending potential of the subjective fragment of God within us. (107:4.7)

Always will the Adjuster be revealing to the mortal personality the wonder of God, and never can this supernal revelation come to an end (107:4.7)

V–VI. Adjuster Mindedness; Adjusters as Pure Spirits

We tend to think of mind as a mediator between spirit and matter. But Adjusters exist on an absolute level prior to all divergence of energy and spirit, so there is nothing for mind to mediate. Even so, Adjusters plan, work, and love, so they must possess powers commensurate with mind. Otherwise they could never be personalized. (107:5.1, 107:5.2)

Even after fusion, the Adjuster’s mind remains distinct from the creature’s mind until the ascender reaches spirit levels. On the finaliter level, the mind factor that had linked the two appears to “supremacize” and augment the experiential endowment of the Supreme Being. One person’s experience, in other words, is added to the evolving God. (107:5.5, 107:5.6)

The Adjuster is man's eternity possibility; man is the Adjuster's personality possibility. (107:6.2)

The Adjuster is pure spirit, but spirit plus. Besides its conjectured mindedness, it contains factors of pure energy and traverses the grand universe instantly over the gravity circuits of Paradise. Yet it is not subject to gravity, for Adjusters are fragments of the ancestor of gravity, not its consequentials. (107:6.4, 107:6.5, 107:6.6)

Source 107:6.4
Element in the AdjusterWhat the text says
SpiritPure spirit, presumably absolute spirit — but spirit plus
MindednessLike that of the Father and the Eternal Son — ancestral to the minds of the Conjoint Actor

The most moving passage is about the Adjuster’s love. The Adjusters truly and divinely love us; they are prisoners of spirit hope confined in human minds, longing for their loneliness to end. From the moment of their bestowal until their release at the natural death of their subjects, they have no relaxation. (107:6.2, 107:6.7)

VII. Adjusters and Personality — Prepersonal Beings with Volition

Adjusters are not personalities, yet they are realities of the purest order known in the universe. How, then, can beings that are not persons select mortal subjects and volunteer to indwell them? The author candidly admits that probably no being in the universe has found the exact answer. (107:7.1, 107:7.2)

Still, the Adjusters’ conduct shows genuine volition. They volunteer to indwell, lay plans for our eternal career, and adapt and modify according to circumstances. Why, then, are they subservient to the mortal will? Because their volition, though absolute in nature, is prepersonal in manifestation. They are neither subpersonal nor superpersonal; the most accurate word is prepersonal. (107:7.3, 107:7.4, 107:7.5)

There are no created beings that would not delight to be hosts to the Mystery Monitors (107:7.7)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 107 does not hide the limits of what is known about Adjusters. It admits ignorance of the technique of their origin, of the whereabouts of the fourth order, of the nature of their will. But one thing is certain: a fragment of God himself lives within us, loves us, and waits for our choices. The paper asks how we will live with that fact. (107:1.6, 107:6.1)
