Introduction — Seven Spokes on a Gigantic Wheel

If Paper 14 shows us the perfect central universe, Paper 15 steps outside it into the creations of time where we actually live. The presenter, a Universal Censor from Uversa, likens the grand universe to one gigantic wheel. The hub is the Isle of Paradise, the seven spokes are the radiations of the Seven Master Spirits, and the rim is the outer regions of the grand universe. (15:0.1)

The same universe looks different depending on where one stands. To the Father the universes are virtually nonexistent, because he is the Father of persons and deals with persons. To the Son and the Spirit the universe is divided into local universes ruled by the Creator Sons and the Creative Spirits. And to the Paradise Trinity there are, outside Havona, just seven superuniverses. (15:0.1)

he deals with personalities; he is the Father of personalities. (15:0.1)

There is a reason the paper begins with physical structure. Only when we first understand the structure of space rightly can we grasp the meaning of the marvelous organization built upon it for spiritual government and for the advancement of will creatures. (15:0.3)

I. The Superuniverse Space Level — Not an Uncharted Plunge

Judged by short human records, our universe seems to be on one long, uncharted plunge into new space. But according to the records of Uversa, the seven superuniverses swing in an orderly way around Paradise and Havona along a great ellipse. Our local universe follows a well-understood counterclockwise course, and it is now passing through the very same space that its predecessors traversed ages ago. (15:1.1, 15:1.2, 15:1.3)

You are following the orderly and predetermined path of the superuniverse space level. (15:1.3)

Orvonton not long ago turned the southeastern bend and is now moving along the long, comparatively straight northern path. Urantia belongs to a system well out toward the borderland of its local universe, and that local universe is traversing the periphery of Orvonton. Even on a cosmic scale, our lives are not aimless drifting but a journey along a charted course. (15:1.5, 15:1.6)

III. The Superuniverse of Orvonton — Our Home Seen from Within the Milky Way

Practically all the stars visible to the naked eye from Urantia belong to Orvonton, the seventh superuniverse. The Milky Way is the central nucleus of Orvonton, and when we gaze through its region of greatest density, we are looking toward the residential universe and the center of all things. The rotational center of our minor sector lies in the star cloud of Sagittarius, and the nucleus of our solar system’s physical system is the center of the onetime Andronover nebula. (15:3.1, 15:3.3, 15:3.5, 15:3.6)

The confusion of star observers arises from seven layered revolutions, from Urantia’s orbit around the sun to the swing of Orvonton and the other superuniverses around Paradise. The paths of the planet and the counterclockwise motion of Orvonton are inherent in origin, while the motions in between are of composite origin, produced in part by the purposeful action of the force organizers. (15:3.7, 15:3.14, 15:3.15)

IV–VI. Nebulae, Space Bodies, and the Spheres of Space

Universe formation begins with nebulae. The Paradise force organizers transmute space potency into primordial force and start tremendous cyclones of force that become nebulae. When energy reaches gravity-responding levels, the power directors establish circuits. Each local universe, regardless of nebular relationship, embraces exactly one one-hundred-thousandth of the total energy of its superuniverse, because energy is universally distributed. (15:4.2, 15:4.4, 15:4.6)

pause to consider that the light you behold left those distant suns almost one million years ago. (15:4.7)

Suns and planets arise in ten principal ways, among them concentric contraction rings, whirled stars, gravity-explosion planets, centrifugal daughters, cumulative spheres, and collisional spheres; our own solar system arose by gravity explosion. Apart from the architectural spheres, all space bodies have an evolutionary origin. God’s creative acts, in other words, have unfolded by a time-space technique through the work of many intelligences. (15:5.2, 15:5.5, 15:6.7)

Several observations about the spheres stand out. The dark islands of space act as balance wheels that hold neighboring systems and keep them from destruction. A comet’s tail shows that light has weight and is a real substance. In our superuniverse not one cool planet in forty is habitable by beings of our order, and Urantia, in size, density, and location, is in many respects ideal for human habitation. (15:6.11, 15:6.13, 15:6.15)

VII. The Architectural Spheres — Worlds That Become Steps of the Ascent

The headquarters world of each government is an architectural sphere built for its special purpose. It has a sun that gives light without heat and is independently lighted and heated. All headquarters worlds are paradisiacal, increasing in size, beauty, and glory from Jerusem to the central Isle, and each is surrounded by training worlds for ascenders. (15:7.1, 15:7.3)

VIII–IX. Energy Control and Regulation; Circuits of the Superuniverses

Superuniverse headquarters function as power-energy regulators, and power centers and physical controllers maintain the balance of energy. When mass becomes overaggregated, collisions occur that turn matter back into energy and restore equilibrium. The farther one goes from Paradise, the harder prediction becomes, and that unpredictability reveals the presence of the Absolutes and the experiential Deities. (15:8.1, 15:8.6, 15:8.9)

Nebulae may disperse, suns burn out, systems vanish, and planets perish, but the universes do not run down. (15:8.10)

Besides the four presence circuits of Paradise, two divisions of circuits function in superuniverse space: the superuniverse circuits and the local universe circuits. Closest to us are the local universe circuits: the Spirit of Truth, the circuit of the Universe Mother Spirit, and the intelligence-ministry circuit that includes the adjutant mind-spirits. (15:9.2, 15:9.11)

While the executive branch comes from the realms of perfection, the legislative assembly springs from the flowering of the evolutionary universes. The representatives in the seven houses on Uversa are chosen from ascending graduates of Orvonton who are accredited for transport to Havona. Remarkably, in the whole history of Orvonton there has never been a disagreement between the executives and the assembly. (15:11.1, 15:11.2, 15:11.3)

It is on such worlds as Uversa that the beings representative of the autocracy of perfection and the democracy of evolution meet face to face. (15:11.1)

In judgment, too, the principle is clear. The Sons of the local universes can decree the survival of mortals, but only the Ancients of Days may sit in judgment on the issues of eternal life and death, such as the extinction of will creatures. The major and minor sector governments deal with intellectual status and physical co-ordination, and the reports of the Trinity observers pass from the constellations up to the superuniverse headquarters. (15:12.2, 15:13.2, 15:13.6)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 15 is full of numbers and administrative units, yet its conclusion is warm. Urantia, one of seven trillion worlds, is cared for as if it were the only one. And at the heart of that vast government, ascendant mortals who rose from evolutionary worlds like ours take part in judgment and legislation. Our journey lies along a charted course, and its end leads toward Havona.
