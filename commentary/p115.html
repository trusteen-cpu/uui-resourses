Introduction — Sonship and Achievement

With Paper 115 begin three papers on the Supreme Being. Sponsored by a Mighty Messenger temporarily sojourning on Urantia, it opens by contrasting two relationships. With the Father, sonship is what counts; with God the Supreme, achievement is the prerequisite to status. (115:0.1, 115:7.9)

one must do something as well as be something (115:0.1)

This short sentence is the key to the whole paper. The Father’s love is a free gift, but the Supreme is a Deity who grows through what Creators and creatures actually achieve in experience. It follows that our efforts and accomplishments do not vanish in the universe; they become part of a divine reality. (115:6.4, 115:7.2)

I. Relativity of Concept Frames — Serviceable Scaffolding

A partial and evolving intellect would be helpless in the universe without its innate ability to form a frame in which to think. When mind cannot reach true origins, it postulates conclusions and invents origins so that it can reason logically within that frame. Such frames are indispensable to rational thought, yet without exception they are more or less in error. (115:1.1)

Our understandings of truth, beauty, goodness, love, time, space, and even Deity are only relatively true. Concepts are scaffolding that must give way as comprehension expands. This humility does not weaken faith; it leaves room to imagine higher frames instead of being imprisoned in our present understanding. (115:1.2)

God is much, much more than a Father, but the Father is man’s highest concept of God (115:1.2)

in the last analysis it exists because God so willed (115:1.4)

A finite creature cannot explain its own existence without appealing to the prior acts and pre-existent will of its Creators. The final answer to the question “Why am I here?” is not logic but the will of God. Even the Father-Son concept will be enlarged by higher conceptions attained in Orvonton, Havona, and Paradise. For now we think within a mortal frame, but we can envision that higher frames exist. (115:1.2, 115:1.4)

II. The Absolute Basis for Supremacy — New Experience within Infinity

From the existential standpoint nothing new can happen in the universe, for the completion of infinity is eternally present in the seven Absolutes. From a finite creature’s viewpoint, however, infinity holds much that is still only potential. The whole scheme of creation and evolution is the conversion of potentialities of space, mind, and spirit into actualities. (115:2.1, 115:2.3)

We cannot comprehend how the value of anything infinite and divine could increase; yet in the experiential universes even divine values grow as actualities through enlarged comprehension of the meanings of reality. The possibility of such experiential growth becomes a universe actuality through triodity relationships that impinge upon the Supreme. (115:2.2, 115:2.4)

III. Original, Actual, and Potential — Growth Without End

No language is adequate to express infinity. Since mind cannot form a concept without first breaking the unity of a reality, it postulates three phases in order to grasp the infinite. The intellect reaches its limit in the concept of the Supreme Being, but the spirit reaches beyond in worship. (115:3.1, 115:3.2, 115:3.5)

Infinity is unity on one side and endless diversity on the other. No creature can understand how a unity becomes duality, triunity, and diversity while remaining one. We meet the same problem when we think of the undivided Trinity alongside the plural personalization of God. Beyond the Supreme, concepts become more and more names and less and less true designations of reality. (115:3.3, 115:3.4)

Though man’s spiritual nature reaches up in the worship experience to the Father who is infinite, man’s intellectual comprehension capacity is exhausted by the maximum conception of the Supreme Being. (115:3.4)
Source 115:3.11
PhaseWhat it isFrom the time viewpoint
The OriginalThe First Source and Center — the original manifestation of the I AMThat which is
The ActualThe triodity of the Eternal Son, the Infinite Spirit, and the Isle of ParadiseThat which was and is
The PotentialThe triodity of the Deity, Unqualified, and Universal AbsolutesThat which is becoming and will be

The interassociation of these three yields tensions within infinity that make all universe growth possible. In the originality of the First Source and Center, we cannot say that either actuality or potentiality exists or emerges. He simply is. (115:3.9, 115:3.10)

From a creature’s viewpoint, actuality is substance and potentiality is capacity. Actuality expands from the center outward into infinity; potentiality comes inward from the periphery and converges at the center. The personality of the spirit Son is the master pattern of all personality, and the substance of the Paradise Isle is revealed perfectly in Havona and progressively in the superuniverses. So too with us: in seeking the actuality of Deity, we evolve the potential divinity within ourselves. (115:3.12, 115:3.14, 115:3.15)

the Father is (115:3.10)

The potential may fail to actualize in part, in time, but never in the aggregate. And because both the Actual and the Potential are encircuited in the I AM, growth has no end. Each human decision actualizes a new reality and at the same time opens a new capacity for growth. (115:3.13, 115:3.16)

The will of God does ultimately prevail, not always concerning the individual but invariably concerning the total. (115:3.13)
The man lives in every child, and the morontia progressor is resident in the mature God-knowing man. (115:3.16)

From a circumscribed view there are many endings, but from a higher view there are only transitions from one phase to another. That is why the philosophers of the universe have concluded that there is no such thing as an end. What looks like failure or loss may, from a longer perspective, be a doorway to the next stage. (115:3.17, 115:3.18)

IV–V. Sources of Supreme Reality; Relation of the Supreme to the Paradise Trinity

Any consideration of the origin of God the Supreme must begin with the Paradise Trinity, for the Trinity is original Deity and the Supreme is derived Deity. The Supreme Being receives different aspects from different sources. (115:4.1, 115:4.2)

Source 115:4.7

It is hard to grasp how the infinite is actually manifest to the finite. Yet the evidence lies within us: the indwelling Adjuster is lasting proof that the absolute God can make direct contact with the least of all will creatures. (115:4.3, 115:4.4)

But the Thought Adjusters indwelling mortal man are one of the eternal proofs that even the absolute God (as absolute) can and does actually make direct contact with even the lowest and least of all universe will creatures. (115:4.4)

Total finite reality is growing dynamically between the unqualified potentials of outer space and the unqualified actuals at the center of all things. When the Architects of the Master Universe have matured the possibilities of the potential Absolutes, the Supreme Creator Personalities emerge from Paradise to undertake the agelong task of bringing the evolving universes into being. Even the birth of a universe has its order of preparation and execution. (115:4.6)

The Supreme Being depends absolutely on the Trinity for the reality of his spirit nature. The Trinity remains forever the center of perfect and infinite stability, around which the evolutionary growth of the Supreme unfolds. As the age of Havona gives way to the age of the superuniverses, direct creation by the Trinity gives way to the creative acts of the children of the Paradise Deities. (115:5.1, 115:5.2)

VI. Relation of the Supreme to the Triodities — A Twofold Motion

Paradise gravity, the spirit gravity of the Son, and the mind gravity of the Conjoint Actor still act directly. But the farther creation moves into uncharted space, the more it depends on developments within the three Absolute potentialities. The Supreme grows as creatures attain Godlikeness and as the finite possibilities are mastered. (115:6.1, 115:6.2, 115:6.4)

Just as the motion of the invisible can be discerned by its effects on the visible, the author says, the movements of Supremacy are detected by their repercussions in the personalities and patterns of the grand universe. The journey of a single ascending person is part of this vast inward motion. (115:6.6, 115:6.7)

VII. The Nature of the Supreme — The Finite Shadow of the Infinite

In the Supreme, the Father has achieved relative liberation from the limitations of infinity, eternity, and absoluteness. The price is that the Supreme becomes subject to the necessity of experience and meets the barriers of time. It is precisely because he is partial and incomplete that he can know growth and development. (115:7.1)

the Father’s plan, which has predicated finite progress upon effort, creature achievement upon perseverance, and personality development upon faith (115:7.2)

The Supreme Being is the personification of the finite shadow cast across the grand universe by the infinite unity of the Paradise Father. He is the focalizer and unifier of evolutionary experience, and all this appears to contribute to the eventuation of God the Ultimate. The Supreme can be fully appreciated only by considering his source in the Trinity, his field of activity in the universe, and his destiny in the Trinity Ultimate. (115:7.3, 115:7.6, 115:7.7)

By summing up evolutionary experience, the Supreme connects the finite with the absonite, much as the mind of the Conjoint Actor integrates the spirituality of the Son with the energies of Paradise. The author concludes that all this unity must be a revelation of the original unity of the First Father-Cause of all things and beings. (115:7.8)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 115 is full of abstract language, but its conclusion is close to home. The Supreme Being grows through experience built on effort, perseverance, and faith. Our honest efforts and patient growth therefore carry cosmic meaning. Concepts may change one day, but the spirit that reaches the Father in worship goes beyond their limits. (115:3.4, 115:7.2)
