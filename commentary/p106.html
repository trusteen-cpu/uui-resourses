Introduction — Seven Levels of Reality

If Paper 105 dealt with the origins of reality, Paper 106 deals with how that reality is being integrated. Its author, a Melchizedek of Nebadon, says that ascending mortals must understand not only Deity’s relation to the universe but also the relationships between themselves and the many levels of reality. Such understanding enhances both our terrestrial orientation and our spiritual directionization. (106:0.1, 106:9.13)

Source 106:0.3
LevelDescription
1. Incomplete finitesThe present status of ascending creatures, including Urantia mortals; universes not yet settled in light and life
2. Maximum finitesExperiential creatures who have attained destiny as revealed within the present universe age
3. TranscendentalsA superfinite level implying the prefinite genesis and the postfinite significance of the finite
4. UltimatesThat which is of master universe significance and touches the destiny of the completed master universe
5. CoabsolutesThe projection of experientials upon a supermaster universe field of creative expression

7. InfinityPre-existential and postexperiential — the unqualified unity of infinity

The author insists that these categories are convenient compromise symbols for the present age and for the mortal perspective. They are limited by mortal language and mind, by the incomplete development of the superuniverses, by our ignorance of the other purposes of superuniverse development, and by our lack of an eternity viewpoint. And reality itself grows from age to age. (106:0.10, 106:0.11, 106:0.18)

Repletion is continually being superimposed upon completion. (106:0.18)

I. Primary Association of Finite Functionals — God the Sevenfold

The primary phases of finite reality find immediate expression in the perfect Havona creation, but the evolutionary, secondary phases become integrated only through growth and attainment. Between the two lies the superuniverse time lag. The text says this lag is an obstacle, and at the same time an opportunity for the creature to become the Creator’s partner in its own evolution. (106:1.1, 106:1.2)

No matter how remote from Paradise, how deep in space, a material survival personality may take origin, God the Sevenfold will be found there present and engaged in the loving and merciful ministry of truth, beauty, and goodness to such an incomplete, struggling, and evolutionary creature. (106:1.3)

God the Sevenfold signifies Paradise Deity’s recognition of the barriers of time in the evolutionary universes. Because we ascend by personal and spiritual progress, it is the personal and spiritual divinity of the Sevenfold Deity that we find. The Sevenfold is the source of the relative unity of functional reality in the present grand universe, and all its phases are destined to be unified in the Supreme. (106:1.3, 106:1.4)

II. Secondary Supreme Finite Integration — The Supreme Being

As God the Sevenfold functionally co-ordinates finite evolution, so the Supreme Being synthesizes destiny attainment. The concept of the Supreme must distinguish spirit person, evolutionary power, and the power-personality synthesis of the two. Spirit comes from Paradise through Havona; energy-matter evolves in the depths of space and is organized as power by the Creator Sons and the children of the Infinite Spirit. (106:2.1, 106:2.2, 106:2.3)

This experiential power is earned and demonstrated power, in contrast to the existential power of the Paradise Deities. As it unites with the spirit personality of God the Supreme on the outer belt of Havona, the power product of time and space is invested with personality. Since mortals too are personal participants in this majestic transaction, they gain the capacity to know the Supreme and to perceive themselves as his true children. (106:2.3, 106:2.4, 106:2.5)

God the Supreme is experiential; therefore is he completely experiencible. (106:2.7)

Just as Michael is like the Father because he shares his Paradise perfection, evolutionary mortals will one day share the evolutionary perfection of the Supreme. The seven Absolutes cannot be known by experience, but the personality realities of the Father, the Son, and the Spirit can be grasped in the attitude of prayer and worship. (106:2.6, 106:2.7)

III–VI. The Experiential Trinities — From the Ultimate to the Absolute

The absonite architects eventuate the plan, the Supreme Creators bring it into existence, and the Supreme Being will consummate its fullness. When the present universe age ends and the Almighty Supreme appears, the first experiential Trinity, the Trinity Ultimate, will function completely. Because the Master Creator Sons are among its members, genuine creature experience is part of this Trinity. (106:3.1, 106:3.2, 106:3.3)

Group associations are enabled to anticipate, even to transcend, individual capacities (106:3.4)

The Trinity Ultimate co-ordinates the master creation, while God the Ultimate is the transcendental power-personalization that gives direction to the whole master universe. As the Supreme is now spiritually and personally present in Havona, so is the Ultimate present there in an absonite sense. What the full emergence of the Ultimate will change, we do not yet know. (106:4.2, 106:4.3)

The Trinity Absolute can be fully formed only after the whole master universe, from Havona to the outermost space level, has been completed. Its full function seems to imply a cosmos-infinite beyond even the master universe. At this point the text restates what a trinity really is. (106:5.1, 106:6.2, 106:6.3)

trinity is deity unity. (106:5.3)

Trinities are not personal, yet they do not contravene personality; they encompass it. Only the deified can become trinity, so the association of the three Absolutes is not a trinity but a triunity or triodity. The eternity action of the Trinity Absolute suggests the personalization of the Deity Absolute—the completion of God the Absolute, the third and last of the experiential Deities. (106:5.3, 106:5.4, 106:6.6)

VII. Finality of Destiny — A Journey Without End

Infinite integration is hard to imagine because it presupposes the experiential realization of all that could ever be. In the three potential Absolutes there always remain possibilities that no amount of experience can exhaust. Every being and every universe has a final destiny of absolute value, yet no being will ever completely attain all of its aspects. (106:7.1, 106:7.4)

The quest for God is endless! (106:7.5)

The text stresses, however, that this is no cause for discouragement. We really can attain the Deity levels of the Sevenfold, the Supreme, and the Ultimate. The infinity of God is, in fact, the supreme assurance that throughout endless futurity the possibilities of personality growth and Deity association will never be exhausted. (106:7.6)

At such a future eternity moment the master universe will still seem youthful; indeed, it will be always young in the face of the limitless possibilities of never-ending eternity. (106:7.8)

VIII. The Trinity of Trinities

In the mechanisms, personalities, and associations of the three Trinities, the author detects the theoretical possibility that the seven absolute phases of the Father-I AM might be reunited. This is the Trinity of Trinities, in which the experiential infinite becomes one with the existential infinite. The text portrays it as three levels of increasing unification. (106:7.10, 106:8.1, 106:8.3)

Source 106:8.4
LevelMembersMeaning
First levelThe Paradise, Ultimate, and Absolute TrinitiesPerfectly synchronized yet distinct groupings of Deity personalities
Second levelThe Supreme · the Ultimate · the AbsoluteThe personal union of the experiential Deities, resulting from the unity of their ancestral Trinities
Third levelThe I AMThe I AM realized as experiencible, as the Father-Infinite

The identity of the third member of the second level is the least clear; the text says it is least distorting to think of it as the Universal Absolute. The personal union of this second level has already appeared in our universe age in the deitization of Majeston. The text also traces the completion of the cycle of reality, as the Father-Son partnership becomes Son-Spirit, then Spirit-Supreme, and on to the Father-Infinite. (106:8.12, 106:8.18, 106:8.20)

But as we view these remote eventualities as personal creatures, we deduce that the final destiny of all personalities is the final knowing of the Universal Father of these selfsame personalities. (106:8.22)

The I AM of past eternity is alone. The I AM of future eternity is existentially unchanged but experientially very different. The difference is that limitless galaxy of personalities who have taken part, by their own will, in his self-revelation. They remain forever as volitional parts of the totality of infinity—final sons of the absolute Father. (106:8.23)

IX. Existential Infinite Unification — Unity at This Very Moment

Experiential integration lies in the remote future, but the existential unification of infinity exists right now in the Paradise Trinity. Time, space, and experience are the tools by which creatures grasp relative reality, and at the same time the barriers to complete perception. On the absolute level there is neither time nor space. (106:9.1, 106:9.2, 106:9.3)

Time, space, and experience are man's greatest aids to relative reality perception and yet his most formidable obstacles to complete reality perception. (106:9.3)
But total integration of reality is unqualifiedly and eternally and existentially present in the Paradise Trinity, within which, at this very universe moment, infinite reality is absolutely unified. (106:9.4)

How, then, can the viewpoint of time and the viewpoint of eternity be reconciled? The author suggests that we accept the Paradise Trinity as the existential unification of infinity, and he gives three reasons why we cannot detect the completed manifestation of the Trinity of Trinities. (106:9.5)

Source 106:9.6
Why we cannot detect itExplanation

The imperfect human statusRemoteness from the absolute level of experientials
The purpose of human existenceDesigned to evolve by experience, we are constitutively dependent on it — only an Absolute can be both existential and experiential

The solitary I AM of the past and the attainable I AM of the future are therefore one and the same hypothesis. No change has taken place in the Infinite; it is our capacity to receive reality that grows. And this lofty discussion arrives at a surprisingly concrete conclusion: all creature growth is proportional to Father identification, and to live divinely is to live the will of God. (106:9.9, 106:9.11)

We arrive at the understanding that living the will of God is the eternal passport to the endless possibility of infinity itself. (106:9.11)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 106 climbs through the seven levels of reality and the three Trinities all the way to infinity, and then, at the end, comes back down to the ground of our daily lives. A life built on the Father’s will is predicated on truth, sensitive to beauty, and dominated by goodness. Such a person is inwardly illuminated by worship and outwardly devoted to a service motivated by love. (106:9.12)
