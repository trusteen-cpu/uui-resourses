Introduction — The One Who Upholds the World of Spirit

If Paper 6 introduced who the Eternal Son is, Paper 7 deals with the relation the Son sustains to the universe. As the Father’s eternal purpose unfolds in the evolving universes, the Original Son is ever concerned with carrying out its spiritual aspects. Just as the Isle of Paradise upholds all things material, the Second Source and Center is the counterpoise who upholds spirit realities. (7:0.1, 7:0.3)

The spirit world is the habit, the personal conduct, of the Son (7:0.4)

That does not mean everything in the spirit world happens as the Son wills. The will of personal creatures is relatively free, so the conduct of spirit personalities does not always truly represent the Son’s character. Even so, the Son’s gravity control over all spirit realities remains absolute. This picture, in which freedom and order exist side by side, underlies the whole of Paper 7. (7:0.5)

To sum up: on the material side the Father is revealed in the beauty of the patterns of the central Isle, and on the spiritual side in the spiritual values of the Eternal Son’s personality. The Isle and the Son are two counterpoised partners. Keeping this pattern in mind makes the later comparison between spirit gravity and physical gravity far easier to follow. (7:0.3)

I. The Spirit-Gravity Circuit — A Pull That Responds to Quality

The key concept of the first section is spirit gravity. The pure spirit gravity of all creation is an exclusively spiritual circuit leading directly back to the Eternal Son on Paradise, and through it the Son holds all true spirit values. The paper sums it up this way: the control of universal spiritual gravity is universal spiritual sovereignty. (7:1.1)

Spirit gravity resembles physical gravity but differs from it decisively. Physical gravity responds to mass—to quantity—while spirit gravity responds to quality. It is also independent of time and space, so it is neither weakened nor delayed by distance. (7:1.2, 7:1.3)

Source 7:1.3
Physical gravitySpirit gravity
Responds toThe quantity of organized energyThe quality of spirit nature
Effect of distanceDecreases with the square of the distanceUndiminished
DelayAffected by the mass of the material creationNever suffers time delays

From the viewpoint of personality, spirit is the soul of creation; matter is the shadowy physical body. (7:1.3)

This pull does not act only on the universe as a whole. There is also a direct attraction between spiritually minded people of like longings, so “kindred spirits” is not merely a figure of speech. And while sin and rebellion can disturb the local universe circuits, they cannot halt the Son’s spirit gravity itself. Even Urantia, quarantined after the Lucifer rebellion, is not outside this circuit. (7:1.6, 7:1.7)

Sin and rebellion may interfere with the operation of local universe circuits, but nothing can suspend the spirit gravity of the Eternal Son. (7:1.7)

This section also touches on the relation with the Deity Absolute. The Eternal Son dominates the realm of actual spirit values, while the Deity Absolute seems to pervade the domain of potential spirit values. So most spiritual reactions are predictable, but in the realm of emerging potentials there are phenomena that are not. (7:1.8, 7:1.9, 7:1.10)

The last paragraph of the section summarizes the cycle of spirit. Spirit emerges from the potentials of the Deity Absolute, finds correlation in the experiential grasp of the Supreme and the Ultimate, and at last finds final destiny in the absolute spirit gravity of the Eternal Son. Our own spiritual growth is part of this great current, and no spiritual value that becomes actual is ever lost. (7:1.11, 7:1.4)

II. The Administration of the Eternal Son — From Paradise to the Local Universe

The Son’s personal activity is most profound and absolute on Paradise and is detected less and less as one moves outward. This does not mean his influence weakens. Rather, the way the Son is present differs at each level of the universe. (7:2.1)

Source 7:2.4
RealmHow the Eternal Son is present
ParadiseHis personal presence and activity are profound and absolute
Havona (the central universe)Discerned in the perfect spiritual harmony of the eternal creation
The seven superuniversesNot personally resident; only a superpersonal representation
The local universesPersonally present in the persons of the Creator Sons

In the superuniverses the Son’s administration is exclusively spiritual and superpersonal, so creatures can hardly discern it. In the local universes it is different: there we meet the Eternal Son close at hand in the Creator Son. The One who is so far away comes to us with a personal face at the place nearest to us. (7:2.3, 7:2.4)

III. Relation of the Eternal Son to the Individual — The Path Prayer Travels

The Son’s spirit does not indwell our minds as the Adjuster does. Yet at every stage of the ascent his beneficence is near and concerned with the welfare of the children of time. And spirit gravity is the inherent secret of the human soul’s Paradise ascension. The mortal mind begins as a material mechanism but becomes ever less subject to material gravity and more responsive to spirit gravity. (7:3.1, 7:3.2)

The spirit-gravity circuit literally pulls the soul of man Paradiseward. (7:3.2)

The most practical teaching of this section concerns prayer. The spirit-gravity circuit is the basic channel that carries sincere prayer to the consciousness of Deity, and whatever in our petitions has true spiritual value passes simultaneously to all the divine personalities concerned. So it does not matter whether we picture the Creator Son or the Eternal Son when we pray. The paper compares this sorting process to the neural circuits of the body. (7:3.3, 7:3.4)

Source 7:3.6
Content of the prayerWhat happens in the spirit circuit
Thoughts filled with the highest spiritual valuesOnce expressed, nothing can prevent their passing directly to the absolute spirit personality
Purely material and self-centered requestsFind no place in the spirit circuit; they fall dead
It is the motivating thought, the spiritual content, that validates the mortal supplication. Words are valueless. (7:3.7)

IV. The Divine Perfection Plans — Three Universal Enterprises

The Father and the Son are as one in the gigantic plan for lifting the material beings of time to the perfection of eternity, and the Infinite Spirit co-operates with them. The plan is made up of three correlated enterprises. (7:4.2, 7:4.3)

Source 7:4.3
PlanProposed byWhat it does
The plan of progressive attainmentThe Universal FatherLifts evolutionary creatures through the bestowal of Thought Adjusters and of personality
The bestowal planThe Eternal SonThe Sons of God are bestowed to make real the Father’s love and the Son’s mercy, and act as rehabilitators in rebellion
The plan of mercy ministryThe Infinite SpiritThe ministry that makes the attainment and bestowal enterprises effective

The bestowal plan also has the character of an emergency provision. When rebellion delays the attainment plan, the Paradise Sons stand pledged to go into those realms as retrievers. On Urantia a Creator Son performed exactly this service. Having proclaimed the mandate, “Be you perfect,” the Father entrusted its execution to the Eternal Son. (7:4.5, 7:4.7)

V. The Spirit of Bestowal — Coming as the Creature Itself

Because the Eternal Son’s personality cannot be fragmented, he cannot come to us as the Father does through the Adjuster. Instead, the Son draws near through a series of downstepping gradations of divine sonship until at last he stands in man’s presence—and at times as man himself. This is the technique of bestowal. (7:5.2, 7:5.3)

Source 7:5.3
The Universal FatherThe Eternal Son
How he comes to the creatureThe indwelling of the prepersonal Thought AdjusterThe incarnation of the Paradise Sons
How he shares creature experienceThe experience of fragmented entitiesThe bestowal experiences of the Sons

When Michael of Nebadon incarnated as Jesus of Nazareth, the Eternal Son truly came to mortal man on Urantia. Long, long ago the Eternal Son also bestowed himself on the seven circuits of Havona, and the original Michael, the first-born Creator Son, personally journeyed through the circuits with the ascending pilgrims. This is an encouragement to every pilgrim passing through Havona. (7:5.4, 7:5.5, 7:5.8)

the Eternal Son of God seven times abdicated the power and glory of Paradise to participate in the experiences of the time-space pilgrims (7:5.9)
It is literally true, whosoever has seen a Paradise Son has seen the Eternal Son of God. (7:5.11)

The spirit of bestowal is not found in the Eternal Son alone. The Creator Sons, the Magisterial Sons, and orders of sonship not yet revealed all share this willingness to give themselves as the creatures themselves. So whenever any Son is bestowed, it becomes true that the Eternal Son has bestowed himself in that bestowal. The principle that the higher a being is, the lower it stoops to serve also sets an example of service for us. (7:5.10)

VI. The Paradise Sons of God

The paper says that ignorance of the many Sons of God is a source of great confusion on Urantia. The Paradise Sons are classified according to which Deities personalized them. The Creator Sons are potentially equal with the Father and the Son in spirit nature, wisdom, and creative power, yet they can administer only what they bring into existence. (7:6.1, 7:6.3, 7:6.4)

Source 7:6.5
Order of SonsPersonalized byChief service
Creator SonsThe Father and the SonOrganize and rule the local universes
Magisterial SonsThe Son and the SpiritEarn through creature incarnation the right to serve as judges of survival
Trinity Teacher SonsThe Father, the Son, and the SpiritSupernal teachers of both human and divine personalities

Between the Eternal Son and all these Sons there is a direct and exclusive channel of communication, distinct from the spirit-gravity circuit. So the Son always has perfect knowledge of the thoughts and activities of every Paradise Son, as well as of every spiritual value in the hearts of all creatures. (7:6.7, 7:6.8)

VII. The Supreme Revelation of the Father

The last section is the conclusion of the paper. The Eternal Son is the complete and final revelation of the Father’s spirit and personality. The Father’s character could not be improved upon in the Son, but it is amplified for creatures by being divested of the nonpersonal and nonspiritual. (7:7.1, 7:7.2)

The Father comes down to you as a personality only through the divine Sons of the Eternal Son. (7:7.4)

The paper then adds a word of comfort for ascenders. To beings who were once human, the Eternal Son will be easier to approach than the Father, and in Havona we will attain the Son long before we can discern the Father. What we can do now is meditate on the life of our own Creator Son, who lived on earth as the Son of Man. (7:7.5, 7:7.6)

the Son of Man and the Son of God (7:7.6)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 7 may look like abstract theology, but it touches our inner life deeply. Because spirit gravity responds to quality, the quality of the thoughts and motives we hold determines where we are being drawn. Prayer rises not by the length of its words but by its spiritual content, and the Father comes nearest to us through the lives of his bestowal Sons.

PracticeThe teaching behind it
When you pray, examine your motive before your wordsIt is the spiritual content that validates a petition
Turn self-centered requests into longings for spiritual valuesSelfish requests do not ascend in the spirit circuit
Share spiritual fellowship with those of like longingsThere is a direct attraction between spiritually minded persons
Come to know the Father by meditating on the life of JesusThe Father comes to us as a personality through his Sons

Paper 8 introduces the Third Person of Deity, the Infinite Spirit, and Paper 9 takes up that Spirit’s relation to the universe. There we can follow how the plan of mercy ministry—one of the three perfection plans met in Paper 7—unfolds.
