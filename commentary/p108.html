Introduction — How God Becomes Our Experiential Father

If Paper 107 described what Adjusters are, Paper 108 describes what they do. The same Solitary Messenger of Orvonton first sums up their fundamental work: to represent, and to be, the Universal Father to the mortal creatures of time and space. The result is a unique being—the eternal union of the perfect Adjuster and the perfected creature—which could not be duplicated by any other universe technique. (108:0.1)

Why must God himself come down? The infinite God is infallible and all-inclusive, yet he cannot experientially know what he has never personally experienced. So the spirit of the Father descends from Paradise to take part in every genuine experience of the finite mortal’s ascending career. (108:0.2)

it is only by such a method that the existential God could become in truth and in fact man's experiential Father. (108:0.2)

Across six sections the paper takes up the selection and assignment of Adjusters, the prerequisites of indwelling, their organization, their relation to other spiritual influences, their mission, and finally God in man. Again and again the author says “we do not know,” yet on one point he is emphatic: Adjusters never fail and never falter. (108:5.3)

I. Selection and Assignment — Volunteers Who Know Everything in Advance

Adjusters dispatched from Divinington are identical in their endowment of divinity, but they differ according to previous experience. The more experienced Adjuster is often observed to indwell the higher type of human mind, so human inheritance seems to be a considerable factor in assignment. The Solitary Messengers firmly believe that all Adjusters are volunteers. (108:1.1, 108:1.2)

Adjusters do not volunteer blindly. The seraphic drafts of ancestry and the projected patterns of life conduct are relayed to Divinington by the reflectivity technique, and the Adjuster volunteers fully apprised of the intimate nature of the mind it will indwell. Three qualifications interest it in particular. (108:1.2, 108:1.3)

Source 108:1.3
QualificationWhat the Adjuster asks
1. Intellectual capacityIs the mind normal? Can the person develop into a genuine will creature? Will wisdom have an opportunity to function?
2. Spiritual perceptionWhat are the prospects of reverence and of a growing religious nature? What is the potential of soul?
3. Combined powersCan the two combine to produce strength of character and contribute to the evolution of a soul of survival value?

When more than one Adjuster volunteers, the supervising personalized orders probably select the one best suited. The sex of the creature plays no part in assignment or service. In the short interval before dispatch, the chosen Adjuster is presumably instructed, using a working pattern of the waiting mortal mind, in the most effective plans for approaching the personality. (108:1.7, 108:1.8)

The average transit time of an Adjuster from Divinington to Urantia is 117 hours, 42 minutes, and 7 seconds. (108:1.9)

II. Prerequisites of Adjuster Indwelling — The First Moral Choice

Adjusters are not actually assigned until their human subjects make their first moral personality decision. That choice registers in the seventh mind-adjutant, is recorded instantly in the presence of the Master Spirit, and is relayed at once to Divinington. On Urantia Adjusters arrive, on average, just before the sixth birthday—in the present generation, on the 2,134th day of earthly life. (108:2.1)

To receive an Adjuster, the mind needs the co-ordinate function of all seven adjutants; it must show the worship outreach and the wisdom to choose between good and evil. Because the Spirit of Truth is present on Urantia, Adjusters have been universally bestowed upon all normal minds of moral status ever since Pentecost. (108:2.2, 108:2.3)

God and man are directly related. (108:2.4)

On worlds before the Spirit of Truth is poured out, the arrival of Adjusters has been observed in connection with several conditions. The list also shows anyone living today where the doors to spiritual growth lie. The fourth and fifth, in particular, are paths anyone can take today. (108:2.5)

III. Organization and Administration — Ministers Who Have Never Faltered

Adjusters are organized as an independent working unit administered directly from Divinington, and they are uniform throughout the seven superuniverses. Outside Divinington, their complete records exist only at superuniverse headquarters; local universe records contain only an assignment number. Human beings are often known by their Adjusters’ numbers and receive real universe names only after fusion, from the destiny guardian. (108:3.1, 108:3.2, 108:3.3)

An evolutionary world that has received a Paradise bestowal Son has a Personalized Adjuster assigned as planetary supervisor of Adjusters. Local universe inspectors always address the planetary chief of Adjusters when they examine a planet. What Tabamantia, sovereign supervisor of the life-experiment planets, said during his inspection of Urantia shows how the universe regards the Adjusters. (108:3.4, 108:3.5)

Not on the records of Nebadon nor before the commissions of Orvonton has there ever been offered an indictment of a divine Adjuster. (108:3.6)

Tabamantia praises the Adjusters for helping to adjust the mistakes and compensate for the shortcomings of all who labor on this confused planet. Before these volunteers, who are apparently under his jurisdiction, he confesses that he all but worships them. This scene of a supervisor bowing before those he supervises shows that the Adjusters’ standing cannot be measured by the hierarchy of universe administration. (108:3.6)

The Solitary Messenger adds that his order is the only group of personalized creatures, apart from Personalized Adjusters, uniformly conscious of the presence of many prepersonal entities. For that reason they may be functioning, unconsciously, as universe correlators. The administration of the higher realms is not fully visible even to those who serve within it. (108:3.8, 108:3.10)

IV. Relation to Other Spiritual Influences — Alone, Yet in Harmony

Adjusters are quite alone in their work in the mortal mind. The Father has apparently resigned the direct exercise of personal power to his Creator children, but through the Adjusters he has reserved the right to draw all creatures to himself. Adjusters seem to come and go independently of other spirit influences, yet long observation shows that they work in perfect co-ordination with all the other spirit ministries in the human mind. (108:4.1, 108:4.3)

Even on a world isolated by rebellion, the Adjusters’ ability to communicate is unaffected. On a planet cut off from outside circuits, as Urantia was after the Caligastia upheaval, the liaison of the Adjusters is, apart from personal messengers, the only means of direct interplanetary communication. However cut off a place may be, the line to God is never severed. (108:4.4)

V. The Adjuster’s Mission — Heavenly Helpers

The Adjuster receives the admonitions of spiritual intelligences and translates them for the material mind. Truths it cannot transmit now, it faithfully preserves for use in the next stage of existence. Adjusters never lose anything committed to their care, and all seven groups are absolutely dependable. (108:5.1, 108:5.2, 108:5.3)

They are absolutely dependable, and this is equally true of all seven groups. (108:5.3)
They are called heavenly helpers, not earthly helpers. (108:5.5)

This section corrects a common expectation about religion. The Adjuster does not try to make life easy; it makes life reasonably difficult so that decisions will be stimulated. In return it confers a sublime peace of mind and a superb tranquillity of spirit. (108:5.5, 108:5.6)

In sharp conflicts between what is really right and wrong, the Adjuster always takes part in a definite and active way, and the fact that we are unconscious of this activity does not lessen its value. And when a person fails to survive, the guardian angel must be adjudicated, but the Adjuster is never examined, for it works in the manner of Paradise perfection. (108:5.9, 108:5.10)

You have perfect guides; therefore is the goal of perfection certainly attainable. (108:5.10)

VI. God in Man — Divine Parent of the Real You

It is a marvel of divine condescension that the perfect Adjusters volunteer to live in beings of animal origin. After the mission of a bestowal Son, the Adjusters truly become the “kingdom of heaven within you.” Through them the Father makes his closest approach to sin and evil, and the Adjusters are tormented by sordid and selfish thoughts and thwarted in their work by animal fears. (108:6.1, 108:6.2)

The “image of God” refers not to physical likeness but to the Father’s spirit presence in the Adjuster. The Adjuster duplicates every mental creation with a spiritual counterpart, slowly re-creating us as we really are for resurrection. These counterparts are actually there, even if they seldom rise into consciousness. (108:6.3, 108:6.5)

And as you are the human parent, so is the Adjuster the divine parent of the real you, your higher and advancing self, your better morontial and future spiritual self. (108:6.6)

The Adjuster is the power of survival that distinguishes us forever from mere animal creatures, the inner spiritual stimulus of thought in contrast with external stimuli coming over the nerves. And how the Adjusters rejoice when they can dispense with symbols and indirection and flash their messages straight to our intellects. (108:6.4, 108:6.7)

The Monitors are the prisoners of undying hope, the founts of everlasting progression. (108:6.7)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 108 shows how thoroughly prepared the Adjusters are when they come and how faithfully they work. What finally decides the outcome of their ministry is our choice. When the clouds gather overhead, faith should accept the presence of the indwelling Adjuster and look beyond the mists of uncertainty to the shining sun of eternal righteousness. (108:5.8, 108:6.8)
