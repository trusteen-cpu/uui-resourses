Introduction — There Is No Magic in Death

Paper 48 opens with a firm declaration. The Gods do not transform a creature of animal origin into a perfected spirit in a single step. The belief that death instantly turns the mortal mind into a perfected spirit is no more than a pleasing fable. Between the mortal estate and spirit status there always lies the morontia transition. (48:0.1, 48:0.2, 48:0.3)

Such beliefs are but ignorant superstitions and pleasing fables. (48:0.2)

This may sound cold, but it is in fact a word of hope. Because growth comes in stages, even our small efforts now are not lost; they become the starting point of the next stage. Paper 47 showed the order of the seven mansion worlds; this paper shows the materials, the people, and the ways of learning that support that path.

I. Morontia Materials — 570 Changes

The morontia realms are the local universe liaison spheres between the material and spiritual levels. These architectural worlds have just double the number of elements of the evolved planets: one hundred physical elements plus exactly one hundred forms of an energy organization called morontia material. In the system the life is very much like that of our present world, becoming more spiritual on the constellation and Salvington spheres. (48:1.1, 48:1.3, 48:1.4)

Source 48:1.5

As the body changes, so does our relation to the spirit. In the days of the flesh the divine spirit indwells us almost as a thing apart, but in the morontia life it becomes a real part of the personality. The number 570 shows how carefully the ascent is divided into steps. (48:1.6)

Morontia materials are not symbols; they are real. The paper explains that Paul spoke of a “better and more enduring substance” in heaven and of “the city which has foundations” because he knew of these worlds. The life to come is not a land of faint shadows but a world of substance more solid than our own. (48:1.7)

II. Morontia Power Supervisors — Makers of the Transition Environment

The Morontia Power Supervisors are offspring of the Mother Spirit. Rather than ministering directly to mortals, they make the transition environment itself possible. The first of them are created when the first mortal survivor arrives on a mansion world, and they come in groups of one thousand, divided into seven classes. (48:2.1, 48:2.2, 48:2.3)

The description of the selective assorters is especially striking. Ascenders may submit to their tests again and again, and as soon as adequate spiritual achievement registers, they are gladly certified for advanced standing. It is real growth, not a fixed timetable, that opens the next stage. (48:2.21, 48:2.23)

III. Morontia Companions — The Personality Luxury of the Ascending Career

Morontia Companions are offspring of the Mother Spirit, and there are over seventy billion of them in Nebadon. There are two types, aggressive and retiring, and after the midway creatures and the morontia cherubim they are the nearest of kin to human beings. Satania lost thousands of them in the Lucifer rebellion, and its quota has only recently been made up. (48:3.1, 48:3.3, 48:3.4)

Technically, the companions are not essential to survival; you could reach Salvington without them. Yet the universe has provided these friends. It shows the character of the ascension plan: it gives not only what is needed but cares for our joy as well. (48:3.18)

IV. The Reversion Directors — Humor, the Divine Antidote

The ascendant life is about equally divided between work and play. The reversion directors are a volunteer corps drawn from beings ranging from Havona natives to morontia progressors, who help their fellows achieve thought change and mind rest. Spirit humor never accentuates the misfortunes of the weak, and it is never blasphemous of divinity. (48:4.1, 48:4.4, 48:4.9)

Source 48:4.4
Level of celestial humorWhat it grows from
Reminiscent jestsMemories of past struggles and childish anxiety — lightening present loads
Current humorThe joy of discovering that many serious worries did not matter
Prophetic joyAssurance that “all things work together for good” and faith in our superiors
Humor is the divine antidote for exaltation of ego. (48:4.15)

Humor is a safety valve that keeps pressure from building up, and it softens the unexpected impact of fact and truth. Play and relaxation are always echoes of a backward glance; without memories of past experience there would be no basis for laughter. Knowing how to laugh, then, is no obstacle to the ascent but something close to essential equipment. (48:4.18, 48:4.19, 48:4.20)

V. The Mansion World Teachers — No Short Cuts

The Mansion World Teachers are deserted but glorified cherubim and sanobim. When a mortal leaves the flesh, the seraphim no longer needs these lieutenants; they pass through the embrace of the Mother Spirit and become teachers. Having already shared experience with their pupils on their native worlds, they are uniquely sympathetic teachers. (48:5.1, 48:5.2, 48:5.9)

Source 48:5.6

The constellationSchools of ethics · administration · social adjustment
Universe headquartersSchools of philosophy · divinity · pure spirituality
There are no royal roads, short cuts, or easy paths to Paradise. (48:5.7)

One purpose of the morontia career is to eradicate such animal vestigial traits as procrastination, equivocation, insincerity, problem avoidance, unfairness, and ease seeking. What we could have learned on earth but did not must be learned there in the end. This passage invites us to ask what we are putting off today. (48:5.7, 48:5.8)

VI. Morontia World Seraphim — Transition Ministers in Seven Divisions

The morontia life actually begins on earth, at the conception of the soul, when the Adjuster indwells a mind of moral status. But we become conscious of the ministry of the transition seraphim only on the mansion worlds. They are the sixth order of seraphim and serve in seven divisions. (48:6.1, 48:6.2, 48:6.3)

The seraphic evangels urge earthly teachers, too, to proclaim the love of God that casts out all fear, and the paper presents a song of trust said to have been whispered in the night to a shepherd boy. It also explains that the “heaven” of most prophets was the first mansion world, and Paul’s “third heaven” the third mansion world. The sevenfold mansion world experience is only the first of the “heaven of heavens,” followed by Jerusem, Edentia, Salvington, Uversa, Havona, and Paradise. (48:6.8, 48:6.20, 48:6.23)

The mind planners never manipulate students or take advantage of their ignorance; they respect the newborn morontia will. The truth they teach even on earth is simple yet profound: if your own mind does not serve you well, you can exchange it for the mind of Jesus. The recorder-teachers teach us to seek truth as well as fact. (48:6.25, 48:6.26, 48:6.32)

One can be technically right as to fact and everlastingly wrong in the truth. (48:6.33)

The teaching of the ministering reserves is the most practical counsel in the paper. The work is important; the self is not. What wears us out is not the weight of the work but the energy spent guarding the dignity of the ego. They also point out that our most disappointing disappointments have sometimes become our greatest blessings. (48:6.36, 48:6.37)

Self-importance, not work-importance, exhausts immature creatures (48:6.37)

VII. Morontia Mota — Philosophy Seen with Two Eyes

Mota is more than a superior philosophy; it is to philosophy as two eyes are to one, giving depth to meanings and values. On the first mansion world the simpler concepts of mota are taught in one column, with analogous statements of human philosophy in the other. The author records twenty-eight statements of human philosophy used in such a lesson. (48:6.28, 48:7.1, 48:7.2)

The act is ours; the consequences God’s. (48:7.13)
The acts of today are the destiny of tomorrow. (48:7.26)

These statements are brief maxims, yet they are where mansion world study begins. Cleverness is no substitute for character; impatience is a spirit poison; knowledge is possessed only by sharing. They are sentences well suited to taking up one at a time, each day, for reflection. (48:7.3, 48:7.20, 48:7.28)

VIII. The Morontia Progressors — Why Such a Painstaking School?

From graduation from the mansion worlds until spirit status in the superuniverse, ascenders are called morontia progressors. The purpose of this elaborate training is not endless ease. If the Gods meant only to take us on an eternal joy excursion, they would not turn the whole universe into one vast training school. Beyond the horizon of the present age lies a goal of transcendent service. (48:8.1, 48:8.3)

The road from mortal man to Paradise finaliter means actually living every phase of perfected finite creature existence. So, the paper says, in any future creation there will be no order of beings whose lives are wholly different from those the finaliters have already lived. (48:8.4)

Conclusion — Living This Paper

Paper 48 paints the everyday life of the hereafter in remarkable detail. The body changes 570 times, languages are learned by study, and teachers, friends, and directors of humor are always near. All of it points to one thing: growth comes not by magic but by experience, and that experience has already begun here and now. (48:6.2)
